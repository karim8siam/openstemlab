Biomedical assumptions and boundary condition limits

The linear sensitivity model applies across the clinical window (35.0 °C to 41.0 °C); wide temperature excursions require polynomial Steinhart-Hart corrections. Evaluated using a 10.0 kΩ nominal (at 25 °C) medical bead thermistor (β = 3950 K) configured in a four-arm equal-resistance bridge with V_in = 5.00 V excitation. Bridge balanced at T_0 = 35.0 °C (V_br = 0 mV). Linear sensitivity S_T is determined across the 35.0 °C to 41.0 °C clinical span.

Biomedical apparatus & physiological sensors.

NTC Medical Bead Thermistor Probe

Provides high-sensitivity thermal transduction with fast thermal time constant (< 0.5 s in liquid).

Precision Constant-Temperature Water Bath

NIST-Traceable Reference Standard Thermometer

Equal-Arm Wheatstone Bridge Conditioning Circuit

Low-Noise Regulated DC Voltage Source

6.5-Digit Precision Digital Multimeter

FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Calibrate a negative temperature coefficient (NTC) bead thermistor in an operational Wheatstone bridge circuit across clinical core body temperatures (35.0 °C to 41.0 °C) and determine bridge sensitivity. Standardized parameter settings: 35.0, 36.2, 37.4, 38.6, 39.8, 41.0 °C.

01

Bridge Null Balance Initialization

Set the precision water bath to exactly 35.00 °C and trim the Wheatstone bridge balancing potentiometer until differential output voltage reads exactly 0.000 mV.

02

Self-Heating Dissipation Verification

Measure thermistor current under 5.00 V excitation; verify power dissipation is under 0.2 mW to ensure thermal self-heating elevation remains below 0.01 °C.

03

Bath Thermal Stabilization

Adjust bath setpoint to 36.20 °C and allow 10 minutes for the circulating bath and immersed probe to achieve thermal equilibrium.

04

Equilibrium Output Voltage Logging

Record 10 successive bridge voltage samples over 60 seconds using the 6.5-digit DMM and compute the mean differential voltage V_br in millivolts.

05

Clinical Temperature Progression

Increment the calibration bath temperature through 37.4, 38.6, 39.8, and 41.0 °C, repeating steady-state voltage logging at each step.

06

Sensitivity Regression and Linearity Analysis

Plot differential bridge output voltage V_br versus temperature T, computing the thermal sensitivity S_T and coefficient of determination R².
