Fundamental Pharmaceutical Chemistry & Biopharmaceutics Principles

The partition coefficient P = C_oct / C_aq reflects the relative thermodynamic affinity of a drug for a hydrophobic lipid phase versus an aqueous environment. The 1-octanol/water model simulates passive biological lipid bilayer membrane permeability as established in Lipinski's Rule of 5.

Thermodynamic, Kinetic & Quality Control Mechanisms

Phase transitions in pharmaceuticals—whether the polymorphic transformation of crystal lattices, the dissolution of solid dosage forms into aqueous gastrointestinal fluids, the micellar incorporation of hydrophobic solutes, or the hydrolytic cleavage of labile drug bonds—are governed by rigorous thermodynamic principles. Reaction rates frequently follow Arrhenius thermal kinetics and pseudo-first-order degradation pathways, while dissolution follows the Noyes-Whitney diffusion model. Experimental precautions and laboratory safety

  • Maintain constant analytical laboratory temperature (25.0 ± 0.5 °C) and protect light-sensitive pharmaceutical reagents from ambient photo-oxidation.
  • Inspect all volumetric glassware, quartz cuvettes, and pressure cells for chemical residues, chips, or scratches before commencing measurements.
  • Use analytical reagent grade (AR) chemicals, fresh ultrapure water (18.2 MΩ·cm), and USP reference standards with traceable certificates of analysis.
  • Wear personal protective equipment including laboratory coats, nitrile gloves, and chemical splash goggles when handling organic solvents and potent drug powders.
