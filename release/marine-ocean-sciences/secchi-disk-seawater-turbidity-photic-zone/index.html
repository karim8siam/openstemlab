  3. Step 3

    Independent Setpoint Parameter Adjustment

    Adjust the independent testing parameter (Reciprocal Secchi Depth) across the designated analytical range (0.05 to 0.3 m⁻¹) in uniform calibrated steps.

  4. Step 4

    Scientific Telemetry Acquisition & Logging

    Execute the experimental run and record steady-state telemetry for Diffuse Light Attenuation Coefficient (K_d) with high-precision digital integration.

  5. Step 5

    Replicate Inspection & Environmental Equilibrium Check

    Perform duplicate test acquisitions at each testing setpoint to verify signal stability, absence of thermal drift, and sample reproducibility.

    Regression Calculation & Oceanographic Parameter Derivation
