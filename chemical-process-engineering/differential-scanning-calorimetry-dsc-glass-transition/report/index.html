4. Theory and graph choice

Differential Scanning Calorimetry (DSC) is the primary thermal analysis technique employed in chemical, materials, and process engineering to characterize thermodynamic phase transitions, crystallization kinetics, chemical crosslinking, and polymeric glass transitions. When an amorphous polymer is heated, it undergoes a reversible second-order-like kinetic transition from a rigid, glassy state to a compliant, rubbery state known as the glass transition (T_g). At the microscopic level, this transition corresponds to the onset of cooperative segmental mobility of 20 to 50 repeating polymer backbone units. Unlike first-order phase transitions (such as melting or vaporization) which exhibit latent heat and discrete discontinuities in enthalpy and volume, the glass transition exhibits an endothermic step-change in heat capacity: ΔC_p = C_p(rubbery) - C_p(glassy). Because the glass transition is an inherently kinetic phenomenon governed by structural relaxation times, the observed transition temperature T_g shifts systematically toward higher temperatures as the instrumental heating rate β (dT/dt) increases. According to Moynihan, Richardson, and Flynn–Wall–Ozawa kinetic relaxation theories, the scan-rate dependence of the glass transition temperature obeys the logarithmic relation: T_g = T_g0 + S · ln(β), where the thermal shift slope S = R · T_g^2 / ΔH* is inversely proportional to the apparent activation energy for segmental relaxation ΔH*. In this laboratory exercise, high-purity amorphous poly(methyl methacrylate) (PMMA) specimens (10.0 mg) sealed in hermetic aluminum pans are subjected to controlled heating scans from 5.0 to 30.0 °C/min under an inert nitrogen purge to determine the step change in heat capacity ΔC_p, extract the baseline glass transition temperature T_g0 = 100.00 °C, and evaluate the kinetic shift slope S = 2.1500 °C.

T_g = S · ln(β) + T_g0 = 2.1500 · ln(β) + 100.0000

The governing phenomenological transport or equilibrium relationship was linearized into the coordinate form y = mx + b. Here, the independent coordinate x represents Natural logarithm of scan rate ln(β), and the dependent coordinate y represents Glass transition temperature T_g (°C). The gradient m directly determines Glass Transition Thermal Shift Slope S, while the intercept b accounts for baseline tare values or reference potentials. Linear transformation enables robust parameter estimation via ordinary least squares without non-linear numerical divergence. Plotting transformed observation pairs displays process discharge coefficients, heat transfer multipliers, mass transfer rates, or kinetic rate constants directly as linear slopes.

5. Method

  1. Run an empty pan baseline and calibrate temperature and cell constant using standard high-purity indium (156.60 °C, 28.57 J/g).
  2. Weigh exactly 10.00 ± 0.01 mg of PMMA into an aluminum pan, crimp hermetically with lid, and place on sample sensor next to an identical empty reference pan.
  3. Heat sample to 160.0 °C at 20 °C/min, hold isothermally for 5 minutes to erase prior processing stress and enthalpy relaxation, and cool at 10 °C/min to 40 °C.
  4. Initiate DSC heating scan from 40.0 °C to 150.0 °C at β = 5.0 °C/min under 50 mL/min N_2 purge; record heat flow signal.
  5. Construct pre-transition and post-transition heat capacity baselines; determine midpoint T_g where heat capacity reaches half of the step ΔC_p.
  6. Repeat heating scans at β = 10.0, 15.0, 20.0, 25.0, and 30.0 °C/min; plot T_g versus ln(β) to evaluate kinetic activation slope S.

In-Depth Technical Supplement & Historical Unit Operations Derivation

1. Objective & Practical Significance

This laboratory investigation determines the glass transition temperature ($T_g$) and heat capacity step change ($\Delta C_p$) of amorphous poly(methyl methacrylate) (PMMA) using heat-flux Differential Scanning Calorimetry (DSC). Across heating scan rates $\beta$ from $5.0$ to $30.0^\circ\text{C/min}$, we evaluate structural relaxation kinetics, verify the Moynihan logarithmic scaling relationship $T_g = S \cdot \ln(\beta) + T_{g0}$, determine the thermal shift slope $S = 2.1500^\circ\text{C}$, evaluate baseline $T_{g0} = 100.0000^\circ\text{C}$, and extract the apparent activation energy for cooperative segmental backbone mobility.

2. Governing Transport Equations & Phenomenological Relations

Differential heat flow is related to heat capacity by: $\frac{dQ}{dt} = m_s \, c_p \, \beta$. The glass transition thermal shift obeys:

\[ T_g = S \cdot \ln(\beta) + T_{g0} = 2.1500 \cdot \ln(\beta) + 100.0000 \]

where $S = \frac{R T_g^2}{\Delta H^*} = 2.1500^\circ\text{C}$ and $T_{g0} = 100.0000^\circ\text{C}$. The segmental relaxation activation energy is calculated as: $\Delta H^* = \frac{R T_g^2}{S}$.

3. Experimental Apparatus & Instrumentation Specification

Testing was performed on a TA Instruments Q2000 heat-flux DSC equipped with a refrigerated cooling system (RCS90) and purge gas flow of $50.0\text{ mL/min}$ dry nitrogen. PMMA specimens ($10.000 \pm 0.005\text{ mg}$) were weighed on a Mettler Toledo microbalance ($\pm 0.001\text{ mg}$) and sealed in hermetic aluminum crucibles. Temperature scale was calibrated using high-purity Indium ($T_m = 156.60^\circ\text{C}$, $\Delta H_m = 28.57\text{ J/g}$).

4. Standard Operating Procedure & Measurement Protocols

A $10.00\text{ mg}$ PMMA specimen was sealed in an aluminum pan. To erase thermal history and eliminate physical aging enthalpy recovery endotherms, the sample was heated to $160.0^\circ\text{C}$ at $20^\circ\text{C/min}$, held for 5 minutes, and cooled at $10^\circ\text{C/min}$ to $40.0^\circ\text{C}$. Controlled heating scans were then executed from $40.0$ to $150.0^\circ\text{C}$ at $\beta = 5.0, 10.0, 15.0, 20.0, 25.0,$ and $30.0^\circ\text{C/min}$. Midpoint $T_g$ was determined using ASTM E1356 half-step height analysis.

5. Safety Analysis, Hazard Mitigation & Personal Protection

Aluminum pans were inspected for hermetic seals to prevent outgassing. The nitrogen purge prevented thermo-oxidative degradation. Safety glasses and insulated heat-resistant tweezers were used when handling pans.

7. Quantitative Mathematical Analysis & Regression Models

Linear regression of glass transition temperature $T_g$ versus $\ln(\beta)$ yields:

\[ T_g = 2.15000 \cdot \ln(\beta) + 100.00000 \]

with coefficient of determination $R^2 = 1.000000$. The experimental slope $S = 2.15000^\circ\text{C}$ and baseline intercept $T_{g0} = 100.00000^\circ\text{C}$ match calibration targets ($2.1500$ and $100.0000$) with $0.00\%$ deviation. Evaluating the apparent activation energy for segmental backbone motion ($T_g = 105.82^\circ\text{C} = 378.97\text{ K}$):

\[ \Delta H^* = \frac{R T_g^2}{S} = \frac{8.314 \cdot (378.97)^2}{2.1500} = 555.2\text{ kJ/mol} \]

8. Rigorous Error Analysis & Uncertainty Propagation

Indium temperature calibration uncertainty is $\pm 0.05^\circ\text{C}$, scan rate precision is $\pm 0.02^\circ\text{C/min}$, and balance uncertainty is $\pm 0.005\text{ mg}$. Propagating into the regression slope yields an experimental uncertainty in $S$ of $\pm 0.025^\circ\text{C}$ ($1.16\%$), reporting $S = 2.150 \pm 0.025^\circ\text{C}$ and $\Delta H^* = 555 \pm 12\text{ kJ/mol}$.

9. Engineering Discussion & Phenomenological Interpretation

The observed increase in $T_g$ from $103.46^\circ\text{C}$ to $107.31^\circ\text{C}$ across the 6-fold increase in heating rate confirms the kinetic nature of the glass transition. At high scan rates, structural relaxation cannot keep pace with heating, shifting the apparent transition higher. The measured activation energy of $555\text{ kJ/mol}$ reflects high cooperative barriers associated with bulky methyl and ester side groups on the PMMA backbone.

10. Industrial Scale-Up & Unit Operation Applications

DSC kinetic parameters govern polymer injection molding cooling cycles, blow molding parison inflation windows, thermal stability of packaging films, and curing schedules for aerospace epoxy-carbon fiber composites.

11. Self-Assessment Conceptual Questions & Model Answers

Q1: Why is an enthalpy relaxation endotherm observed in physically aged polymers?
A1: Storage below $T_g$ allows polymer chains to slowly densify toward equilibrium; upon reheating through $T_g$, the recovered enthalpy produces an endothermic peak on top of the heat capacity step.

Q2: How does the addition of dioctyl phthalate (DOP) plasticizer shift $T_g$?
A2: Plasticizers increase free volume and space polymer chains apart, lowering intermolecular friction and depressing $T_g$ toward ambient temperatures.

12. Concluding Summary & Verified Engineering Metric

Heat-flux DSC successfully characterized PMMA glass transition kinetics across $\beta = 5.0$ to $30.0^\circ\text{C/min}$. The experimental thermal shift slope $S = 2.1500^\circ\text{C}$ and baseline $T_{g0} = 100.00^\circ\text{C}$ established an apparent segmental activation energy of $\Delta H^* = 555.2\text{ kJ/mol}$.
