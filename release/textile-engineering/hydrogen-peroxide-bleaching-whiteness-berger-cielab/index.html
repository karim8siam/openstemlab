FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Investigate the oxidative degradation of natural cotton chromophores by perhydroxyl anions (OOH⁻) across bleaching times, quantifying Berger whiteness. Standardized parameter settings: 15.0, 25.0, 35.0, 45.0, 55.0, 65.0 min.

  1. Specimen Conditioning & Atmospheric Equilibration: Condition all textile test specimens in the standard conditioning room at 20.0 ± 2.0 °C and 65.0 ± 4.0% relative humidity for a minimum of 24 hours per ISO 139.
  2. Visual Inspection & Apparatus Zero Tare: Inspect all mechanical grips, pneumatic clamps, optical mirrors, and electronic load cells. Execute zero tare balance on digital indicators and calibrate displacement gauges.
  3. Initial Gauge Alignment & Clamping Pre-Tension: Mount the specimen securely between jaws or on the test platform. Apply standardized pre-tension to eliminate slack and align the bleaching duration with the primary zero datum.
  4. Systematic Parameter Variation & Acquisition: Increment the bleaching duration systematically across the designated range (15.0 to 65.0 min), permitting full mechanical or chemical stabilization at each test increment.
  5. Multi-Sensor Telemetry & Signal Logging: Record the primary dependent variable (Berger Whiteness Index WI) alongside auxiliary channel telemetry. Verify linear proportionality and signal stability before advancing.
  6. Controlled De-Energization & Data Archival: Release all mechanical clamping loads smoothly to zero, de-energize primary drives, secure moving linkages, and export all digital telemetry observations to the persistent laboratory notebook.
