FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Identify mineral inclusions, paste matrix fabric, and volcanic/sand temper volumetric percentage in archaeological sherds using point-counting polarized microscopy. Standardized parameter settings: 8.0, 14.0, 20.0, 26.0, 32.0, 38.0 grains/100.

  1. Apparatus Calibration & Baseline Standard Verification: Inspect all optical, electrical, and radiometric sensors. Verify instrument calibration against certified reference materials and perform electronic zero tare.
  2. Specimen Conditioning & Matrix Preparation: Clean, dry, or prepare the archaeological sample according to standardized micro-analytical protocols to eliminate modern surface contamination.
  3. Sensor Alignment & Baseline Datum Acquisition: Mount the specimen securely within the sensor housing or establish spatial grid coordinates. Verify alignment of the point-counted inclusions with primary excavation datum.
  4. Systematic Parameter Variation & Acquisition: Increment the point-counted inclusions systematically across the designated domain (8.0 to 38.0 grains/100), permitting full signal equilibration at each measurement station.
  5. Multi-Channel Telemetry Recording & Signal Integration: Record the primary dependent variable (Temper Volumetric Area Fraction V_v) alongside auxiliary environmental monitors. Validate signal stability and confirm low noise thresholds.
  6. Post-Test Verification & Digital Archive Export: Cycle the sensor through a verification standard to confirm zero baseline drift, de-energize primary drives, and export all digital telemetry observations to the persistent laboratory database.
