Agricultural and physical foundations of Disc Plow Penetration Draft.

Disc plows are widely employed in hard, dry, stony, or root-infested soils where moldboard plows suffer severe share wear or fail to penetrate. Unlike moldboard plows which slide continuously through the furrow slice, the individually mounted concave steel discs roll along the furrow line, cutting, lifting, and rolling the soil through a combined action of disc angle (the horizontal angle between the disc diameter and the direction of travel, typically 40° to 45°) and tilt angle (the vertical inclination from perpendicular, typically 15° to 25°). Penetration depends almost entirely on implement deadweight rather than suction. Because the soil reaction force exerts an oblique thrust across the disc face, disc plows generate substantial lateral side draft which must be balanced by a spring-loaded furrow wheel or tractor stabilizer sway chains. In this laboratory investigation, a mounted 3-disc plow instrumented with multi-axis load cells is tested across increasing working depths to evaluate the penetration resistance gradient c_d and the resultant draft vector.

Viva voce questions & analytical theory

Distinguish between disc angle and tilt angle in a disc plow.

Disc angle is the horizontal angle formed between the plane of the cutting disc rim and the direction of forward travel (controlling furrow width and cutting action), while tilt angle is the vertical angle between the disc face and the vertical plane (controlling soil lifting, turning, and penetration).

Why do disc plows rely primarily on heavy deadweight for soil penetration?

Unlike moldboard plows which feature bottom share suction that draws the tool into the ground, rolling discs have upward vertical soil reactions that tend to push the discs out of the ground; hence, heavy frame weight is required to force the cutting edges into hard soil.

How is lateral side draft counteracted in mounted disc plows?

Side draft is counteracted by a spring-loaded, angled rear furrow wheel that presses against the vertical furrow wall, transferring lateral soil reactions into the undisturbed ground, supplemented by tractor lower link sway stabilizers.

Under what agricultural conditions is a disc plow preferred over a moldboard plow?

Disc plows are preferred in very hard dry soils, abrasive stony ground where shares would break, sticky soils that do not scour on moldboards, and land with heavy roots or shrub stumps because the rolling discs roll over obstacles.

What is the function of the disc scrapers mounted on each disc standard?

Scrapers peel sticky soil and residues from the concave disc face, assisting in inverting the furrow slice and preventing soil buildup that would destroy the cutting and rolling geometry.

How does increasing the tilt angle affect penetration and draft?

Increasing tilt angle improves penetration in soft to medium soils by providing more horizontal cutting action, but excessive tilt reduces the soil turning ability and can increase vertical bearing resistance.

Experimental precautions and agricultural field safety

  • Ensure disc sealed bearings spin freely by hand without axial play or binding before field trials.
  • Verify that the rear furrow wheel spring tension is sufficiently preloaded to prevent lateral crab steering.
  • Keep clear of rotating discs during operation; never stand behind the implement when lowering under hydraulic power.
  • Check that all three discs are set to identical disc and tilt angles to avoid inter-disc draft imbalances.

Theoretical assumptions & biological limits

  • Assumes consistent soil penetration resistance without hidden boulder strikes or subsoil rock shelves.
  • Linear depth relationship applies within normal plowing depth limits (10 to 25 cm) before the disc hub begins to drag on the soil surface.
  • Side draft measurements can be influenced by tire side-wall deflection in the furrow bottom.
  • Does not account for bearing frictional torque degradation under prolonged dusty field service.
