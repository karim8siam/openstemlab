Biomedical assumptions and boundary condition limits

The linear approximation applies to initial inversion times (TI ≤ 600 ms); full recovery curves require three-parameter non-linear fitting. Evaluated on a benchtop 1.0 Tesla permanent-magnet NMR/MRI spectrometer (Larmor frequency f_0 = 42.58 MHz for ¹H) using an aqueous CuSO₄ doped tissue phantom at 22 °C. Repetition time TR = 5000 ms ensures full equilibrium recovery between shots. Linear inversion recovery slope k_t = 0.00145 ms⁻¹ yields an empirical T_1 ≈ 2 / k_t = 1379 ms across inversion times TI from 100 to 600 ms.

FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Determine the longitudinal spin-lattice relaxation rate constant k_t and calculate T₁ relaxation time of a biological tissue phantom using an MRI inversion recovery pulse sequence. Standardized parameter settings: 100.0, 200.0, 300.0, 400.0, 500.0, 600.0 ms.

01

Magnet Thermal Stabilization and Shimming

Power on permanent magnet temperature controller, allow 30 minutes for thermal stabilization, and adjust active gradient shim coils to achieve linewidth < 5 Hz.

02

90° and 180° RF Pulse Duration Calibration

Execute nutation pulse sweep to determine exact RF pulse widths for 90° excitation (typically 6.5 μs) and 180° inversion (13.0 μs).

03

Sample Loading and Thermal Equilibration

Insert 5 mm NMR sample tube containing aqueous CuSO₄ phantom into probe coil and allow 10 minutes for thermal equilibration at 22.0 °C.

04

Inversion Recovery Sequence Execution

Set repetition time TR = 5000 ms (> 3 · T_1) and apply 180° inversion pulse; after inversion time delay TI = 100 ms, apply 90° readout pulse.

05

FID Amplitude Integration

Digitize the free induction decay (FID) signal, perform phase correction, and record initial FID amplitude as proportional to M_z(TI).

06

Inversion Time Progression Sequence

Increment inversion delay TI through 200, 300, 400, 500, and 600 ms, capturing normalized longitudinal magnetization M_z/M_0 at each step.

07

T_1 Relaxation Rate Linear Regression

Plot normalized magnetization M_z/M_0 versus inversion time TI, extract initial slope k_t, and compute spin-lattice relaxation time T_1 = 2 / k_t.
