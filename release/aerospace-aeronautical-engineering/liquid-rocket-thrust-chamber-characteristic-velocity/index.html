FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Measure propellant mass flow rate m_dot, chamber pressure P_c, and thrust F to determine characteristic exhaust velocity c* and thrust coefficient C_F. Standardized parameter settings: 1.5, 2.2, 3.0, 4.0, 5.0, 6.0 kg/s.

01

Cryogenic Chilldown and Purge

Execute gaseous nitrogen purge and liquid oxygen line chilldown until liquid temperature reaches 90 K at injector inlet.

02

Torch Igniter Activation

Ignite methane-oxygen torch pilot flame, verifying optical ignition sense before main propellant valve opening.

03

Main Valve Opening and Low-Throttle Fire

Open main LOX and RP-1 propellant valves to establish baseline firing at m_dot = 1.5 kg/s, stabilizing P_c = 1050 kPa.

04

Systematic Mass Flow Throttling

Throttle propellant flow through m_dot = 2.2, 3.0, 4.0, 5.0, and 6.0 kg/s while maintaining constant oxidizer-to-fuel ratio (O/F = 2.4).

05

Chamber Pressure and Thrust Recording

Record steady-state chamber pressure P_c and thrust F across 2000 samples per channel at each throttle setting.

06

Characteristic Velocity Linear Regression

Plot P_c (kPa) versus m_dot (kg/s), perform linear regression to determine slope c*/A_t, and compute c* from throat area A_t = 0.0025 m^2.
