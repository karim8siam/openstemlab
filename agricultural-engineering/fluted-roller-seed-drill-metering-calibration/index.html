Agricultural assumptions and boundary condition limits

The calibration applies strictly to the tested seed lot; different crop varieties or seed treatments require re-calibration. Evaluated at constant drive shaft rotational speed of 40 RPM using certified seed wheat (thousand-grain weight 42.5 g, bulk density 0.78 g/cm³).

FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Calibrate an agricultural fluted-roller seed drill metering mechanism and establish the linear discharge characteristic across variable active roller exposures. Standardized parameter settings: 10.0, 15.0, 20.0, 25.0, 30.0, 35.0 mm.

01

Seed Inspection & Hopper Loading

Fill the seed drill hopper with clean, certified seed wheat, ensuring seeds are free of chaff, broken kernels, and foreign matter to prevent flute clogging.

02

Initial Flute Exposure Setting

Loosen the lateral feed adjustment collar and set the fluted roller active exposure to L = 10.0 mm using the vernier micrometer, locking the collar clamp securely.

03

Pre-Run Prime & Leveling

Operate the drive shaft for 20 revolutions to fill all flute recesses and establish steady-state gravity seed flow across all cups.

04

Timed Calibration Collection

Place catch pans under the seed tubes, operate the drive shaft at exactly 40 RPM for exactly 1.0 minute (or 50 revolutions), and collect all discharged grain.

05

Gravimetric Weighing

Weigh the collected seed from each row on the electronic balance, averaging across the rows to calculate mean discharge rate q (g/min).

06

Linear Calibration Curve

Repeat the procedure at 15, 20, 25, 30, and 35 mm flute exposures, plot discharge versus exposure, and extract the calibration gradient c_q.
