FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Determine incident planar shock wave speed and pressure jump in a diaphragm-driven shock tube as a function of driver-to-driven gas pressure ratio P_4 / P_1. Standardized parameter settings: 0.15, 0.25, 0.38, 0.5, 0.65, 0.8 dimensionless.

01

Diaphragm Clamping and Evacuation

Clamp a pre-scored aluminum diaphragm between flanges and evacuate the driven section to initial pressure P_1 = 10 kPa.

02

Driver Section Charging

Charge the driver section with nitrogen to pressure P_4, setting target pressure ratios corresponding to parameter x = 0.15 to 0.80.

03

Diaphragm Rupture Execution

Trigger the pneumatic piercer to burst the diaphragm, generating an unsteady planar shock wave and backward expansion fan.

04

Time-of-Flight Recording

Record the trigger timestamps as the shock front passes sensor stations spaced Delta x = 1.0 m apart.

05

Shock Speed and Mach Calculation

Compute shock propagation speed V_s = Delta x / Delta t and incident shock Mach number M_s = V_s / a_1.

06

Shock Tube Equation Regression

Plot (M_s - 1.0) versus acoustic parameter x, perform linear regression to determine coupling slope m, and verify 1D gasdynamics.
