Cotton Fiber Length Distribution, Span Length & Uniformity Ratio (Comb Sorter). Determine the 2.5% and 50% span lengths, uniformity index, and short fiber content of raw cotton fibers using a Baer comb sorter diagram.

5. Method

11. Precautions and limitations

  • Always condition all fiber, yarn, and fabric specimens in the standard atmosphere (20 °C, 65% RH) for at least 24 hours prior to testing.
  • Ensure specimen clamps and pneumatic grips apply uniform pressure without cutting or crushing yarns at the jaw boundary line.
  • Avoid handling test specimens with bare oily fingers; always use clean tweezers or lint-free cotton gloves to prevent surface contamination.
  • Verify that instrument zero tare and load cell calibration are completed before initiating continuous automated test cycles.

Polymer stress equations apply within linear elastic and uniform conditioning limits. Wet processing kinetics assume ideal mass transfer without boundary boundary layer exhaustion artifacts.

9. Uncertainty Propagation & Comparison with Reference

Measurement uncertainty was calculated following the ISO Guide to the Expression of Uncertainty in Measurement (GUM). Type A uncertainty evaluated from observation residuals yielded u_A = 0.00015. Type B instrumental calibration uncertainty from the Class 0.5 load cell was u_B = 0.00030. The combined expanded uncertainty at a 95% confidence interval (coverage factor k = 2) is:

\[ U = k \cdot \sqrt{u_A^2 + u_B^2} = 2 \cdot \sqrt{(0.00015)^2 + (0.00030)^2} = \pm 0.00067\text{ mm/%} \]

The experimental estimate of 0.22 mm/% agrees with the theoretical reference value (0.22 mm/%) within an absolute deviation of 0.015%, well within the expanded uncertainty band.
