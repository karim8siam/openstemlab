FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Measure longitudinal surface bending strain and calculate fiber stresses along a loaded cantilever beam using bonded foil strain gauges and a digital Wheatstone bridge indicator. Standardized parameter settings: 20.0, 40.0, 60.0, 80.0, 100.0, 120.0 N.

  1. Visual Inspection & Apparatus Zero Tare: Inspect all mechanical fixtures, load train alignments, safety cages, and hydraulic/electrical connections. Execute zero tare on digital indicators and balance the measurement bridge.
  2. Initial Calibration & Datum Alignment: Apply a minor pre-load to eliminate mechanical backlash and seating slack. Calibrate the tip load force against the primary standard datum.
  3. Systematic Parameter Variation & Settling: Increment the tip load force systematically across the designated experimental range (20.0 to 120.0 N), permitting mechanical and thermal equilibrium at each step.
  4. Multi-Sensor Telemetry Acquisition: Record the primary dependent variable (Root Fiber Bending Stress σ_b) alongside auxiliary channel telemetry. Verify linear proportionality and signal stability before advancing.
  5. Forward & Reverse Hysteresis Verification: Execute both ascending loading and descending unloading sequences to detect any friction lag, mechanical backlash, or permanent plastic deformation in the test assembly.
  6. Controlled De-Energization & Data Archival: Release all mechanical loads smoothly to zero, de-energize primary drives, secure moving linkages, and export all digital telemetry observations to the persistent laboratory notebook.
