FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Determine the sensible heat transfer, moisture carrying capacity, and psychrometric drying potential of heated air across deep grain beds using psychrometric enthalpy balances. Standardized parameter settings: 35.0, 42.0, 49.0, 56.0, 63.0, 70.0 °C.

01

Ambient Air Baseline Acquisition

Record ambient air dry-bulb temperature (20.0°C) and relative humidity (60.0%), calculating baseline humidity ratio W_in = 8.80 g H₂O/kg dry air from psychrometric relations.

02

Duct Heating & Airflow Stabilization

Set duct air velocity to 0.4 m/s through the grain column, powering the heating stage to achieve initial dry-bulb temperature T_db = 35.0°C.

03

Sensible Heating Verification

Verify that absolute humidity ratio W upstream of the grain bed matches ambient W_in, confirming pure sensible heating without moisture addition.

04

Exhaust Psychrometric Logging

Measure exhaust air wet-bulb and dry-bulb temperatures downstream of the grain bed, determining saturation humidity ratio W_sat at the adiabatic wet-bulb temperature.

05

Temperature Stepping

Increment heater temperature in five uniform stages to 42, 49, 56, 63, and 70°C, recording steady-state inlet and outlet psychrometric states for each run.

06

Drying Potential Linear Regression

Calculate evaporative capacity ΔW = W_sat - W_in for each temperature, plot ΔW versus T_db, and determine the thermal drying sensitivity gradient s_dp.
