FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Perform manual red blood cell enumeration of feline whole blood using a Neubauer hemocytometer grid to evaluate non-regenerative anemia without automated impedance artifacts. Standardized parameter settings: 5.0, 10.0, 15.0, 20.0, 25.0, 30.0 μL.

  1. Step 1

    Diagnostic Setup & Zero-Baseline Calibration

    Initialize the veterinary diagnostic equipment for Feline Manual Hemocytometry, calibrate baseline telemetry against certified reference standards, and verify optical/mechanical alignment.

  2. Step 2

    Biological Specimen Collection & Conditioning

    Collect, anticoagulate, centrifuge, or dilute patient biological specimens under strictly regulated environmental conditions conforming to CLSI veterinary guidelines.

  3. Step 3

    Independent Setpoint Parameter Adjustment

    Adjust the independent testing parameter (Dilution Volume) across the designated range (5.0 to 30.0 μL) in steady increments.

  4. Step 4

    Diagnostic Telemetry Acquisition & Logging

    Execute the analytical run and record real-time clinical readings for Chamber Enumerated Erythrocyte Density with high-precision digital telemetry.

  5. Step 5

    Replicate Inspection & Matrix Stability Check

    Verify signal stability, inspect sample clarity, and confirm replicate reproducibility across sequential diagnostic scans.

  6. Step 6

    Regression Calculation & Clinical Parameter Derivation

    Plot Dilution Volume (μL) against Erythrocyte Density (10⁶/μL), calculate ordinary least-squares (OLS) regression parameters, and derive Neubauer Chamber Microdilution Scaling Factor (m) against certified veterinary reference benchmarks.
