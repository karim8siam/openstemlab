FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Quantify the resonance angle shift of a gold-film SPR immunosensor as a function of analyte protein concentration to determine association kinetics and Langmuir adsorption linearity. Standardized parameter settings: 5.0, 10.0, 15.0, 20.0, 25.0, 30.0 nM.

01

Sensor Chip Docking and Buffer Baseline Stabilization

Dock the functionalized CM5 gold chip into the optical block and prime flow channels with HBS-EP running buffer at 30 μL/min until baseline drift is < 0.2 mdeg/min.

02

Reference Flowcell Subtraction Setup

Configure Channel 1 as non-specific reference (mock-immobilized) and Channel 2 as active antibody surface for inline differential subtraction.

03

Initial Antigen Injection (5.0 nM)

Inject 5.0 nM human IgG antigen for 180 seconds, allowing binding association to reach equilibrium plateau; log net resonance angle shift Δθ in millidegrees.

04

Surface Regeneration Cycle

Inject a 30-second pulse of 10 mM glycine-HCl (pH 2.0) to dissociate bound antigen and regenerate baseline antibody binding capacity.

05

Concentration Progression Sequence

Sequentially inject antigen standards of 10.0, 15.0, 20.0, 25.0, and 30.0 nM, executing regeneration and baseline re-referencing at each step.

06

Sensitivity Regression and Affinity Analysis

Plot steady-state angle shift Δθ against concentration C_ag, performing linear regression to evaluate SPR refractive sensitivity k_spr.
