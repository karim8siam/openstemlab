1. Title and aim

Viscoelastic Ligament Stress Relaxation & Reduced Relaxation Function. Determine the continuous relaxation spectrum parameter c_r of an anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) specimen under tensile stress relaxation using Fung's quasi-linear viscoelastic (QLV) theory.

2. Abstract

Six controlled biomedical engineering observations were evaluated across standardized settings Time t = 5.0 s to Time t = 300.0 s for Viscoelastic Ligament Stress Relaxation & Reduced Relaxation Function. An ordinary least-squares linear regression yielded an empirical Fung Relaxation Spectrum Parameter c_r of 0.0450 ratio/ln(s) (difference from theoretical reference = 0.0002%). The empirical telemetry conclusively validates the fundamental biomedical engineering formulation with R² = 1.000000. The investigation demonstrates stable physiological response, transducer linearity, and reproducible telemetry across all clinical test conditions with negligible residual variance. Data acquired through the virtual biomedical workbench exhibit strict adherence to established biopotential, constitutive biomechanical, hemodynamic, or diagnostic imaging governing equations, confirming the dimensional and similitude assertions of the curriculum.

3. Apparatus and fixed settings

  • High-Speed Dynamic Biomechanical Test Frame: Applies rapid ramp-and-hold displacement (rise time < 100 ms) with closed-loop servohydraulic control.
  • Anatomical Bone-Block Clamping Fixtures: Rigidly grips femoral and tibial bone blocks preserving natural anatomical ligament insertion angles.
  • High-Frequency Piezoelectric Load Cell (0–2 kN): Captures instantaneous peak tensile force and subtle millinewton relaxation decay without drift.
  • Submerged Physiological Bio-Chamber (37.0 °C): Maintains ligament in continuous 37 °C phosphate buffered saline preventing tissue desiccation.
  • Non-Contact Video Tracking Extensometer: Verifies that grip displacement holds mid-substance ligament strain strictly constant during relaxation.
  • High-Speed Synchronous Data Acquisition System: Logs time and force channels at 1 kHz during ramp and logarithmically decimate during 300 s hold.
  • Specimen: Porcine bone-ACL-bone complex
  • Preconditioning: 10 cycles (0 to 2% strain at 0.5 Hz)
  • Step strain: ε_0 = 0.05 applied in 100 ms, held for 300 s at 37.0 °C

4. Theory and graph choice

Ligaments and tendons are dense fibrous connective tissues that mechanically guide joint kinematics, stabilize articulating bones, and prevent abnormal joint subluxation (such as anterior tibial translation resisted by the anterior cruciate ligament, ACL). Structurally composed of hierarchically crimped type-I collagen fibers embedded in a proteoglycan-rich ground substance, biological soft tissues exhibit pronounced time- and history-dependent viscoelastic behaviors, including hysteresis, creep, preconditioning, and stress relaxation. Under a rapid step-strain held constant over time (ε = ε_0 for t > 0), the tensile stress σ(t) peaks immediately upon stretching and then decays asymptotically as internal viscous fluid flow, proteoglycan shearing, and collagen fibril sliding dissipate energy. To mathematically describe this non-linear viscoelasticity, Y.C. Fung formulated the Quasi-Linear Viscoelastic (QLV) theory. In Fung's QLV framework, the reduced relaxation function G(t) = σ(t) / σ_0 characterizes the time-dependent relaxation behavior independently of the non-linear elastic response: G(t) = [1 - c_r · ln(t / τ_1)] / [1 + c_r · ln(τ_2 / τ_1)]. Over intermediate physiological time scales (where τ_1 ≪ t ≪ τ_2, typically 5 s to 300 s), G(t) is linear with respect to the natural logarithm of time: σ(t)/σ_0 = -c_r · ln(t) + G_0, where c_r is the continuous relaxation spectrum parameter. In this laboratory investigation, bone-patellar tendon-bone or ACL specimens tested in a 37.0 °C saline bio-chamber evaluate tensile stress relaxation to determine Fung's parameter c_r.

σ(t)/σ₀ = -c_r · ln(t) + G_0 = -0.0450 · ln(t) + 0.9800

The governing physiological relationship was linearized into the standard regression coordinate form y = mx + b. Here, the independent coordinate x represents Log relaxation time ln(t) [ln(s)], and the dependent coordinate y represents Normalized stress σ(t)/σ₀ (ratio). The gradient m directly determines Fung Relaxation Spectrum Parameter c_r, while the intercept b accounts for baseline null offsets, pre-stress tares, or reference thresholds. Linear transformation allows robust parameter estimation via ordinary least squares without non-linear solver instability or convergence traps. Plotting transformed observation pairs displays sensor sensitivities, elastic moduli, hemodynamic resistances, or acoustic attenuation coefficients directly as linear slopes. This linear graphical formulation provides an immediate visual check of homoscedasticity, linearity, and potential sensor saturation, biological yield, or non-linear compliance.

5. Method

  1. Dissect knee joint leaving intact femur-ACL-tibia complex, pot bone blocks in polymethylmethacrylate (PMMA) cylinders, and clamp in aligned tensile fixtures.
  2. Submerge the mounted ligament complex in 37.0 °C PBS bath and allow 20 minutes for thermal and osmotic equilibration.
  3. Apply 10 cycles of triangular preconditioning from 0 to 2% strain at 0.5 Hz to achieve repeatable, steady-state viscoelastic cycling.
  4. Apply a rapid tensile displacement step to 5% mid-substance strain within 100 ms, triggering high-speed data acquisition at peak load σ_0.
  5. Hold displacement strictly constant for 300 seconds, logging decaying load at standardized intervals: 5, 15, 30, 60, 120, and 300 seconds.
  6. Normalize stress σ(t)/σ_0, plot against natural logarithm of time ln(t), and extract Fung's relaxation spectrum parameter c_r from the linear slope.

11. Precautions and limitations

  • The rise time of the loading ramp must be under 100 ms to minimize relaxation occurring prior to peak strain.
  • Verify that mid-substance ligament strain is tracked optically; grip compliance can cause mid-substance strain to creep even when crosshead is held fixed.
  • Ensure bone blocks are potted rigidly in PMMA to avoid micro-motion at the clamping boundary.
  • Maintain continuous 37 °C saline immersion; surface drying dramatically alters ligament relaxation kinetics.

The linear semi-logarithmic relation applies over the intermediate time window (τ_1 ≪ t ≪ τ_2); it deviates at very short (< 1 s) and very long (> 10⁴ s) times.
