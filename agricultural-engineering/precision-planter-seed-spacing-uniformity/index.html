FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Evaluate the seed spacing uniformity, miss index, and multiple index of a vacuum disc precision planter unit as a function of forward planting speed. Standardized parameter settings: 3.0, 4.2, 5.4, 6.6, 7.8, 9.0 km/h.

01

Seed Disc Selection & Vacuum Set

Install a 24-hole corn disc (4.5 mm hole diameter) into the metering unit, setting the vacuum level to 4.5 kPa using the variable-frequency blower.

02

Singulator Clearance Adjustment

Adjust the dual singulator fingers to dislodge doubles while retaining single kernels over each suction orifice.

03

Greased Belt Stand Synchronization

Set the belt drive motor to initial forward speed V = 3.0 km/h, synchronizing the planter drive shaft to achieve a nominal theoretical seed spacing of 15.0 cm.

04

Continuous Seed Drop Logging

Operate the planter unit to deposit a sample run of at least 250 consecutive seeds onto the moving greased belt.

05

Spatial Coordinate Mapping

Record the center-to-center distances between consecutive seeds using the laser scanner, computing mean spacing x_bar, standard deviation σ_s, miss index, and multiple index.

06

Velocity Stepping

Repeat the test across speeds of 4.2, 5.4, 6.6, 7.8, and 9.0 km/h, plotting spacing standard deviation versus speed to determine sensitivity b_v.
