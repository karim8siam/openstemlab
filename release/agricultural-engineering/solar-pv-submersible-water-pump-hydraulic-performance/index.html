FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Evaluate the electrical-to-hydraulic power conversion efficiency and water discharge rate of a solar PV-powered brushless submersible drip irrigation pump across varying solar irradiance power levels. Standardized parameter settings: 200, 350, 500, 650, 800, 950 W.

01

Test Column Priming and Pressure Calibration

Submerge the pump assembly, purge air from the discharge column, and adjust the throttle valve to maintain constant 35.0 m total dynamic head.

02

DC Power Simulator Initialization

Configure the programmable DC power supply to simulate the output of a 1.2 kWp solar PV array at an initial power level of 200 W.

03

MPPT Inverter Tracking Stabilization

Energize the motor controller and allow 3 minutes for the internal MPPT algorithm to track the optimum power voltage and stabilize pump RPM.

04

Hydraulic Flow and Pressure Acquisition

Record stabilized discharge flow rate Q (m³/h), delivery pressure head H (m), DC voltage V_dc, and current I_dc over a 5-minute sampling window.

05

Electrical Power Progression Sequence

Increment PV simulator power through 350, 500, 650, 800, and 950 W, repeating hydraulic power and telemetry logging at each step.

06

Hydraulic Power Calculation and Regression

Calculate hydraulic power P_hyd = ρ · g · Q · H and plot against array electrical power P_elec, computing subsystem efficiency η_sys.
