FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Measure the liquid discharge rate and patternation uniformity of an agricultural hydraulic flat-fan spray nozzle across varying operating pressures using a patterned patternator table. Standardized parameter settings: 1.0, 1.3, 1.6, 1.9, 2.2, 2.5 bar^0.5.

01

Nozzle Mounting & Boom Alignment

Mount the ISO 110-02 nozzle on the test boom at exactly 50 cm height above the patternator table, aligning the spray fan at a 5° angle to the boom axis to prevent interference.

02

Pressure Stabilization

Start the diaphragm pump, bleeding all air from the boom line, and adjust the pressure relief valve to initial pressure P = 1.00 bar (√P = 1.00 bar^0.5).

03

Timed Discharge Measurement

Collect the full nozzle discharge in a graduated cylinder over exactly 60 seconds, recording the total volume Q (L/min) on the digital balance.

04

Patternator Volume Logging

Allow spray to drain into the patternator graduated tubes for 2.0 minutes, recording the liquid height in each channel to plot the transverse distribution profile.

05

Pressure Stepping

Increase operating pressure across five additional increments up to 6.25 bar (√P = 1.3, 1.6, 1.9, 2.2, 2.5 bar^0.5), repeating discharge and droplet VMD scans.

06

Flow Coefficient Extraction

Plot discharge Q against √P, perform linear regression to determine nozzle flow coefficient C_n, and evaluate the pressure-droplet size trade-off.
