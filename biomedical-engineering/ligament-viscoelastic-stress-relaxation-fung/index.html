Biomedical assumptions and boundary condition limits

The linear semi-logarithmic relation applies over the intermediate time window (τ_1 ≪ t ≪ τ_2); it deviates at very short (< 1 s) and very long (> 10⁴ s) times. Evaluated on porcine bone-ACL-bone complexes mounted in anatomical tensile grips in 37.0 °C saline. Subjected to a rapid ramp-and-hold tensile strain (ε_0 = 0.05 applied in 0.1 s) and held for 300 s. Semi-logarithmic slope c_r = 0.0450 ratio/ln(s) evaluated across times from 5 s to 300 s.

FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Determine the continuous relaxation spectrum parameter c_r of an anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) specimen under tensile stress relaxation using Fung's quasi-linear viscoelastic (QLV) theory. Standardized parameter settings: 5.0, 15.0, 30.0, 60.0, 120.0, 300.0 s.

01

Specimen Dissection and Anatomical Mounting

Dissect knee joint leaving intact femur-ACL-tibia complex, pot bone blocks in polymethylmethacrylate (PMMA) cylinders, and clamp in aligned tensile fixtures.

02

Saline Bio-Chamber Equilibration

Submerge the mounted ligament complex in 37.0 °C PBS bath and allow 20 minutes for thermal and osmotic equilibration.

03

Preconditioning Cyclic Protocol

Apply 10 cycles of triangular preconditioning from 0 to 2% strain at 0.5 Hz to achieve repeatable, steady-state viscoelastic cycling.

04

Ramp-and-Hold Step Strain Execution

Apply a rapid tensile displacement step to 5% mid-substance strain within 100 ms, triggering high-speed data acquisition at peak load σ_0.

05

Isometric Relaxation Data Collection

Hold displacement strictly constant for 300 seconds, logging decaying load at standardized intervals: 5, 15, 30, 60, 120, and 300 seconds.

06

Fung QLV Semi-Logarithmic Regression

Normalize stress σ(t)/σ_0, plot against natural logarithm of time ln(t), and extract Fung's relaxation spectrum parameter c_r from the linear slope.
