FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Determine the saturation drain current (I_DSS), pinch-off voltage (V_P), and mutual transconductance (g_m) of an N-channel JFET in common-source configuration. Standardized parameter settings: -3.5, -3.0, -2.5, -2.0, -1.5, -1.0 V.

  1. Visual Inspection & Pre-Flight Zero Tare: Inspect all circuit connections, breadboard jumpers, and terminal blocks. Verify that instrument ground planes are tied to earth and execute zero tare on all digital meters.
  2. Excitation Source & Baseline Calibration: Energize the regulated supply, set baseline operating thresholds, and calibrate the gate voltage v_gs using the precision standard reference meter.
  3. Systematic Parameter Variation & Settling: Increment the gate voltage v_gs systematically across the designated experimental range (-3.5 to -1.0 V), permitting thermal settling at each setpoint.
  4. Multi-Meter & Oscilloscope Waveform Acquisition: Record the primary output metric (Square Root of Drain Current √(I_D)) alongside auxiliary telemetry. Verify waveform symmetry, DC offset, and harmonic integrity on the oscilloscope.
  5. Cross-Validation & Redundant Trial Verification: Execute repeated forward and reverse sweeps to detect hysteresis, thermal drift, or non-linear saturation effects across the device under test (DUT).
  6. Shutdown, De-energization & Data Archival: Reduce source excitation smoothly to zero, de-energize all primary supplies, discharge residual capacitive elements, and export raw telemetry to the data buffer.
