FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Calibrate mass discharge response, dynamic latency, and distribution linearity of an electro-hydraulic variable-rate granular fertilizer metering roller across stepper motor drive speeds. Standardized parameter settings: 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60 RPM.

01

Fertilizer Conditioning and Hopper Loading

Inspect granular urea for caking, pass through a 4.0 mm conditioning screen, and charge the 30-liter feed hopper.

02

Motor Controller Zeroing and CAN Calibration

Initialize the stepper motor drive, verify zero backlash, and set the controller to 10 RPM test velocity.

03

Pre-Run Flute Priming

Operate the roller for 60 seconds to ensure all 12 helical flute pockets are fully primed with granular material.

04

Dynamic Gravimetric Mass Flow Acquisition

Initiate continuous mass data collection on the electronic catch scale over a precisely timed 60-second discharge window.

05

Speed Progression Sequence

Systematically increase roller speed to 20, 30, 40, 50, and 60 RPM, recording 60-second gravimetric discharge collections at each speed.

06

Linearity Regression and Gradient Analysis

Plot mass flow rate m_dot_f (kg/min) against drive speed ω (RPM), performing least-squares linear regression to determine c_m.
