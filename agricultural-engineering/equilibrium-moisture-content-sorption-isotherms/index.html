Static gravimetric equilibration requires 2 to 4 weeks per trial, during which mold can develop at high humidities (RH > 80%). Evaluated using shelled yellow dent corn at constant 25°C in static gravimetric chambers containing analytical saturated salt solutions (LiCl, MgCl₂, Mg(NO₃)₂, NaCl, KCl).

FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Determine the equilibrium moisture content (EMC) of agricultural cereal grain at constant temperature across variable relative humidities and calibrate the Modified Henderson sorption isotherm model. Standardized parameter settings: -2.25, -1.71, -1.17, -0.63, -0.09, 0.45 transformed.

01

Saturated Salt Solution Preparation

Prepare analytical saturated salt solutions (LiCl, MgCl₂, K₂CO₃, NaNO₂, NaCl, KCl) with excess undissolved crystals in six sealed desiccator jars to establish standard relative humidities: 11.3%, 32.8%, 43.2%, 64.3%, 75.3%, and 84.3% at 25.0°C.

02

Grain Sample Weighing & Loading

Weigh exactly 10.000 g of pre-wetted corn kernels into perforated stainless steel baskets, suspending one basket in the vapor headspace of each sealed salt jar.

03

Isothermal Incubation

Place all sealed desiccator jars inside the 25.0°C environmental incubator, leaving undisturbed for 21 days to ensure complete vapor-solid thermodynamic equilibrium.

04

Equilibrium Mass Verification

Weigh sample baskets every 48 hours until consecutive readings over 4 days show mass stability within ±0.002 g, confirming asymptotic EMC.

05

Oven Bone-Dry Determination

Transfer equilibrated grain samples into the 103°C oven for 72 hours to determine bone-dry mass and calculate dry-basis equilibrium moisture content M_e (%).

06

Modified Henderson Model Linearization

Transform RH and M_e into ln[-ln(1 - RH)] and ln(M_e), plot the linearized relationship, and evaluate Henderson parameters c_h and K_h via linear regression.
