FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Determine starting torque, running torque, starting winding current, and centrifugal disconnect switch operation in a single-phase capacitor-start induction motor under variable capacitance. Standardized parameter settings: 20.0, 40.0, 60.0, 80.0, 100.0, 120.0 μF.

  1. Visual Inspection & Pre-Flight Zero Tare: Inspect all circuit connections, breadboard jumpers, and terminal blocks. Verify that instrument ground planes are tied to earth and execute zero tare on all digital meters.
  2. Excitation Source & Baseline Calibration: Energize the regulated supply, set baseline operating thresholds, and calibrate the starting capacitor using the precision standard reference meter.
  3. Systematic Parameter Variation & Settling: Increment the starting capacitor systematically across the designated experimental range (20.0 to 120.0 μF), permitting thermal settling at each setpoint.
  4. Multi-Meter & Oscilloscope Waveform Acquisition: Record the primary output metric (Breakaway Starting Torque τ_start) alongside auxiliary telemetry. Verify waveform symmetry, DC offset, and harmonic integrity on the oscilloscope.
  5. Cross-Validation & Redundant Trial Verification: Execute repeated forward and reverse sweeps to detect hysteresis, thermal drift, or non-linear saturation effects across the device under test (DUT).
  6. Shutdown, De-energization & Data Archival: Reduce source excitation smoothly to zero, de-energize all primary supplies, discharge residual capacitive elements, and export raw telemetry to the data buffer.
