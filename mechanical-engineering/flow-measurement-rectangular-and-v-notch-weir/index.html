FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Calibrate rectangular suppressed weirs and a 90° triangular V-notch weir in an open tilting flume, determining discharge exponents and discharge coefficients. Standardized parameter settings: 1.0, 2.5, 4.2, 6.0, 8.0, 10.0 m^2.5 ×10⁻³.

  1. Visual Inspection & Apparatus Zero Tare: Inspect all mechanical fixtures, load train alignments, safety cages, and hydraulic/electrical connections. Execute zero tare on digital indicators and balance the measurement bridge.
  2. Initial Calibration & Datum Alignment: Apply a minor pre-load to eliminate mechanical backlash and seating slack. Calibrate the head parameter h^2.5 against the primary standard datum.
  3. Systematic Parameter Variation & Settling: Increment the head parameter h^2.5 systematically across the designated experimental range (1.0 to 10.0 m^2.5 ×10⁻³), permitting mechanical and thermal equilibrium at each step.
  4. Multi-Sensor Telemetry Acquisition: Record the primary dependent variable (Open Channel Gravimetric Flow Rate Q) alongside auxiliary channel telemetry. Verify linear proportionality and signal stability before advancing.
  5. Forward & Reverse Hysteresis Verification: Execute both ascending loading and descending unloading sequences to detect any friction lag, mechanical backlash, or permanent plastic deformation in the test assembly.
  6. Controlled De-Energization & Data Archival: Release all mechanical loads smoothly to zero, de-energize primary drives, secure moving linkages, and export all digital telemetry observations to the persistent laboratory notebook.
