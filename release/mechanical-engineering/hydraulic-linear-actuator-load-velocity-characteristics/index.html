FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Determine linear extension speed and flow-pressure drop characteristics of an electro-hydraulic ram driven by a proportional spool valve under varying mechanical opposition loads. Standardized parameter settings: 1.0, 2.5, 4.0, 5.5, 7.0, 9.0 V.

  1. Visual Inspection & Apparatus Zero Tare: Inspect all mechanical fixtures, load train alignments, safety cages, and hydraulic/electrical connections. Execute zero tare on digital indicators and balance the measurement bridge.
  2. Initial Calibration & Datum Alignment: Apply a minor pre-load to eliminate mechanical backlash and seating slack. Calibrate the valve command voltage against the primary standard datum.
  3. Systematic Parameter Variation & Settling: Increment the valve command voltage systematically across the designated experimental range (1.0 to 9.0 V), permitting mechanical and thermal equilibrium at each step.
  4. Multi-Sensor Telemetry Acquisition: Record the primary dependent variable (Hydraulic Cylinder Extension Velocity v_cyl) alongside auxiliary channel telemetry. Verify linear proportionality and signal stability before advancing.
  5. Forward & Reverse Hysteresis Verification: Execute both ascending loading and descending unloading sequences to detect any friction lag, mechanical backlash, or permanent plastic deformation in the test assembly.
  6. Controlled De-Energization & Data Archival: Release all mechanical loads smoothly to zero, de-energize primary drives, secure moving linkages, and export all digital telemetry observations to the persistent laboratory notebook.
