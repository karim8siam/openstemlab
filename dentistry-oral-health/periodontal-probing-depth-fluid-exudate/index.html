FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Quantify the clinical correlation between periodontal probing depth (PD) and gingival crevicular fluid exudate flow rate in inflamed sulci. Standardized parameter settings: 2.0, 2.5, 3.0, 3.5, 4.0, 4.5, 5.0, 5.5, 6.0, 6.5, 7.0 mm.

  1. Step 1

    Specimen Conditioning & Calibration

    Prepare and condition standardized dental specimens for Periodontal Pocket GCF Flow under strictly regulated ambient conditions (23.0 ± 1.0 °C and 50 ± 5% relative humidity) according to ISO testing standards.

  2. Step 2

    Transducer Zeroing & Baseline Initialization

    Calibrate the instrumentation zero baseline. Verify sensor sensitivity using certified reference standards prior to initiating experimental runs.

  3. Step 3

    Independent Setpoint Parameter Adjustment

    Set the independent variable (Probing Depth) across the designated experimental span (2.0 to 7.0 mm) in steady increments.

  4. Step 4

    Data Acquisition & Real-Time Monitoring

    Execute the measurement sequence and record real-time transducer telemetry for GCF Exudate Flow Rate with 64-bit precision logging.

  5. Step 5

    Replicate Verification & Artifact Inspection

    Inspect specimen integrity, confirm absent interfacial flaws or micro-cracks, and replicate measurements across successive trials.

  6. Step 6

    Mathematical Regression & Parameter Evaluation

    Plot Probing Depth (mm) against GCF Flow (µL/min), calculate ordinary least-squares fit parameters, and derive GCF Exudate Elevation Rate (k_GCF) against standard reference benchmarks.
