Physical chemistry assumptions and metrological limits

The Clément-Desormes formula assumes ideal gas behavior, strictly adiabatic expansion of the bulk gas without acoustic wave dissipation, negligible heat transfer during the 0.8 s valve release, and valid first-order Taylor expansion ln(1 + h/P0) ≈ h/P0 for small excess pressures (h/P0 < 0.03). At h = 40 cm DBP (~4 kPa), the second-order Taylor term contributes <0.2% error, which is effectively minimized by multi-point linear regression. The physical simulation model calculates the final isochoric re-equilibrated manometer pressure h₂ via the linearized Clément-Desormes relation h₂ = [ (γ − 1) / γ ] · h₁ + h₀. Thermodynamic parameters: dry diatomic gas (atmospheric air, 78% N₂, 21% O₂); equipartition degrees of freedom f = 5 (3 translational + 2 rotational); accepted theoretical adiabatic index: γ = C_p / C_v = 7/5 = 1.4000. Theoretical regression slope: m_slope = (γ − 1) / γ = (1.400 − 1.0) / 1.400 = 0.400 / 1.400 = 2/7 = 0.285714. For initial excess pressures h₁ from 15.0 cm to 40.0 cm (dibutyl phthalate manometer fluid, density ρ = 1.047 g/cm³), re-equilibrated pressures span h₂ = 4.29 cm to 11.43 cm. Ordinary least-squares linear regression isolates γ = 1 / (1 − m_slope) with R² = 1.00000; the multi-point linear regression method eliminates zero-point parallax errors and valve oscillation offsets into the intercept h₀.

Analytical chemistry apparatus & tools.

15-Liter Heavy-Walled Glass Carboy Vessel

Provides a large gas expansion volume ensuring that bulk gas in the center undergoes true adiabatic expansion unperturbed by boundary layer heat conduction from the vessel walls.

Large-Bore Ground-Glass Quick-Release Stopper Valve (20 mm)

Opens rapidly to allow instantaneous pressure equalization with ambient atmosphere (0.8 s) and seals hermetically without gas leakage.

Open-Ended U-Tube Liquid Manometer (Dibutyl Phthalate)

Hand Rubber Bellows Pump and Inline Desiccant Drying Tube

Pressurizes the carboy with dry air while scrubbing water vapor through anhydrous calcium chloride granules to prevent moisture-induced heat capacity distortions.

Digital Millisecond Solenoid Actuator and Timing Controller

Standardizes the valve opening duration to precisely 0.80 ± 0.02 seconds, eliminating human reaction time variability and preventing acoustic resonance oscillations.

Precision Cathetometer / Optical Mirror Scale (±0.05 cm)

Reads the liquid meniscus height difference across both arms of the U-tube manometer with zero parallax error.

FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Determine the heat capacity ratio (γ = C_p/C_v) and adiabatic index of dry atmospheric air using the classical Clément-Desormes method, analyzing the rapid adiabatic expansion and subsequent isochoric thermal re-warming cycles, and extracting γ via ordinary least-squares linear regression. Suggested independent volumetric settings: 15.0, 20.0, 25.0, 30.0, 35.0, 40.0 cm.

01

System Sealing and Desiccant Check

Inspect the 15-liter glass carboy, ground-glass valve, and rubber tubing connections for airtight integrity. Ensure the inline drying tube is filled with active anhydrous calcium chloride granules to guarantee that dry air enters the system.

02

Initial Carboy Pressurization

Close the main release valve. Using the hand bellows pump, gently pump dry air through the desiccant tube into the carboy until the dibutyl phthalate manometer shows an initial excess pressure slightly above the assigned h₁ value (e.g., ~27 cm for h₁ = 25.0 cm). Seal the intake clamp.

03

Thermal Pre-Equilibration

Allow the carboy to stand undisturbed for 3 to 5 minutes. Compression of the gas during pumping causes a slight initial temperature rise; observe the manometer as the gas cools back to room temperature T₀, settling at the exact target initial excess pressure h₁.

04

Rapid Adiabatic Expansion

Trigger the quick-release valve mechanism. The large-bore stopper opens, allowing excess air to rush out until internal pressure drops to atmospheric (P₂ = P₀), and automatically snaps shut after exactly 0.80 seconds. The gas inside cools adiabatically.

05

Isochoric Thermal Re-Warming and Final Pressure Logging

With the vessel sealed at constant volume (V₃ = V₂), observe the manometer liquid level rise as the chilled gas slowly absorbs heat from the room through the glass walls. After 3 to 5 minutes, when the pressure stabilizes at a constant maximum plateau, record the final re-equilibrated excess pressure h₂.

06

Multi-Pressure Series and Linear Regression

Repeat the measurement across six initial excess pressures: h₁ = 15.0, 20.0, 25.0, 30.0, 35.0, and 40.0 cm. Perform ordinary least-squares linear regression of h₂ against h₁, extract the slope m_slope, and compute the adiabatic index γ = 1 / (1 − m_slope).
