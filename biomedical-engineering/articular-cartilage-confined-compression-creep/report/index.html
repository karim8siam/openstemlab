  • Sintered Porous Titanium Platen (pore size 20 μm): Allows unhindered one-dimensional axial exudation of interstitial fluid while supporting the solid matrix.
  • Precision Stainless Steel Confined Compression Well: Provides rigid radial boundary confinement preventing lateral cartilage expansion (radial strain ε_r = 0).

  • Porous filter: Sintered titanium platen (20 μm pore size)

4. Theory and graph choice

Articular cartilage is a specialized avascular, aneural connective tissue that lines the articulating surfaces of diarthrodial joints (e.g., knee and hip), providing near-frictionless gliding and load transmission across millions of gait cycles. Mechanically, cartilage is modeled using the biphasic theory formulated by Van C. Mow, which treats the tissue as a mixture of an incompressible porous-permeable solid matrix (composed of a type-II collagen fibrillar network entrapping negatively charged aggregating proteoglycans/aggrecan) and an interstitial fluid phase (water with mobile physiological electrolytes, comprising 65–80% of wet weight). Under confined compression within an impermeable chamber loaded via a rigid porous filter platen, fluid exudes axially until interstitial fluid pressure dissipates completely. At creep equilibrium (as t → ∞), fluid flow ceases, the solid matrix alone supports the total applied compressive stress σ_c, and the tissue strain ε_c reaches a steady plateau governed by the aggregate modulus: σ_c = H_A · ε_c. The aggregate modulus H_A represents the intrinsic stiffness of the cartilage solid matrix in uniaxial confined compression, combining Young's modulus and Poisson's ratio: H_A = E·(1 - ν)/[(1 + ν)·(1 - 2ν)]. In this experiment, cylindrical osteochondral cartilage plugs are tested in a precision confined compression chamber to evaluate equilibrium stress-strain behavior and quantify H_A.

σ_c = H_A · ε_c = 0.6500 · ε_c

The governing physiological relationship was linearized into the standard regression coordinate form y = mx + b. Here, the independent coordinate x represents Equilibrium compressive strain ε_c, and the dependent coordinate y represents Equilibrium compressive stress σ_c (MPa). The gradient m directly determines Equilibrium Aggregate Modulus H_A, while the intercept b accounts for baseline null offsets, pre-stress tares, or reference thresholds. Linear transformation allows robust parameter estimation via ordinary least squares without non-linear solver instability or convergence traps. Plotting transformed observation pairs displays sensor sensitivities, elastic moduli, hemodynamic resistances, or acoustic attenuation coefficients directly as linear slopes. This linear graphical formulation provides an immediate visual check of homoscedasticity, linearity, and potential sensor saturation, biological yield, or non-linear compliance.

5. Method

  1. Harvest 4.0 mm diameter full-thickness cartilage plugs from bovine femoral condyles and measure initial uncompressed thickness h_0 using a current-sensing micrometer.
  2. Place the plug snugly into the precision stainless steel confining well, submerge in 0.15 M physiological saline, and allow 30 minutes for osmotic equilibration.
  3. Lower the porous titanium platen onto the cartilage surface until a small tare load (0.01 N) is registered, establishing the zero reference displacement position.
  4. Apply a static axial compressive stress step and track continuous vertical displacement as fluid exudes through the porous platen.
  5. Monitor displacement rate until equilibrium is reached (defined as displacement change < 0.2 μm over 15 minutes, typically requiring 45 to 60 minutes per step).
  6. Repeat creep equilibration across successive strain levels ε_c = 0.05, 0.10, 0.15, 0.20, 0.25, and 0.30, and determine the equilibrium aggregate modulus H_A.

11. Precautions and limitations

  • Ensure perfect cylindrical fit of the specimen in the confining well to eliminate lateral fluid leakage around the sample perimeter.
  • Maintain constant 0.15 M NaCl ionic strength; hypoosmotic solutions cause proteoglycan swelling that alters baseline thickness.
  • Wait until creep rate satisfies the rigorous equilibrium criterion (dε/dt < 10⁻⁶ s⁻¹) before recording equilibrium stress.
  • Keep testing temperature stable at 22 °C to avoid thermal expansion drift of the displacement transducer frame.

The linear aggregate modulus model applies to infinitesimal to moderate strains (ε_c ≤ 0.30); higher compressions exhibit strain-dependent stiffening.
