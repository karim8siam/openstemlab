FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Quantify hillslope sheet and rill erosion rates using calibrated washer-pin transects and validate Universal Soil Loss Equation (USLE) rainfall erosivity predictions. Standardized parameter settings: 50.0, 100.0, 150.0, 200.0, 250.0, 300.0 MJ·mm/(ha·h).

  1. Sensor Calibration & Zero Benchmark Verification: Inspect all hydrographic, meteorological, or optical sensors. Verify calibration against certified reference instruments and establish digital zero tare.
  2. Field Site Setup & Geodetic Survey Benchmark Alignment: Deploy the measurement rig across the designated landscape transect or flume channel. Align survey axes with local geodetic benchmarks and leveling datum.
  3. Initial Gauge Alignment & Parameter Setpoint Selection: Position the sensor array at the target sampling station. Select the initial rainfall erosivity factor r setpoint and confirm steady-state baseline readings.
  4. Systematic Parameter Variation & Acquisition: Increment the rainfall erosivity factor r systematically across the designated domain (50.0 to 300.0 MJ·mm/(ha·h)), permitting full hydrodynamic or atmospheric stabilization at each step.
  5. Multi-Sensor Telemetry Recording & Signal Integration: Record the primary dependent variable (Measured Specific Hillslope Soil Loss Yield A) alongside auxiliary meteorological and channel parameters. Verify signal stability and repeatability.
  6. Post-Survey Verification & Data Archive Export: Check sensors against the baseline benchmark to verify absence of post-run drift, secure all field equipment, and export digital observations to the persistent geospatial database.
