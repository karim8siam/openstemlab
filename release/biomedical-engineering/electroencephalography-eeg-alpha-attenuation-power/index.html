FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Quantify the reduction in occipital 8–12 Hz alpha-band spectral power during eye-opening (Berger effect) and determine visual sensory attenuation ratio using Fast Fourier Transform (FFT). Standardized parameter settings: 50, 100, 150, 200, 250, 300 lux.

01

Electrode Montage and Impedance Calibration

Position Ag/AgCl electrodes at O1, O2, Cz, and linked earlobe references (A1, A2); verify all contact impedances are under 3.0 kΩ.

02

Dark-Adapted Closed-Eye Baseline

Record 60 seconds of resting EEG with eyes closed in darkness; verify presence of synchronized 10.0 Hz occipital alpha rhythm.

03

Illuminance Step Standardization

Instruct subject to open eyes and gaze at a neutral fixation cross; set photopic illuminance to initial test level of 50 lux.

04

Alpha Spectral Power Density Acquisition

Record 30 seconds of artifact-free EEG; compute average power spectral density in the 8-12 Hz band (P_alpha in μV²/Hz).

05

Illuminance Progression Sequence

Stepwise increment ambient light through 100, 150, 200, 250, and 300 lux, allowing 60 seconds for visual adaptation before logging P_alpha.

06

Attenuation Regression Analysis

Plot alpha band power P_alpha against illuminance E_v, performing least-squares linear regression to determine the photopic attenuation gradient k_alpha.
