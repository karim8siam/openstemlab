FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Calibrate the test-section velocity field of a closed-circuit subsonic wind tunnel and determine ambient air density by measuring dynamic pressure q across calibrated airspeed settings. Standardized parameter settings: 15.0, 20.0, 30.0, 40.0, 50.0, 60.0 m/s.

01

Initial Ambient Benchmarking

Record the ambient barometric pressure, room temperature, and relative humidity to calculate baseline thermodynamic air density.

02

Probe Alignment and Zero Balancing

Mount the Pitot-static probe along the geometric center of the test section parallel to the tunnel axis and zero the differential micromanometer.

03

Drive Fan Spin-Up

Power the axial fan drive, setting the initial motor inverter frequency to establish a steady baseline airspeed of 15 m/s.

04

Differential Pressure Acquisition

Allow airflow to stabilize for 60 seconds, then record differential dynamic pressure Delta P across 1000 synchronized digital samples.

05

Systematic Velocity Stepping

Step the tunnel airspeed through 20, 30, 40, 50, and 60 m/s, recording the steady-state dynamic pressure at each velocity plateau.

06

Transverse Boundary Survey

Perform a transverse probe sweep toward the test section sidewalls to verify core flow uniformity within 0.5% dynamic head variation.
