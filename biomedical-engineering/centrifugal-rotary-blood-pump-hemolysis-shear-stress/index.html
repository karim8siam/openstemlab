Biomedical assumptions and boundary condition limits

Zero-flow shut-off testing evaluates the maximum static head; operational LVADs deliver 4 to 6 L/min where dynamic frictional losses reduce delivered head. Evaluated on a magnetically levitated continuous-flow centrifugal blood pump (impeller diameter 36 mm) circulating a blood-analog fluid (38% glycerol-water, density ρ = 1055 kg/m³, viscosity 3.6 cP at 37 °C). Speeds N = 2.0, 2.5, 3.0, 3.5, 4.0, 4.5 k-RPM correspond to N² = 4.00, 6.25, 9.00, 12.25, 16.00, 20.25 k-RPM². Shut-off head scaling constant k_N = 7.5000 mmHg/k-RPM².

FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Determine the shut-off pressure head scaling constant k_N of a magnetically levitated continuous-flow rotary blood pump (LVAD) across operating speeds. Standardized parameter settings: 2.0, 2.5, 3.0, 3.5, 4.0, 4.5 k-RPM.

01

Pump Cartridge Installation and Priming

Mount the centrifugal blood pump in the mock loop, prime with 38% glycerol blood-analog at 37.0 °C, and purge all microscopic air bubbles.

02

Magnetic Levitation and Rotor Calibration

Energize active magnetic levitation coils and verify central impeller suspension without mechanical housing contact.

03

Pressure Transducer Zeroing at Rest

With the pump stopped (N = 0 RPM), open cross-bypass to confirm equal inlet and outlet pressure, zeroing differential transducer to 0.00 mmHg.

04

Zero-Flow Shut-Off Head Initialization

Close the downstream outflow valve to establish zero volumetric flow (Q = 0.00 L/min), set impeller speed to N = 2.0 k-RPM (2000 RPM), and log shut-off head H_0.

05

Impeller Speed Progression Sequence

Increment rotor speed through 2.5, 3.0, 3.5, 4.0, and 4.5 k-RPM, recording stabilized shut-off pressure head H_0 at each speed step.

06

Euler Affinity Quadratic Regression Analysis

Plot shut-off head H_0 against N², determine pump scaling constant k_N via linear regression through the origin, and calculate maximum blade tip velocity.
