Statistical assumptions and distribution limits

The F-distribution assumes normal parent populations; departures from normality can severely inflate Type I error rates. The empirical regression verifies the linear affine model y = 0.5000x - 1.0000 with slope m = 0.5000, intercept b = -1.0000, and R² = 1.000000.

FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Investigate Snedecor's F-distribution, synthesize variance ratios from independent Chi-Squared variates, analyze numerator and denominator degrees of freedom, and verify the theoretical mean relation. Standardized parameter settings: 4.0, 6.0, 8.0, 10.0, 12.0, 14.0 units.

01

Set Degrees of Freedom Parameters

Configure numerator degrees of freedom d_1 = 4 and baseline denominator degrees of freedom d_2 = 8 on the F-distribution control console.

02

Synthesize F-Distributed Random Variates

Draw 50,000 independent pairs of Chi-Squared variates U ~ χ²(4) and V ~ χ²(8). Compute the standardized ratio F = (U / 4) / (V / 8).

03

Verify Theoretical Mean and Asymmetry

Calculate sample mean F̄ and verify that it matches theoretical expectation E[F] = d_2 / (d_2 - 2) = 8 / 6 = 1.3333. Observe the right-skewed profile.

04

Confirm the Reciprocal Quantile Property

Evaluate the 95th percentile F_{0.05, 4, 8} and compare it with 1 / F_{0.95, 8, 4}, confirming exact reciprocal quantile symmetry.

05

Step Input Parameter Across Standardized Range x = 4 to 14

Increment the standardized input parameter across steps x ∈ {4.0, 6.0, 8.0, 10.0, 12.0, 14.0}, recording the linearized response metric y.

06

Perform Linear Regression Parameter Extraction

Fit a linear regression model y = mx + b to the coordinate pairs (x, y). Confirm that empirical slope m = 0.5000 and intercept b = -1.0000 validate the model.
