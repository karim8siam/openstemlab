Biomedical assumptions and boundary condition limits

The linear relation applies across the classic microvascular range (20 to 100 μm); below 7 μm, the curve inverts sharply upward. Evaluated on calibrated silica micro-capillary tubes (diameters D = 20, 35, 50, 65, 80, 95 μm; length L = 30 mm) perfused with human blood (feed hematocrit H_F = 45%, plasma viscosity η_p = 1.20 mPa·s) at high shear rates (γ̇_w > 500 s⁻¹) at 37.0 °C. Apparent relative viscosity slope k_d = 0.0125 μm⁻¹ with baseline η_0 = 1.2500 (η_rel = 1.500 at 20 μm, rising to 2.438 at 95 μm).

FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Quantify the Fåhræus-Lindqvist reduction in apparent relative blood viscosity η_rel in micro-capillary glass tubes as lumen diameter D narrows from 95 μm to 20 μm. Standardized parameter settings: 20.0, 35.0, 50.0, 65.0, 80.0, 95.0 μm.

01

Blood Specimen Standardization and Plasma Viscometry

Prepare fresh anti-coagulated human blood (Hct = 45.0%); centrifuge an aliquot to isolate plasma and measure plasma viscosity η_p on cone-plate viscometer (1.20 mPa·s).

02

Micro-Capillary Alignment and Pre-Wetting

Mount 20 μm silica micro-capillary into the perfusion manifold, pre-wet with plasma to prevent bubble entrapment, and seal pressure ports.

03

Driving Pressure Application

Apply pneumatic pressure drop ΔP to achieve high wall shear rate (γ̇_w > 500 s⁻¹), ensuring fully dispersed non-rouleaux flow.

04

Volumetric Flow Rate Measurement

Determine steady volumetric flow rate Q via timed micro-gravimetric collection on ultra-microbalance over 180 seconds.

05

Apparent Viscosity Computation

Calculate apparent blood viscosity from Poiseuille's formula: η_app = [π · D⁴ · ΔP] / [128 · L · Q], and compute relative viscosity η_rel = η_app / η_p.

06

Capillary Diameter Progression Sequence

Repeat standardized perfusion across tube diameters D = 35, 50, 65, 80, and 95 μm, recording apparent relative viscosity η_rel at each diameter.

07

Fåhræus-Lindqvist Linear Regression

Plot apparent relative viscosity η_rel against capillary diameter D and extract slope k_d and baseline intercept η_0 via linear regression.
