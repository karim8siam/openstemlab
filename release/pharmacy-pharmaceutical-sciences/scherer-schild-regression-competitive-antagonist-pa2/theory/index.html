Thermodynamic, Kinetic & Quality Control Mechanisms

Phase transitions in pharmaceuticals—whether the polymorphic transformation of crystal lattices, the dissolution of solid dosage forms into aqueous gastrointestinal fluids, the micellar incorporation of hydrophobic solutes, or the hydrolytic cleavage of labile drug bonds—are governed by rigorous thermodynamic principles. Reaction rates frequently follow Arrhenius thermal kinetics and pseudo-first-order degradation pathways, while dissolution follows the Noyes-Whitney diffusion model.
