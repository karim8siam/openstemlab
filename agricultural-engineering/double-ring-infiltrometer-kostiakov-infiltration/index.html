Agricultural assumptions and boundary condition limits

Double-ring measurements evaluate positive ponding head, whereas sprinkler and rainfall infiltration occur under zero-ponding negative pressure. Evaluated on undisturbed agricultural silt loam soil at 14% initial moisture. Cumulative infiltration data is linearized via natural logarithm transformation to extract α and c.

FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Measure the cumulative water infiltration and infiltration rate of an agricultural soil using a double-ring infiltrometer and determine Kostiakov model parameters. Standardized parameter settings: 10.0, 30.0, 50.0, 70.0, 90.0, 120.0 min.

01

Ring Siting & Driving

Select a representative, level site free of large stones and surface cracks. Place the double rings concentrically and drive them vertically into the soil to exactly 10 cm depth using the driving plate and hammer.

02

Surface Scour Protection

Place a thin burlap fabric or perforated plastic sheet inside the inner ring to prevent soil surface puddling, detachment, and slaking when pouring water.

03

Buffer Ring Filling

Fill the outer ring with water first to approximately 5 cm depth to establish an outer saturated buffer zone and prevent lateral capillary spread.

04

Inner Ring Mariotte Startup

Rapidly fill the inner ring to exactly 5 cm depth, remove the burlap, connect the Mariotte syphon or hook gauge, and start the master stopwatch at t = 0.

05

Elapsed Interval Logging

Record hook gauge water level drops at standardized elapsed intervals: 10, 30, 50, 70, 90, and 120 minutes, replenishing the water level to maintain steady head.

06

Kostiakov Logarithmic Regression

Transform cumulative infiltration I (mm) and elapsed time t (min) into natural logarithms, plot ln I versus ln t, and perform linear regression to evaluate α and c.
