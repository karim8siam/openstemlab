Biomedical assumptions and boundary condition limits

The linear aggregate modulus model applies to infinitesimal to moderate strains (ε_c ≤ 0.30); higher compressions exhibit strain-dependent stiffening. Evaluated on cylindrical articular cartilage test plugs (diameter 4.0 mm, initial thickness h_0 = 1.50 mm) excised from bovine femoral condyles. Tested in 0.15 M NaCl saline bath at 22 °C under stepwise creep loads until equilibrium strain ε_c is achieved. Linear aggregate slope H_A = 0.6500 MPa across strains 0.05 to 0.30.

FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Determine the equilibrium aggregate compressive modulus H_A of articular cartilage under confined compression creep testing using Mow's biphasic biomechanical theory. Standardized parameter settings: 0.05, 0.1, 0.15, 0.2, 0.25, 0.3 ratio.

01

Cartilage Plug Harvest and Thickness Metrology

Harvest 4.0 mm diameter full-thickness cartilage plugs from bovine femoral condyles and measure initial uncompressed thickness h_0 using a current-sensing micrometer.

02

Confined Well Placement and Osmotic Equilibration

Place the plug snugly into the precision stainless steel confining well, submerge in 0.15 M physiological saline, and allow 30 minutes for osmotic equilibration.

03

Tare Load and Zero Baseline Contact

Lower the porous titanium platen onto the cartilage surface until a small tare load (0.01 N) is registered, establishing the zero reference displacement position.

04

Stepwise Creep Load Application

Apply a static axial compressive stress step and track continuous vertical displacement as fluid exudes through the porous platen.

05

Equilibrium Creep Criterion Verification

Monitor displacement rate until equilibrium is reached (defined as displacement change < 0.2 μm over 15 minutes, typically requiring 45 to 60 minutes per step).

06

Multi-Step Stress-Strain Regression

Repeat creep equilibration across successive strain levels ε_c = 0.05, 0.10, 0.15, 0.20, 0.25, and 0.30, and determine the equilibrium aggregate modulus H_A.
