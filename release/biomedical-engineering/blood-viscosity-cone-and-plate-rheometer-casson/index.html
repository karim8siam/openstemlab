Biomedical assumptions and boundary condition limits

The Casson model is an empirical continuum approximation; it applies across 10 to 300 s⁻¹ but does not capture zero-shear viscoelasticity. Evaluated on anti-coagulated human whole blood (Hct = 45%, EDTA treated) at 37.0 °C in a titanium cone-and-plate rheometer (cone angle θ = 1.0°, diameter 60 mm). Standard shear rates γ̇ = 16, 36, 64, 100, 144, 196 s⁻¹ correspond to √γ̇ = 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14 s⁻⁰·⁵. Casson slope √μ_c = 0.0620 Pa⁰·⁵·s⁰·⁵ (asymptotic viscosity μ_c = 0.00384 Pa·s = 3.84 cP) with yield root √τ_y = 0.1800 Pa⁰·⁵ (yield stress τ_y = 0.0324 Pa).

Biomedical apparatus & physiological sensors.

Precision Controlled-Stress Cone-and-Plate Rheometer

Titanium Rheometer Cone (diameter 60 mm, cone angle 1.0°)

Peltier Thermoelectric Temperature Control Plate (37.0 ± 0.05 °C)

Anti-Evaporation Solvent Trap with Humidified Ring

High-Precision Micro-Pipettor (500 μL)

FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Determine the Casson asymptotic plastic viscosity μ_c and yield stress τ_y of whole human blood across low-to-medium shear rates using cone-and-plate rheometry. Standardized parameter settings: 16.0, 36.0, 64.0, 100.0, 144.0, 196.0 s⁻¹.

01

Blood Specimen Resuspension and Hematocrit Verification

Mix EDTA-anticoagulated whole blood on roller mixer for 10 minutes; verify standard hematocrit (Hct = 45 ± 1%) via microhematocrit centrifuge.

02

Peltier Plate Thermal Stabilization

Set rheometer Peltier bottom plate to exactly 37.0 °C and allow 15 minutes for thermal equilibrium.

03

Zero Gap Calibration and Specimen Loading

Zero the normal force and calibrate the mechanical contact gap; pipette exactly 500 μL of blood onto the plate center and lower cone to truncation height (50 μm).

04

Solvent Trap Placement and High-Shear Preshear

Engage the humidified solvent trap and apply a 30-second preshear at 300 s⁻¹ to completely disperse pre-existing rouleaux aggregates.

05

Stepwise Shear Rate Sweep

Step through shear rates γ̇ = 16, 36, 64, 100, 144, and 196 s⁻¹, holding each step for 15 seconds to achieve steady torque before recording shear stress τ.

06

Casson Linearized Coordinate Regression

Compute √γ̇ and √τ, plot √τ versus √γ̇, and extract Casson asymptotic viscosity root √μ_c from the slope and yield stress root √τ_y from the y-intercept.
