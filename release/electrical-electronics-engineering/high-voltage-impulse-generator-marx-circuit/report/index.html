Type B instrumental calibration uncertainty from the 6.5-digit multimeter was u_B = 0.0005. The combined expanded uncertainty at a 95% confidence interval (coverage factor k = 2) is:

\[ U = k \cdot \sqrt{u_A^2 + u_B^2} = 2 \cdot \sqrt{(0.0002)^2 + (0.0005)^2} = \pm 0.0011\text{ kV/stage} \]
