Biomedical assumptions and boundary condition limits

The simple Q = I · t relationship assumes a perfect rectangular current pulse; real inductive leads introduce finite rise and fall times. Evaluated on a clinical constant-current FES neuroprosthesis (Bioness NESS L300 architecture) delivering charge-balanced symmetric biphasic rectangular pulses (pulse duration t_pw = 250 μs = 0.2500 ms, frequency 35 Hz) into a 1.0 kΩ parallel RC tissue load in 37.0 °C saline. Cathodic injected charge slope t_pw = 0.2500 μC/mA across pulse currents I_stim from 5.0 to 30.0 mA.

FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Verify net zero electrochemical charge balance and evaluate cathodic phase charge injection Q_p as a function of stimulus pulse current I_stim in a Functional Electrical Stimulation (FES) neuroprosthesis. Standardized parameter settings: 5.0, 10.0, 15.0, 20.0, 25.0, 30.0 mA.

01

Tissue Phantom Load Connection and Calibration

Connect the FES stimulation output across the 1.0 kΩ precision non-inductive load in parallel with 10 nF double-layer capacitor; verify zero DC bias voltage.

02

Biphasic Waveform Symmetry Configuration

Program stimulator to deliver charge-balanced symmetric biphasic pulses: pulse width t_pw = 250 μs cathodic, 50 μs interphase delay, followed by 250 μs anodic reversal at 35 Hz.

03

Initial Current Step Initialization

Set stimulation current amplitude to I_stim = 5.0 mA and initiate continuous stimulation train.

04

Cathodic Phase Charge Integration

Record the voltage drop across the precision 10.0 Ω sensing resistor, integrate current over the 250 μs cathodic duration, and calculate injected charge Q_p = I_stim · t_pw in micro-Coulombs.

05

Net DC Charge Balance Verification

Integrate the complete cycle (cathodic plus anodic phases) over 100 pulses to confirm net DC offset charge is strictly < 0.01 μC (safe charge balance).

06

Current Progression Sequence

Increment stimulation current through 10.0, 15.0, 20.0, 25.0, and 30.0 mA, recording cathodic injected charge Q_p at each current step.

07

Charge Injection Linear Regression Analysis

Plot injected charge Q_p versus stimulation current I_stim and verify that the regression slope matches the programmed pulse duration t_pw = 0.2500 μC/mA.
