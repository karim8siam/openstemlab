1. Title and aim

Titanium Alloy Orthopaedic Bone Plate Four-Point Bending Compliance. Evaluate the structural bending compliance C_b and flexural rigidity of a medical Ti-6Al-4V ELI bone fracture fixation plate under standardized ASTM F382 four-point bending.

4. Theory and graph choice

Internal bone fracture fixation plates are medical devices surgically affixed across fractured cortical bone segments to maintain anatomical reduction, stabilize fragments, and facilitate primary or secondary osseous healing. To evaluate the mechanical performance, bending stiffness, and fatigue strength of orthopaedic fracture plates, the American Society for Testing and Materials established the standard specification ASTM F382 ('Standard Specification and Test Method for Metallic Bone Plates'). Four-point bending is universally specified over three-point bending because it creates a region of pure bending between the two inner loading rollers—subjecting the critical plate segment (typically containing screw holes that create stress concentrations) to a constant bending moment M = (W/2) · a without transverse shear forces. Under linear elastic Euler-Bernoulli beam theory, the mid-span deflection δ of a plate supported over outer span L with inner span a is directly proportional to total applied load W: δ = [W·a·(3L² - 4a²) / (48·E·I)] = C_b · W, where C_b is the structural bending compliance (mm/N) and E·I is the flexural rigidity (N·m²). Titanium alloy Ti-6Al-4V ELI (Extra Low Interstitial) is the clinical gold standard due to its excellent biocompatibility, corrosion resistance, and lower modulus (110 GPa vs. 200 GPa for 316L stainless steel), which mitigates stress shielding. In this laboratory investigation, an instrumented ASTM F382 four-point bending fixture evaluates the structural compliance C_b of a medical titanium dynamic compression plate (DCP).

δ = C_b · W = 0.0042 · W

The governing physiological relationship was linearized into the standard regression coordinate form y = mx + b. Here, the independent coordinate x represents Applied bending load W (N), and the dependent coordinate y represents Mid-span deflection δ (mm). The gradient m directly determines Bending Compliance C_b, while the intercept b accounts for baseline null offsets, pre-stress tares, or reference thresholds. Linear transformation allows robust parameter estimation via ordinary least squares without non-linear solver instability or convergence traps. Plotting transformed observation pairs displays sensor sensitivities, elastic moduli, hemodynamic resistances, or acoustic attenuation coefficients directly as linear slopes. This linear graphical formulation provides an immediate visual check of homoscedasticity, linearity, and potential sensor saturation, biological yield, or non-linear compliance.

5. Method

  1. Set outer roller span to exactly L = 100.0 mm and inner roller span to a = 25.0 mm using precision gauge blocks; verify parallelism of all four cylindrical rollers.
  2. Position the titanium plate symmetrically across the support rollers so that a central screw hole aligns with the middle of the pure bending span.
  3. Position the precision LVDT directly beneath the plate center span and adjust the spring-loaded probe tip to make positive contact.
  4. Apply a seating tare load of 10 N to eliminate mechanical play and zero both load and deflection readings.
  5. Advance crosshead at 1.0 mm/min through load increments of 100, 200, 300, 400, 500, and 600 N, recording stabilized mid-span deflection δ at each step.
  6. Plot mid-span deflection δ versus applied load W and compute the structural bending compliance C_b and flexural rigidity E·I via linear regression.

11. Precautions and limitations

  • Verify that the LVDT probe tip contacts the plate bottom surface directly to avoid including roller Hertzian indentation in deflection measurements.
  • Ensure inner and outer spans are precisely centered and parallel to prevent asymmetrical torsional twisting.
  • Do not exceed the elastic limit of the titanium plate (650 N) to avoid permanent plastic deformation during calibration.
  • Verify that roller surfaces are polished and lubricated with light oil to minimize frictional axial binding.

The linear compliance model applies strictly within the elastic limit; plastic yielding requires non-linear elastoplastic analysis.
