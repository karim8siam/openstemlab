FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Determine the anticoagulant biological potency of low-molecular-weight heparin (LMWH) formulations using a chromogenic synthetic peptide Factor Xa substrate assay. Standardized parameter settings: 0.1, 0.28, 0.46, 0.64, 0.82, 1.0 IU/mL.

  1. Step 1

    Instrument Initialization & Baseline Tare

    Power on the analytical instrumentation for Heparin Anti-Factor Xa Assay, allow a 30-minute electronic warm-up period, and verify transducer zero-tare using certified standards.

  2. Step 2

    Standard Solution & Matrix Specimen Preparation

    Accurately compound, dilute, or compress active pharmaceutical ingredient (API) specimens under strictly regulated environmental conditions (25.0 ± 0.5 °C).

  3. Step 3

    Systematic Independent Variable Setting

    Adjust the independent testing parameter (Heparin Potency) across the designated analytical range (0.1 to 1.0 IU/mL) in uniform calibrated steps.

  4. Step 4

    Analytical Signal Acquisition & Telemetry Logging

    Execute the measurement cycle and record steady-state telemetry for Residual Factor Xa Cleavage Absorbance (405 nm) with high-precision digital integration.

  5. Step 5

    Replicate Verification & Equilibrium Inspection

    Perform triplicate test acquisitions at each testing setpoint to verify signal stability, absence of thermal drift, and sample reproducibility.

  6. Step 6

    Regression Modeling & Pharmacopoeial Parameter Derivation

    Plot Heparin Potency (IU/mL) against Factor Xa Absorbance (AU), execute ordinary least-squares (OLS) linear regression, and calculate Factor Xa Antithrombotic Inhibition Rate (m) against compendial benchmarks.
