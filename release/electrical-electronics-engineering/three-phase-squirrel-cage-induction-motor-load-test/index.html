FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Determine torque, rotational speed, rotor slip (s), power factor, and efficiency of a three-phase squirrel-cage induction motor under progressive mechanical brake loading. Standardized parameter settings: 2.0, 3.5, 5.0, 6.5, 8.0, 9.5 N·m.

  1. Visual Inspection & Pre-Flight Zero Tare: Inspect all circuit connections, breadboard jumpers, and terminal blocks. Verify that instrument ground planes are tied to earth and execute zero tare on all digital meters.
  2. Excitation Source & Baseline Calibration: Energize the regulated supply, set baseline operating thresholds, and calibrate the brake torque τ using the precision standard reference meter.
  3. Systematic Parameter Variation & Settling: Increment the brake torque τ systematically across the designated experimental range (2.0 to 9.5 N·m), permitting thermal settling at each setpoint.
  4. Multi-Meter & Oscilloscope Waveform Acquisition: Record the primary output metric (Rotor Slip Percentage s) alongside auxiliary telemetry. Verify waveform symmetry, DC offset, and harmonic integrity on the oscilloscope.
  5. Cross-Validation & Redundant Trial Verification: Execute repeated forward and reverse sweeps to detect hysteresis, thermal drift, or non-linear saturation effects across the device under test (DUT).
  6. Shutdown, De-energization & Data Archival: Reduce source excitation smoothly to zero, de-energize all primary supplies, discharge residual capacitive elements, and export raw telemetry to the data buffer.
