FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Determine the critical micelle concentration (CMC) and degree of counterion binding of sodium lauryl sulfate (SLS) using precision electrolytic conductivity titrimetry. Standardized parameter settings: 1.0, 2.2, 3.4, 4.6, 5.8, 7.0 mM.

  1. Step 1

    Instrument Initialization & Baseline Tare

    Power on the analytical instrumentation for Surfactant CMC & Conductometry, allow a 30-minute electronic warm-up period, and verify transducer zero-tare using certified standards.

  2. Step 2

    Standard Solution & Matrix Specimen Preparation

    Accurately compound, dilute, or compress active pharmaceutical ingredient (API) specimens under strictly regulated environmental conditions (25.0 ± 0.5 °C).

  3. Step 3

    Systematic Independent Variable Setting

    Adjust the independent testing parameter (SLS Concentration) across the designated analytical range (1.0 to 7.0 mM) in uniform calibrated steps.

  4. Step 4

    Analytical Signal Acquisition & Telemetry Logging

    Execute the measurement cycle and record steady-state telemetry for Specific Electrolytic Conductivity with high-precision digital integration.

  5. Step 5

    Replicate Verification & Equilibrium Inspection

    Perform triplicate test acquisitions at each testing setpoint to verify signal stability, absence of thermal drift, and sample reproducibility.

  6. Step 6

    Regression Modeling & Pharmacopoeial Parameter Derivation

    Plot SLS Concentration (mM) against Conductivity (μS/cm), execute ordinary least-squares (OLS) linear regression, and calculate Premicellar Monomer Molar Conductivity (m) against compendial benchmarks.
