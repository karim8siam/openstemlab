FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Evaluate the surface rebound hardness and estimate the unconfined uniaxial compressive strength (UCS) of outcrop rock cores using an instrumented Schmidt hammer. Standardized parameter settings: 20.0, 28.0, 36.0, 44.0, 52.0, 60.0 R.

  1. Step 1

    Instrument Initialization & Environmental Baseline Tare

    Power on the analytical instrumentation for Schmidt Hammer Rock Hardness, calibrate baseline telemetry against certified reference standards, and verify optical/mechanical alignment.

  2. Step 2

    Specimen Preparation & Geological Conditioning

    Prepare, cut, calibrate, or orient the testing specimens under strictly regulated environmental laboratory conditions conforming to USGS and IAU guidelines.

  3. Step 3

    Independent Setpoint Parameter Adjustment

    Adjust the independent testing parameter (Rebound Number) across the designated analytical range (20.0 to 60.0 R) in uniform calibrated steps.

  4. Step 4

    Scientific Telemetry Acquisition & Logging

    Execute the experimental run and record steady-state telemetry for Unconfined Compressive Strength (UCS) with high-precision digital integration.

  5. Step 5

    Replicate Inspection & Environmental Equilibrium Check

    Perform duplicate test acquisitions at each testing setpoint to verify signal stability, absence of thermal drift, and sample reproducibility.

  6. Step 6

    Regression Calculation & Geophysical Parameter Derivation

    Plot Rebound Number (R) against Compressive Strength (MPa), calculate ordinary least-squares (OLS) linear regression parameters, and derive Geomechanical Hardness-to-Strength Scaling (k_UCS) against certified reference benchmarks.
