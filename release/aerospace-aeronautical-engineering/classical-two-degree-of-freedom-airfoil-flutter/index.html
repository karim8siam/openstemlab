Aeronautical assumptions and boundary condition limits

2-DOF pitch-plunge model assumes rigid 2D airfoil sections; real aircraft wings experience 3D spanwise bending and control surface flutter. Linear regression of the frequency separation metric against airspeed squared yields a coalescence slope k_f = 0.0450 rad^2/m^2 with R^2 > 0.999999.

Aeronautical apparatus & wind tunnel sensors.

2-DOF Aeroelastic Airfoil Rig

Rigid NACA 0012 section (c = 200 mm) mounted on external linear plunge springs and torsional flexure pivots.

Non-Contact Laser Displacement Sensors

Twin triangulation lasers measuring plunge displacement h(t) and pitch rotation alpha(t) at 5 kHz.

Subsonic Aeroelastic Wind Tunnel

Provides smooth airspeed control from V_inf = 15 to 55 m/s with automatic emergency brake doors.

Electrodynamic Modal Shaker Actuator

Provides initial impulse excitation to measure modal frequencies and damping ratios across airspeeds.

Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) Modal Analyzer

Computes real-time cross-spectral density and tracks pitch/plunge peak frequencies.

Automated Flutter Suppression Damper

Electromagnetic safety brake that clamps model motion instantly upon flutter onset.

FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Measure coupled plunge displacement h(t) and pitch angle alpha(t) of an elastically mounted airfoil to detect frequency coalescence and the flutter boundary V_flutter. Standardized parameter settings: 500.0, 800.0, 1200.0, 1600.0, 2000.0, 2400.0 m^2/s^2.

01

Zero-Wind Modal Benchmarking

With wind tunnel off (V_inf = 0), pluck the model to record baseline plunge frequency omega_h0 = 18.85 rad/s (3.0 Hz) and pitch frequency omega_alpha0 = 31.42 rad/s (5.0 Hz).

02

Low-Speed Flow Initialization

Start wind tunnel at V_inf = 22.36 m/s (V_inf^2 = 500 m^2/s^2), apply small impulse, and acquire h(t) and alpha(t) time series.

03

Systematic Airspeed Stepping

Step airspeed through V_inf^2 = 800, 1200, 1600, 2000, and 2400 m^2/s^2 (V_inf up to 49.0 m/s).

04

Modal Frequency Extraction

Compute FFT power spectrum at each speed, tracking the convergence of the two modal frequency peaks.

05

Frequency Separation Calculation

Calculate squared frequency separation Delta(omega^2) = |omega_alpha^2 - omega_h^2| at each velocity setting.

06

Coalescence Linear Regression

Plot Delta(omega^2) versus V_inf^2, perform linear regression to determine coalescence slope k_f = 0.0450, and extrapolate the flutter speed V_flutter.
