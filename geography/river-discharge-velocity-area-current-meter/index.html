FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Calculate river cross-sectional stream discharge (Q) and stage-discharge rating relationships using the velocity-area method and a calibrated propeller current meter. Standardized parameter settings: 0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 2.5, 3.0 m.

  1. Sensor Calibration & Zero Benchmark Verification: Inspect all hydrographic, meteorological, or optical sensors. Verify calibration against certified reference instruments and establish digital zero tare.
  2. Field Site Setup & Geodetic Survey Benchmark Alignment: Deploy the measurement rig across the designated landscape transect or flume channel. Align survey axes with local geodetic benchmarks and leveling datum.
  3. Initial Gauge Alignment & Parameter Setpoint Selection: Position the sensor array at the target sampling station. Select the initial stream stage elevation setpoint and confirm steady-state baseline readings.
  4. Systematic Parameter Variation & Acquisition: Increment the stream stage elevation systematically across the designated domain (0.5 to 3.0 m), permitting full hydrodynamic or atmospheric stabilization at each step.
  5. Multi-Sensor Telemetry Recording & Signal Integration: Record the primary dependent variable (Total River Discharge Q) alongside auxiliary meteorological and channel parameters. Verify signal stability and repeatability.
  6. Post-Survey Verification & Data Archive Export: Check sensors against the baseline benchmark to verify absence of post-run drift, secure all field equipment, and export digital observations to the persistent geospatial database.
