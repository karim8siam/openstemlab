FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Measure the steady-state cathodic reduction limiting diffusion current of a membrane-covered Clark polarographic oxygen electrode across calibrated dissolved oxygen partial pressures. Standardized parameter settings: 2.0, 5.0, 8.0, 11.0, 14.0, 17.0 kPa.

01

Membrane Inspection and Polarizing Setup

Inspect the 12 μm Teflon membrane for micro-tears, fill electrolyte well with fresh 0.1 M KCl buffer, and apply -0.700 V cathodic polarizing potential.

02

Zero-Oxygen Baseline Calibration

Perfuse the flowcell with zero-oxygen calibration fluid (deoxygenated with sodium sulfite) and record residual dark background current (I_0 < 0.1 nA).

03

Initial Oxygen Step Calibration

Perfuse saline tonometered to P_O2 = 2.0 kPa (15.0 mmHg) at 37.0 °C, establishing a constant perfusion velocity to prevent boundary layer depletion.

04

Diffusion Plateau Current Acquisition

Allow 45 seconds for diffusion boundary layer stabilization; record steady-state cathodic current I_d in nanoamperes.

05

Oxygen Partial Pressure Progression

Increment tonometered oxygen partial pressure through 5.0, 8.0, 11.0, 14.0, and 17.0 kPa (up to physiological normoxia ~100-128 mmHg), logging I_d at each step.

06

Diffusion Sensitivity Regression Analysis

Plot reduction current I_d against oxygen partial pressure P_O2, performing linear regression through zero to calculate diffusion sensitivity k_O2.
