FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Determine the discharge-pressure relationship, emitter discharge exponent x, and manufacturing coefficient of variation (CV) for agricultural drip irrigation emitters. Standardized parameter settings: 3.912, 4.3175, 4.6052, 4.9416, 5.247, 5.5215 ln(kPa).

01

Manifold Flushing & Filter Inspection

Flush the drip manifold with clean water through the end flush valve for 2.0 minutes, verifying that the 120-mesh disc filter is clean.

02

Pressure Stabilization at 50 kPa

Set the electronic regulator to P = 50 kPa (ln P = 3.9120), allowing all 25 emitters to discharge into waste troughs until steady flow is established.

03

Timed Catch Collection

Position the 25 graduated catch cups under the emitters simultaneously for exactly 10.0 minutes, then remove them synchronously.

04

Gravimetric Emitter Auditing

Weigh each individual catch cup to calculate the discharge q_i (L/h) of each emitter, computing mean discharge q_bar and standard deviation s_q to find CV.

05

Pressure Stepping

Increment operating pressure across five additional stages: 75, 100, 140, 190, and 250 kPa (ln P up to 5.5215), repeating catch audits at each pressure.

06

Flow Exponent Logarithmic Regression

Plot ln(q_bar) versus ln(P), perform linear regression to determine the emitter flow exponent x and coefficient k, and compute Emission Uniformity EU.
