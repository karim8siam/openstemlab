FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Transform continuous-time Butterworth low-pass prototypes into stable discrete IIR difference equations using the bilinear z-transform (BZT), validating frequency pre-warping. Standardized parameter settings: 1000.0, 2200.0, 3600.0, 5000.0, 6500.0, 8000.0 rad/s.

  1. Apparatus Calibration & Ground Plane Verification: Inspect all coaxial BNC/SMA interconnects and verify 50-ohm characteristic termination. Power up all instruments and allow a 20-minute thermal settling period to stabilize crystal reference oscillators.
  2. Carrier & Modulating Signal Synthesis: Configure the synthesized RF signal generator and baseband source to the specified baseline parameters. Calibrate the cutoff frequency using the precision digital multimeter and oscilloscope.
  3. Systematic Telemetry Acquisition & Setpoint Stepping: Increment the cutoff frequency systematically across the experimental range (1000.0 to 8000.0 rad/s), logging the resulting pre-warped digital frequency metric tan(ω_d / 2) after signal settling.
  4. Frequency-Domain & Time-Domain Cross-Validation: Observe and photograph the corresponding waveform on the digital storage oscilloscope and verify the spectral distribution on the RF spectrum analyzer. Verify that harmonic distortion remains below -45 dBc.
  5. Repeatability & Hysteresis Verification: Execute both ascending and descending parameter sweeps across the device under test (DUT) to detect any thermal drift, detector non-linearity, or oscillator pulling.
  6. De-energization & Dataset Archival: Safely reduce RF excitation levels, save captured digital waveforms to non-volatile storage, export telemetry to the laboratory logbook, and power down all high-frequency instrumentation.
