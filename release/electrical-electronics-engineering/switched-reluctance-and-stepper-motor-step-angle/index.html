FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Measure static holding torque, detent torque, dynamic pull-in/pull-out torque curves, and step-angle positional accuracy in a 1.8° two-phase hybrid stepper motor under microstepping control. Standardized parameter settings: 50.0, 100.0, 150.0, 200.0, 250.0, 300.0 Hz.

  1. Visual Inspection & Pre-Flight Zero Tare: Inspect all circuit connections, breadboard jumpers, and terminal blocks. Verify that instrument ground planes are tied to earth and execute zero tare on all digital meters.
  2. Excitation Source & Baseline Calibration: Energize the regulated supply, set baseline operating thresholds, and calibrate the step frequency using the precision standard reference meter.
  3. Systematic Parameter Variation & Settling: Increment the step frequency systematically across the designated experimental range (50.0 to 300.0 Hz), permitting thermal settling at each setpoint.
  4. Multi-Meter & Oscilloscope Waveform Acquisition: Record the primary output metric (Rotor Angular Velocity ω) alongside auxiliary telemetry. Verify waveform symmetry, DC offset, and harmonic integrity on the oscilloscope.
  5. Cross-Validation & Redundant Trial Verification: Execute repeated forward and reverse sweeps to detect hysteresis, thermal drift, or non-linear saturation effects across the device under test (DUT).
  6. Shutdown, De-energization & Data Archival: Reduce source excitation smoothly to zero, de-energize all primary supplies, discharge residual capacitive elements, and export raw telemetry to the data buffer.
