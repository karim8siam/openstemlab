1. Title and aim

Axial-Flow Threshing Cylinder Peripheral Speed & Grain Separation Efficiency. Determine the effect of threshing drum peripheral speed on grain separation efficiency and unthreshed grain loss in an axial-flow combine threshing mechanism.

4. Theory and graph choice

Grain threshing in modern combine harvesters detaches grain kernels from their seed heads or pods through a combination of impact, rubbing friction, and centrifugal separation. In axial-flow threshing cylinders, crop material spirals longitudinally along the rotor axis inside a perforated concave grate, making multiple passes over rasp-bars or spiked teeth. The drum peripheral speed v_c = π·D·N / 60 is the dominant operational variable governing threshing effectiveness. Increasing peripheral velocity imparts higher kinetic impact energy to shatter glumes and pods, accelerating grain detachment and improving the separation percentage through the concave grates. However, excessive cylinder speed shatters brittle straw (overloading the cleaning shoe sieves) and induces internal grain pericarp cracking and embryo damage, reducing germination and seed market grade. Conversely, insufficient speed leaves unthreshed heads that are lost in the straw discharge. In this laboratory investigation, an instrumented pilot threshing drum processes standard grain batches across six controlled peripheral speeds to calibrate the separation sensitivity gradient.

E_t = s_t · v_c + E_0 = 1.1500 · v_c + 60.0000

The governing physical relationship was linearized into the standard regression coordinate form y = mx + b. Here, the independent axis x represents Cylinder velocity v_c (m/s), and the dependent axis y represents Threshing separation metric E_t (%). The gradient m directly determines Threshing Sensitivity Gradient s_t, while the intercept b accounts for zero-load offsets, baseline tares, or reference thresholds. Linear transformation allows robust parameter estimation via ordinary least squares without non-linear solver instability or convergence failure. Plotting transformed observation pairs displays soil resistance gradients, flow exponents, drying kinetics, or power conversion efficiencies directly as linear slopes. This linear graphical formulation provides an immediate visual check of homoscedasticity, linearity, and potential soil shear failure, flow cavitation, or saturation limits.

5. Method

  1. Set the front concave inlet clearance to 12.0 mm and the rear outlet clearance to 6.0 mm using feeler thickness gauges, locking the adjustment lever.
  2. Prepare uniform 10.0 kg batches of conditioned wheat sheaves with known grain-to-straw ratio (approximately 1:1.2).
  3. Set rotor peripheral velocity to v_c = 20.0 m/s (rotor speed 510 RPM on 75 cm diameter cylinder), allowing the motor to stabilize at idle.
  4. Feed the 10.0 kg wheat batch uniformly onto the feed conveyor over exactly 15 seconds, collecting all concave-separated material and straw discharge.
  5. Clean and weigh all grain separated through the concave (m_sep), hand-thresh all remaining unthreshed heads in the straw to find unthreshed loss (m_loss), and compute separation percentage E_t = m_sep / (m_sep + m_loss) × 100%.
  6. Repeat testing across velocities of 22.4, 24.8, 27.2, 29.6, and 32.0 m/s, plotting E_t versus v_c and evaluating kernel mechanical damage.

11. Precautions and limitations

  • Feed crop uniformly along the conveyor; slug feeding causes cylinder speed drop and inaccurate loss measurements.
  • Ensure protective wire safety cage is locked around the rotor before engaging drive motors.
  • Allow the cylinder to run for 30 seconds after feeding to clear all residual grain before collecting samples.
  • Inspect rasp-bars for stone damage, bent teeth, or uneven wear that would create localized clearance variations.

Tested with wheat; crops with delicate seed coats (e.g., soybeans, edible dry beans) require much lower peripheral speeds (10 to 18 m/s). Evaluated using conditioned wheat sheaves (grain moisture 13.5% d.b.) at constant concave clearance of 12 mm inlet / 6 mm outlet.
