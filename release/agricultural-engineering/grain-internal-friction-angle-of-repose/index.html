FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Measure the static and dynamic angle of repose of agricultural grains using a circular heaping platform and determine grain-on-metal sliding friction coefficients on a tilting plane. Standardized parameter settings: 10.0, 15.0, 20.0, 25.0, 30.0, 35.0 cm.

01

Platform Leveling & Funnel Centering

Mount the 10.0 cm radius circular platform on the rigid test base, verifying horizontal level with a bubble spirit level. Center the discharge funnel 10 cm above the platform.

02

Grain Heap Formation

Fill the funnel with clean wheat, release the bottom gate, and allow grain to pour continuously until excess grain rolls off all sides and a stable symmetrical cone is formed.

03

Peak Height Measurement

Carefully lower the vernier height gauge point to touch the exact central apex of the conical grain pile, recording cone height H (cm).

04

Radius Stepping

Repeat the conical formation on circular platforms of 15, 20, 25, 30, and 35 cm radius, measuring cone height H for each base radius.

05

Tilting Plate Friction Test

Place a bottomless wooden box filled with grain onto the horizontal galvanized steel plate, slowly elevating the plate with the lead screw until the box begins sliding, logging the friction angle α.

06

Repose Tangent Linear Regression

Plot cone height H against radius R, perform linear regression through the origin, and determine the slope tan θ to calculate the angle of repose θ = arctan(slope).
