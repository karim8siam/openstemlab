FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Determine the radial water distribution profile, Christiansen Uniformity Coefficient (CU), and overlapping pattern efficiency of an agricultural impact sprinkler nozzle across varying operating pressures. Standardized parameter settings: 150.0, 200.0, 250.0, 300.0, 350.0, 400.0 kPa.

01

Catch-Can Grid Siting & Leveling

Lay out the catch cans in a square 1.5 m × 1.5 m matrix across the indoor test hall, ensuring all can rims sit level and at uniform elevation.

02

Riser Mounting & Pressure Set

Mount the impact sprinkler on the central 1.2 m vertical riser, setting the pressure regulator to initial low pressure P = 150.0 kPa.

03

Timed Test Precipitation Run

Start the sprinkler pump and allow the sprinkler to rotate continuously for exactly 30.0 minutes, distributing water across the catch-can grid.

04

Can Volumetric Auditing

Measure and record the depth of water caught in each can X_i (mm), computing the mean depth X_bar and the mean absolute deviation Σ|X_i - X_bar| / n.

05

Christiansen CU Computation

Simulate a 12 m × 12 m overlapping sprinkler layout and calculate the Christiansen Uniformity Coefficient CU = 100·[1 - Σ|X_i - X_bar| / (n·X_bar)].

06

Pressure Stepping

Repeat the procedure at 200, 250, 300, 350, and 400 kPa, plotting CU versus pressure to extract the sensitivity slope s_p.
