FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Determine the shock wave angle beta over supersonic compression wedges of varying deflection angles theta and validate the theta-beta-M oblique shock relation. Standardized parameter settings: 5.0, 7.5, 10.0, 12.5, 15.0, 17.5 deg.

01

Wedge Installation and Alignment

Install the theta = 5.0 degree wedge model, aligning the bottom flat surface parallel to the tunnel axis using optical laser leveling.

02

Schlieren Optical Alignment

Adjust the Schlieren light source, parabolic mirrors, and knife-edge to achieve uniform grey background illumination across test section.

03

Supersonic Tunnel Firing

Open the supersonic tunnel main valve, stabilizing upstream Mach number at M_1 = 2.50 with stagnation pressure P_01 = 400 kPa.

04

Schlieren Image Capture

Trigger high-resolution digital image capture of the attached oblique shock wave originating from the wedge apex.

05

Deflection Angle Stepping

Repeat the test across modular wedges of theta = 7.5, 10.0, 12.5, 15.0, and 17.5 degrees, recording the optical shock angle beta.

06

Shock Polar Regression

Plot (beta - beta_0) versus theta, where beta_0 = arcsin(1/M_1) = 23.58 deg, perform linear regression to determine the deflection gradient, and compare with theta-beta-M theory.
