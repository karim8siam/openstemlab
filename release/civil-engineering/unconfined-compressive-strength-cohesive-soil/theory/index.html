Civil engineering mechanics & structural foundations of Unconfined Compression Test.

The Unconfined Compressive Strength (UCS) test provides the most rapid, economical, and widely used laboratory method for determining the undrained shear strength (c_u) of cohesive fine-grained soils. Standardized under ASTM D2166, the test is a special limiting case of the unconsolidated-undrained (UU) triaxial compression test where confining cell pressure is zero (σ_3 = 0). A cylindrical undisturbed cohesive soil specimen (typically 38 mm or 50 mm diameter, with standard length-to-diameter aspect ratio L/D = 2.0 to 2.5) is mounted between flat end platens and loaded axially in compression without lateral lateral restraint. Loading is applied at a rapid strain rate of 0.5% to 2.0% per minute so that testing is completed within 10 to 15 minutes, ensuring that zero water can drain from low-permeability clay pores (undrained condition). As axial compressive force P increases, axial strain ε = ΔL / L_0 increases, and cross-sectional area expands from initial area A_0 to corrected area A = A_0 / (1 - ε). Deviator stress σ_d = P / A rises to a peak unconfined compressive strength q_u. According to the Mohr–Coulomb failure criterion for total stress with friction angle ϕ_u = 0, the Mohr circle passes through the origin (σ_3 = 0) and has diameter q_u. Consequently, the undrained shear strength is exactly half the unconfined compressive strength: c_u = q_u / 2. In this laboratory, cohesive soil specimens are tested across axial strains from 1% to 6% to calibrate the undrained modulus E_u and evaluate undrained shear strength c_u.

σ_d = 28.5000 · ε

1. Total Stress Analysis & The ϕ_u = 0 Concept

When saturated cohesive clay is subjected to rapid external loading without allowing pore water drainage, the soil responds under undrained total stress conditions. Because water is practically incompressible relative to the soil skeleton, any increase in confining pressure $\Delta \sigma_3$ is transferred entirely to pore water pressure ($\Delta u = \Delta \sigma_3$), meaning effective stress remains completely unchanged. Consequently, the undrained friction angle on a total stress basis is identically zero ($\phi_u = 0$).

2. Mohr Circle at Failure in Unconfined Compression

In unconfined compression, minor principal total stress is atmospheric: $\sigma_3 = 0$. The major principal total stress at peak load is $\sigma_1 = q_u$. The Mohr failure envelope is a horizontal line of constant shear stress $\tau = c_u$. The single Mohr circle has center at $C = (q_u / 2, 0)$ and radius $R = q_u / 2$. Therefore:

\[ c_u = R = \frac{\sigma_1 - \sigma_3}{2} = \frac{q_u - 0}{2} = \frac{q_u}{2} \]

3. Cross-Sectional Area Correction for Bulging

Because saturated clay maintains constant volume during undrained deformation ($V = A_0 L_0 = A L$), axial shortening produces lateral radial expansion (bulging). When axial strain reaches $\varepsilon = \Delta L / L_0$, the current length is $L = L_0(1 - \varepsilon)$. The average corrected cross-sectional area $A$ is:

\[ A = \frac{V}{L} = \frac{A_0 L_0}{L_0(1 - \varepsilon)} = \frac{A_0}{1 - \varepsilon} \]

The true axial deviator stress $\sigma_d$ is evaluated as:

\[ \sigma_d = \frac{P}{A} = \frac{P (1 - \varepsilon)}{A_0} \]

4. Linear Elastic Modulus Model

In the pre-failure deformation domain ($\varepsilon = 1.0\%\text{ to }6.0\%$), deviator stress scales linearly with strain:

\[ \sigma_d = E_{slope} \cdot \varepsilon \]

where $E_{slope} = 28.5000\text{ kPa/\%}$. In standard units, undrained Young's modulus is $E_u = 28.50\text{ kPa}/0.01 = 2850\text{ kPa} = 2.850\text{ MPa}$. Peak UCS reaches $q_u = 171.0\text{ kPa}$, corresponding to undrained shear strength $c_u = 85.50\text{ kPa}$.

Viva voce questions & analytical theory

Why is undrained shear strength c_u equal to exactly half of the unconfined compressive strength (q_u / 2)?

In unconfined compression, minor principal stress is zero (sigma_3 = 0) and major principal stress at failure is sigma_1 = q_u; for saturated clay under rapid loading, friction angle is zero (phi_u = 0), so the Mohr circle radius equals c_u = (sigma_1 - sigma_3) / 2 = q_u / 2.

Why must the cross-sectional area be corrected as A = A_0 / (1 - ε) during compressive loading?

In undrained saturated clay, water cannot escape and soil solids are incompressible, meaning total specimen volume remains constant (A_0 L_0 = A L); as length shortens to L = L_0 (1 - ε), area expands to A = A_0 / (1 - ε).

Why is the unconfined compression test completely invalid for clean dry sands?

Clean sand has zero cohesion (c = 0); without lateral confining pressure (sigma_3 = 0), a dry sand cylinder has zero shear strength (tau_f = sigma_3 tan phi = 0) and collapses under its own self-weight into a loose heap.

What is soil sensitivity (S_t) and how is it evaluated using the UCS test?

Sensitivity is S_t = q_u (undisturbed) / q_u (remolded at identical moisture); sensitive marine clays possess S_t = 4 to 8, and quick clays exceed 16, losing virtually all shear strength upon remolding or seismic shaking.

How does the specimen aspect ratio (L/D = 2.0 to 2.5) influence unconfined compressive strength?

Platen friction restrains lateral expansion at cylinder ends, creating conical confining zones; an aspect ratio L/D ≥ 2.0 ensures the central region is free from platen restraint, permitting an unconstrained 45° failure plane.

What typical failure modes are observed in unconfined compression testing?

Stiff, fissured, or overconsolidated clays fail along a clean, well-defined diagonal shear plane inclined at θ ≈ 45° to 60°; soft, normally consolidated clays fail by plastic bulging into a barrel shape.

Experimental precautions and laboratory safety

  • Maintain rapid testing speed (completed within 10 to 15 minutes) to ensure truly undrained conditions without moisture evaporation.
  • Ensure cylinder ends are trimmed perfectly flat, parallel to each other, and perpendicular to the longitudinal axis.
  • Always apply the area correction formula A = A_0 / (1 - ε); using uncorrected initial area A_0 introduces up to 15% error in q_u.
  • Handle undisturbed clay cores with extreme care to prevent microstructural disturbance that artificially lowers strength.

Theoretical assumptions & boundary condition limits

  • The civil engineering physical models apply within the specified calibrated operational window (1.0 to 6.0 %).
  • Assumes homogeneous, isotropic continuum material behavior without unmodeled micro-fracture anomalies.
  • Continuous sensor readings reflect ordinary least-squares regression across successive observations.
  • Extreme inelastic yielding or cyclic degradation requires non-linear constitutive continuum damage mechanics.
