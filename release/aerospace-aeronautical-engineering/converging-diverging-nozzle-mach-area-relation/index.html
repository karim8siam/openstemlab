FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Validate the isentropic area-Mach number relation (A/A*) in a supersonic de Laval nozzle and determine supersonic Mach expansion across nozzle area ratios. Standardized parameter settings: 1.2, 1.4, 1.68, 2.0, 2.4, 2.97 dimensionless.

01

Nozzle Pre-Run Inspection

Inspect the nozzle throat and divergent duct for moisture or particulate contamination and lock quartz optical windows.

02

Reservoir Charging and Stagnation Stabilization

Charge the reservoir to 8.0 bar and set the regulator to maintain stagnation total pressure P_0 = 500 kPa.

03

Throat Choking Verification

Initiate blowdown flow and verify that the throat static pressure ratio drops to P*/P_0 = 0.528, confirming sonic choking (M = 1).

04

Divergent Tap Pressure Acquisition

Acquire static pressures at divergent stations corresponding to A/A* = 1.20, 1.40, 1.68, 2.00, 2.40, and 2.97.

05

Isentropic Mach Number Computation

Compute local Mach numbers from the isentropic relation: M = sqrt(5 * [(P_0/P)^(2/7) - 1]) for air (gamma = 1.4).

06

Mach-Area Linear Regression

Plot (M - 1.0) versus (A/A* - 1.0), perform linear regression to determine the supersonic expansion gradient, and verify isentropic flow.
