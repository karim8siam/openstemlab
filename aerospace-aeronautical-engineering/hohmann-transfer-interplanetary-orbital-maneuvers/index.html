FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Calculate impulse velocity increments Delta V_1 and Delta V_2 required to transfer between circular orbits and evaluate delta-v budgets across orbit radius ratios. Standardized parameter settings: 0.08, 0.14, 0.2, 0.26, 0.32, 0.38 dimensionless.

01

Initial LEO Orbit Configuration

Set initial circular Low Earth Orbit at altitude h_1 = 300 km (r_1 = 6678.14 km), confirming circular speed v_1 = 7.780 km/s.

02

Target Orbit Altitude Stepping

Define target circular orbit radii r_2 corresponding to parameter x = sqrt(2*r_2/(r_1+r_2)) - 1 = 0.08, 0.14, 0.20, 0.26, 0.32, and 0.38.

03

First Tangential Burn Execution

Apply impulsive tangential burn Delta V_1 at periapsis to inject spacecraft into the Hohmann transfer ellipse.

04

Transfer Orbit Propagation

Propagate the spacecraft along the elliptical arc over transfer time t_trans = pi * sqrt(a_trans^3 / mu) until apoapsis arrival.

05

Second Circularization Burn

Apply circularizing impulse Delta V_2 at apoapsis to match target circular speed v_2, recording total delta-v budget.

06

Delta-V Linear Regression

Plot Delta V_1 versus the radius parameter x, perform linear regression to determine slope v_1 = 7.780 km/s, and verify Tsiolkovsky propellant mass.
