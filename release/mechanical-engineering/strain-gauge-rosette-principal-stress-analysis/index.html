FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Measure three-axis surface strains on an aluminum beam undergoing combined bending and torsion using a 45° rectangular rosette, determining principal stresses and maximum shear stress. Standardized parameter settings: 100.0, 200.0, 300.0, 400.0, 500.0, 600.0 N.

  1. Visual Inspection & Apparatus Zero Tare: Inspect all mechanical fixtures, load train alignments, safety cages, and hydraulic/electrical connections. Execute zero tare on digital indicators and balance the measurement bridge.
  2. Initial Calibration & Datum Alignment: Apply a minor pre-load to eliminate mechanical backlash and seating slack. Calibrate the bending load p against the primary standard datum.
  3. Systematic Parameter Variation & Settling: Increment the bending load p systematically across the designated experimental range (100.0 to 600.0 N), permitting mechanical and thermal equilibrium at each step.
  4. Multi-Sensor Telemetry Acquisition: Record the primary dependent variable (Calculated Maximum Principal Stress σ_1) alongside auxiliary channel telemetry. Verify linear proportionality and signal stability before advancing.
  5. Forward & Reverse Hysteresis Verification: Execute both ascending loading and descending unloading sequences to detect any friction lag, mechanical backlash, or permanent plastic deformation in the test assembly.
  6. Controlled De-Energization & Data Archival: Release all mechanical loads smoothly to zero, de-energize primary drives, secure moving linkages, and export all digital telemetry observations to the persistent laboratory notebook.
