Aeronautical assumptions and boundary condition limits

Linear divergence theory assumes 2D strip aerodynamics without 3D wingtip vortex relief. Linear regression of the twist metric against dynamic pressure yields a slope m = 0.3125 kPa^-1 corresponding to q_div = 3.200 kPa with R^2 > 0.999999.

FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Measure elastic wing twist theta under aerodynamic loading in a wind tunnel and extrapolate the critical divergence dynamic pressure q_div and airspeed V_div. Standardized parameter settings: 0.3, 0.6, 0.9, 1.2, 1.5, 1.8 kPa.

01

Zero Airspeed Angle Setting

Set the root incidence angle to an initial geometric angle of attack alpha_0 = 2.0 degrees with zero wind.

02

Laser Inclinometer Baseline Alignment

Zero the optical laser mirror receiver, confirming theta = 0 deg relative to root incidence.

03

Low-Speed Dynamic Pressure Initialization

Run the wind tunnel to dynamic pressure q = 0.30 kPa, recording steady-state aeroelastic twist angle theta.

04

Systematic Dynamic Pressure Stepping

Step dynamic pressure through q = 0.60, 0.90, 1.20, 1.50, and 1.80 kPa, recording twist angle theta at each plateau.

05

Twist Amplification Metric Calculation

Compute total incidence (alpha_0 + theta) and calculate the non-dimensional metric (1 - alpha_0 / theta).

06

Divergence Extrapolation Regression

Plot (1 - alpha_0 / theta) versus q, perform linear regression to determine slope 1 / q_div, and calculate divergence dynamic pressure q_div = 1 / m.
