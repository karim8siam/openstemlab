Agricultural assumptions and boundary condition limits

The linear model applies to the upper 30 cm of uniform, settled topsoil prior to reaching dense glacial till or bedrock. Evaluated in an agricultural silt loam soil (gravimetric moisture content 17.5%, bulk density 1.35 g/cm³) using an ASABE standard 30° cone (base area 3.23 cm²). Linear penetration gradient is evaluated across the upper 30 cm depth profile at standard 30 mm/s penetration rate.

Agricultural apparatus & field instrumentation sensors.

Motorized Hydraulic Penetrometer Rig

Drives the penetrometer shaft vertically into soil at standard ASABE velocity of 30 mm/s (±5 mm/s).

ASABE Standard 30° Stainless Steel Cone

Provides standardized soil cutting geometry with base diameter 20.27 mm (base area 323 mm²).

Tension-Compression S-Beam Load Cell

Measures vertical penetration resistance force with 1.0 N resolution up to 2000 N capacity.

Draw-Wire Linear Position Transducer

Measures continuous insertion depth from 0 to 60 cm with 0.5 mm spatial resolution.

Field Data Acquisition Telemetry Logger

Synchronously samples depth and force at 100 Hz to generate continuous high-density penetration curves.

Time-Domain Reflectometry (TDR) Moisture Probe

Measures volumetric soil moisture content concurrently at 5 cm depth intervals.

FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Measure vertical cone penetration resistance force across soil depth to identify tillage pans, compaction layers, and calculate the ASABE Cone Index (CI). Standardized parameter settings: 5.0, 10.0, 15.0, 20.0, 25.0, 30.0 cm.

01

Rig Leveling and Anchor Stabilization

Position the mobile penetrometer frame over the surveyed test grid and anchor hydraulic outriggers to eliminate frame deflection.

02

Transducer Zeroing and Taring

Inspect the 30° cone for surface wear, align the shaft vertically with a spirit level, and zero the load cell in free air above the soil.

03

Penetration Rate Standardization

Set the electric-hydraulic servo actuator to maintain a constant vertical advance velocity of 30 mm/s.

04

Continuous Insertion Profile Acquisition

Initiate automated insertion down to 30 cm depth while logging instantaneous force F_p and depth z continuously.

05

Discrete Depth Sampling Extraction

Extract representative penetration force readings at standard 5 cm depth increments (5.0, 10.0, 15.0, 20.0, 25.0, 30.0 cm).

06

Cone Index and Gradient Calculation

Calculate Cone Index CI = F_p / A_c and plot F_p against depth z, performing linear regression to evaluate the penetration gradient k_p.
