Agricultural assumptions and boundary condition limits

Linear proportionality D = k_s·A holds strictly at constant operating speed; speed variations introduce quadratic dynamic errors. FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Measure the horizontal draft force of a multi-bottom moldboard plow as a function of furrow cross-sectional area and evaluate specific soil resistance. Standardized parameter settings: 0.04, 0.06, 0.08, 0.1, 0.12, 0.14 m².

01

Plow Leveling & Alignment

Set tractor wheel tread to match furrow bottom width and adjust tractor three-point top link and leveling rod so the plow frame sits parallel to the ground in both lateral and longitudinal directions.

02

Baseline Soil Profiling

Sample soil moisture and bulk density at 5 cm depth increments across the test plot to verify uniform pre-tillage soil mechanical conditions.

03

Initial Depth Calibration

Set the gage wheel to initial shallow depth (d = 12 cm, A = 0.042 m²) and verify full furrow slice inversion without scouring failure.

04

Steady-State Draft Logging

Operate the tractor at constant 5.5 km/h over a 60-meter measuring strip, recording three-point hitch draft load cell channels continuously.

05

Depth Incrementation

Systematically lower the plow depth in five discrete steps from 12 cm to 28 cm, measuring furrow width and depth with the profiler for each run.

06

Specific Resistance Computation

Calculate furrow cross-sectional area A = width × depth, plot draft force versus area, and compute the slope representing specific soil resistance k_s.
