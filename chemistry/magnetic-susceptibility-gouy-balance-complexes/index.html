Experimental parameters: sample mass m_s = 2.500 g (0.00250 kg), tube inner diameter d = 4.00 mm, powder packing height h = 50.00 mm (0.0500 m), gravitational acceleration g = 9.80665 m/s², magnetic permeability μ_0 = 4π × 10⁻⁷ N/A². Across the six magnetic flux density settings B = [0.40, 0.60, 0.80, 1.00, 1.20, 1.40] T (B² = [0.16, 0.36, 0.64, 1.00, 1.44, 1.96] T²), the measured apparent mass gains are Δm = [102.312, 230.203, 409.249, 639.452, 920.811, 1253.326] mg. Ordinary least-squares regression yields an empirical slope S = 639.4521 mg/T² (6.39452 × 10⁻⁴ kg/T²) and intercept b = −0.0001 mg with Pearson correlation coefficient R² = 0.9999999999998. Applying the Gouy force relation extracts a gram magnetic susceptibility χ_g(SI) = 3.15209 × 10⁻⁷ m³/kg (χ_g(cgs) = 2.50834 × 10⁻⁵ emu/g). Multiplying by molar mass gives raw molar susceptibility χ_M = 0.009836 emu/mol. Applying Pascal's diamagnetic correction for [Fe(H2O)6](NH4)2(SO4)2 (|χ_dia| = 230 × 10⁻⁶ emu/mol) yields the corrected paramagnetic molar susceptibility χ_M,corr = 0.010066 emu/mol. Applying the Curie relation μ_eff = √[8 · T · χ_M,corr] extracts an effective magnetic moment μ_eff = 4.90 μ_B (difference = 0.00007% from the 4.90 μ_B theoretical spin-only benchmark for high-spin d⁶ Fe²⁺), rigorously confirming four unpaired d-electrons.

FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Determine the mass and molar magnetic susceptibility, effective magnetic moment μ_eff, and number of unpaired d-electrons in hexaaquairon(II) ammonium sulfate (Mohr's salt) using a calibrated Gouy magnetic balance. Suggested independent volumetric settings: 0.16, 0.36, 0.64, 1.0, 1.44, 1.96 T².

01

Pulverization and Uniform Packing of Mohr's Salt

Finely pulverize high-purity Mohr's salt crystals in an agate mortar to produce a uniform microcrystalline powder (< 100 mesh). Carefully transfer the powder into the clean, dry Gouy tube in small increments of ~5 mm, tapping the tube firmly on a rubber mat after each addition to eliminate air voids and establish uniform packing density up to a height of h = 50.00 ± 0.05 mm. Record net sample mass m_s = 2.500 g.

02

Gouy Balance Tare and Empty Tube Diamagnetic Blank Calibration

Suspend the empty Pyrex Gouy tube between the electromagnet pole faces using the phosphor-bronze wire. With the magnet power switched off (B = 0 T), tare the microbalance to 0.0000 g. Energize the electromagnet across field settings (0.40 to 1.40 T) and record the small diamagnetic mass change of the empty tube (Δm_tube) to serve as a baseline blank correction.

03

Magnetic Gap Flux Density Calibration via Hall Probe

Position the calibrated transverse Hall probe at the central horizontal axis between the pole caps where the lower tip of the Gouy tube will hang. Increment the regulated DC current from 2.0 A to 15.0 A to establish target magnetic flux densities B = 0.40, 0.60, 0.80, 1.00, 1.20, and 1.40 T (B² = 0.16, 0.36, 0.64, 1.00, 1.44, and 1.96 T²). Confirm field stability and linearity.

04

Alignment of the Packed Sample Tube in the Field Gradient

Suspend the packed Mohr's salt tube from the balance arm so that its lower flat base coincides with the central horizontal plane of the pole caps (maximum field B) while its upper powder meniscus resides in the near-zero fringe field (B₀ ≈ 0). Ensure the tube hangs freely without touching the pole faces or draft shield walls. Record the unperturbed initial mass m₀ at B = 0 T.

05

Measurement of Paramagnetic Downward Force vs Field Strength

Apply magnetic fields B = 0.40, 0.60, 0.80, 1.00, 1.20, and 1.40 T sequentially. At each field setting, allow the balance to mechanically damp and equilibrate for 60 seconds, recording the apparent mass m_field. Calculate the net downward paramagnetic mass change Δm = (m_field − m₀) − Δm_tube in milligrams.

06

Regression Analysis, Susceptibility & Spin-Only Electron Extraction

Plot Δm (mg) against B² (T²). Execute ordinary least-squares linear regression to determine the empirical slope S = (m_s · χ_g) / (2 μ₀ g h). Convert the mass susceptibility χ_g to molar susceptibility χ_M, apply Pascal's diamagnetic corrections (χ_dia = −230 × 10⁻⁶ cm³/mol) to isolate paramagnetic molar susceptibility χ_M,corr, and compute the effective magnetic moment μ_eff = 2.828 √(χ_M,corr · T), establishing the number of unpaired d-electrons via the spin-only formula.
