Subject Orientation, Dark/Light Adaptation & Informed Consent: Greet participant, confirm normal or corrected-to-normal vision and hearing, obtain informed consent, and allow 10 minutes of dark or photopic ambient adaptation in the testing booth before initiating Bistable Necker Cube & Binocular Rivalry Perceptual Reversal Dynamics.
  2. Hardware System Diagnostics & Photometric Calibration: Verify millisecond timer polling frequency, calibrate display gamma curve with the spot photometer, and verify zero baseline latency on the response console.
  3. Practice Familiarization Trials & Criterion Stabilization: Administer 20 practice trials with immediate accuracy and latency feedback to familiarize the participant with task demands and eliminate transient motor learning effects.
  4. Experimental Acquisition & Setpoint Progression: Systematically vary the independent variable (Duration) across the designated parameter space. Capture at least 30 randomized trials per level, recording millisecond behavioral response latency and accuracy.
  5. Signal Quality Audit & Artifact Screening: Inspect real-time telemetry logs. Flag anticipatory responses (<150 ms), attentional lapses (>2500 ms), eye blinks during critical stimulus epochs, or response key recording errors for trial repetition.
  6. Psychometric Function Fitting & Parameter Extraction: Compile observation vectors across all valid test trials.
