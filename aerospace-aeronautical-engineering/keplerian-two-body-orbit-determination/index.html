FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Determine spacecraft orbital velocity v(r) and specific mechanical energy epsilon from geocentric radius r using Kepler's laws and the Vis-Viva energy equation. Standardized parameter settings: 1.05, 1.14, 1.22, 1.31, 1.4, 1.48 m^-1.

01

Orbit Definition and Initialization

Initialize an eccentric Earth orbit with perigee altitude h_p = 400 km and apogee altitude h_a = 10,842 km (semi-major axis a = 12,000 km, e = 0.435).

02

Gravitational Parameter Datum

Confirm Earth gravitational parameter mu = 3.986004 x 10^14 m^3/s^2 and Earth mean radius R_E = 6378.137 km.

03

Telemetry State Vector Sampling

Track the satellite through true anomaly positions corresponding to inverse radius (1/r) x 10^7 = 1.05, 1.14, 1.22, 1.31, 1.40, and 1.48 m^-1.

04

Orbital Velocity Acquisition

Record instantaneous inertial velocity magnitude v at each radial station using simulated Doppler telemetry.

05

Specific Energy Verification

Calculate specific orbital energy epsilon = 0.5 * v^2 - mu / r, confirming constancy across all observation points.

06

Vis-Viva Linear Regression

Plot (v^2) x 10^-7 versus (1/r) x 10^7, perform linear regression to determine slope 2*mu x 10^-14, and compute semi-major axis from the intercept.
