FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Determine producer gas heating value and cold gas thermal efficiency of an agricultural residue downdraft gasifier as a function of equivalence ratio. Standardized parameter settings: 0.2, 0.24, 0.28, 0.32, 0.36, 0.4 ratio.

01

Feedstock Loading and Moisture Verification

Fill the reactor hopper with 25.0 kg of dried corncob chunks (size 25-50 mm, moisture 11.2%) and latch the airtight feed lid.

02

Char Bed Ignition and Pre-Heating

Ignite the charcoal bed at the tuyeres with an LPG torch, drawing primary air via the suction blower until throat temperature exceeds 800 °C.

03

Equivalence Ratio Standardization

Adjust blast air flow rate to establish the initial test equivalence ratio Φ = 0.20 based on measured biomass consumption rate.

04

Steady Thermal Gasification Operation

Operate the gasifier for 30 minutes at steady state, monitoring throat temperature (950-1050 °C) and syngas flare flame stability.

05

Syngas Composition and Flow Acquisition

Sample clean, conditioned syngas through the continuous infrared/thermal conductivity gas analyzer and log volumetric flow rate.

06

Equivalence Progression and HHV Evaluation

Step the equivalence ratio through 0.24, 0.28, 0.32, 0.36, and 0.40, computing producer gas HHV = Σ (y_i · HHV_i) and fitting linear regression.
