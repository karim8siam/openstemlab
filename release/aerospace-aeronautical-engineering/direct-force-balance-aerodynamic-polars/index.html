Aeronautical assumptions and boundary condition limits

Parabolic polar relationship C_D = C_D0 + k * C_L^2 is valid strictly for attached flow prior to the stall boundary. The linear regression of C_D against C_L^2 yields an induced drag factor k = 0.0450 and parasite drag C_D0 = 0.0180 with R^2 > 0.999999.

FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Measure lift and drag forces simultaneously on a wing model using an external 3-component strain-gauge balance to determine parasite drag coefficient C_D0 and induced drag factor k. Standardized parameter settings: 0.04, 0.16, 0.36, 0.64, 0.81, 1.0 dimensionless.

01

Tare Weight and Balance Calibration

Execute zero-flow weight tare across all balance load cells to eliminate gravity tare moments across the pitch range.

02

Wind Tunnel Velocity Baseline

Start the wind tunnel and bring test-section speed to V_inf = 30 m/s, stabilizing dynamic pressure at q_inf = 551 Pa.

03

Zero-Lift Alignment Verification

Set angle of attack to alpha = 0 deg and confirm that measured lift on the symmetric wing is zero within balance tolerance.

04

Systematic Drag Polar Sweep

Step the model through angles of attack corresponding to C_L^2 = 0.04, 0.16, 0.36, 0.64, 0.81, and 1.00.

05

Force Resolution to Wind Axes

Resolve measured normal and axial balance forces into wind-axis lift L and drag D using measured pitch angles.

06

Drag Polar Plotting and Regression

Plot C_D versus C_L^2, perform linear regression to determine parasite drag C_D0 and induced drag factor k, and compute (L/D)_max.
