    Prepare, cut, calibrate, or orient the testing specimens under strictly regulated environmental laboratory conditions conforming to USGS and IAU guidelines.

  3. Step 3

    Independent Setpoint Parameter Adjustment

    Adjust the independent testing parameter (Grain Diameter Squared) across the designated analytical range (0.2 to 2.0 mm²) in uniform calibrated steps.

  4. Step 4

    Scientific Telemetry Acquisition & Logging

    Execute the experimental run and record steady-state telemetry for Terminal Gravitational Settling Velocity with high-precision digital integration.

  5. Step 5

    Replicate Inspection & Environmental Equilibrium Check

    Perform duplicate test acquisitions at each testing setpoint to verify signal stability, absence of thermal drift, and sample reproducibility.

  6. Step 6

    Regression Calculation & Geophysical Parameter Derivation

    Plot Grain Diameter Squared (mm²) against Settling Velocity (mm/s), calculate ordinary least-squares (OLS) linear regression parameters, and derive Stokes Sedimentation Hydrodynamic Constant (C_settle) against certified reference benchmarks.
