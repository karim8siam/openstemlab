FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Evaluate the peak discharge current I_peak of a clinical external defibrillator across human transthoracic impedances (TTI) using an RLC damped sinusoidal circuit. Standardized parameter settings: 30.0, 45.0, 60.0, 75.0, 90.0, 105.0 Ω.

01

High-Voltage Safety and Analyzer Setup

Connect defibrillator paddle cables to high-voltage analyzer load bank; verify equipment grounding and post high-voltage safety warning signage.

02

Defibrillator Energy Selection

Power on defibrillator and select standard adult energy level: 200 Joules delivered into 50 Ω.

03

Transthoracic Load Resistance Initialization

Configure analyzer precision non-inductive load resistance to R_th = 30.0 Ω and arm the measurement digitizer.

04

Capacitor Charging and Discharge Execution

Charge the internal capacitor bank to full 200 J potential; clear the testing perimeter and depress the shock discharge button.

05

Peak Discharge Current Capture

Capture the damped sinusoidal current waveform on the analyzer oscilloscope and record peak current I_peak (A) and total delivered energy (J).

06

Transthoracic Impedance Progression

Increment load resistance through 45.0, 60.0, 75.0, 90.0, and 105.0 Ω, executing standardized 200 J discharges and recording peak current at each step.

07

Current-Impedance Sensitivity Regression

Plot peak discharge current I_peak versus transthoracic impedance R_th and calculate sensitivity slope k_r and zero-impedance current I_0 via linear regression.
