Biomedical assumptions and boundary condition limits

The linear sensitivity model applies to static axial loading; dynamic ambulation introduces coupled shear stresses and moment loads. Evaluated on an anatomically contoured robotic residual limb mannequin with silicone soft tissue surrogate (Shore 20A durometer) fitted inside a carbon-fiber PTB prosthetic socket. Instrumented with calibrated thin-film piezoresistive pressure sensors at the patellar-tendon bar. Linear sensitivity k_soc = 0.2200 kPa/N across loads from 200 N to 700 N.

Biomedical apparatus & physiological sensors.

Anatomical Transtibial Residual Limb Mannequin

Provides rigid skeletal core (tibia/fibula) surrounded by elastomeric soft-tissue simulating muscle and skin.

Carbon-Fiber Patellar-Tendon-Bearing Prosthetic Socket

Custom-laminated below-knee socket with inward patellar-tendon weight-bearing indentation bar.

Thin-Film Piezoresistive Pressure Sensor Array (Tekscan F-Socket)

Matrix of flexible sensors (thickness 0.15 mm) measuring local interface pressure up to 250 kPa.

Servohydraulic Biomechanical Axial Loading Ram

Applies controlled vertical limb loads W from 200 N to 700 N with closed-loop force control.

Multichannel Piezoresistive Digital Interfacing Hub

Scans 96 pressure matrix sensing cells at 100 Hz with 8-bit gray-scale spatial mapping.

Pneumatic Pressure Sensor Calibration Bladder

Applies uniform hydrostatic pressure steps to calibrate piezoresistive array prior to limb insertion.

FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Map the interface contact pressure at the patellar-tendon bar of a patellar-tendon-bearing (PTB) prosthetic socket as a function of axial limb weight-bearing load. Standardized parameter settings: 200.0, 300.0, 400.0, 500.0, 600.0, 700.0 N.

01

Sensor Matrix Equi-Pressure Calibration

Place thin-film sensor array inside pneumatic bladder calibration chamber and apply 50, 100, and 150 kPa pressures to generate cell-by-cell calibration lookup tables.

02

Sensor Placement along Patellar-Tendon Bar

Affix calibrated sensor matrix securely along the patellar-tendon bar and medial tibial flare of the residual limb mannequin using skin-safe adhesive film.

03

Prosthetic Socket Donning and Alignment

Carefully don a 6 mm silicone gel prosthetic liner and seat the residual limb into the carbon-fiber PTB socket, verifying anatomical alignment.

04

Axial Load Initialization and Seating Tare

Position the prosthetic assembly under the vertical loading ram and apply a 50 N tare load to bed down the elastomeric liner.

05

Stepwise Axial Weight-Bearing Progression

Increment axial vertical load through 200, 300, 400, 500, 600, and 700 N, recording stabilized mean patellar-tendon contact pressure P_ptb at each step.

06

Load Sensitivity Regression Analysis

Plot patellar-tendon interface pressure P_ptb against total axial load W and compute the socket load transfer constant k_soc via linear regression.
