    Instrument Initialization & Baseline Zero Tare

    Power on the analytical instrumentation for Microbial Respiration & Dissolved Oxygen, calibrate baseline telemetry against certified standard seawater or reference solutions, and verify optical/hydrodynamic alignment.

  2. Step 2

    Sample Collection & Oceanographic Conditioning

    Prepare, filter, equilibrate, or collect the marine test specimens under strictly regulated environmental laboratory conditions conforming to IOC, UNESCO, and WOCE guidelines.

  3. Step 3

    Independent Setpoint Parameter Adjustment

    Adjust the independent testing parameter (Incubation Time) across the designated analytical range (4.0 to 24.0 hours) in uniform calibrated steps.

  4. Step 4

    Scientific Telemetry Acquisition & Logging

    Execute the experimental run and record steady-state telemetry for Residual Dissolved Oxygen Concentration with high-precision digital integration.

  5. Step 5

    Replicate Inspection & Environmental Equilibrium Check

    Perform duplicate test acquisitions at each testing setpoint to verify signal stability, absence of thermal drift, and sample reproducibility.

  6. Step 6

    Regression Calculation & Oceanographic Parameter Derivation

    Plot Incubation Time (h) against Residual Oxygen (mg/L), calculate ordinary least-squares (OLS) linear regression parameters, and derive Specific Heterotrophic Microbial Respiration Rate (R_bact) against certified reference benchmarks.
