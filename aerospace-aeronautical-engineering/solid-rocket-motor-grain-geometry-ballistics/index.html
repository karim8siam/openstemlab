FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Measure chamber pressure P_c(t) and thrust F(t) as a function of propellant burning surface area A_b(t) and Saint-Robert's burn rate law r = a * P_c^n. Standardized parameter settings: 2.5, 3.5, 5.0, 6.5, 8.0, 10.0 MPa.

01

Motor Assembly and Throat Measurement

Measure graphite nozzle throat diameter with optical micrometer (A_t = 125 mm^2) and assemble BATES grain.

02

Safety Interlock and Bunker Evacuation

Arm pyrotechnic squib circuit and evacuate personnel to blast bunker, verifying sensor calibration.

03

Static Firing Execution

Ignite the motor, sampling chamber pressure P_c(t) and thrust F(t) at 10,000 samples per second.

04

Action Time and Burn Rate Extraction

Determine burn action time t_b from the 75% pressure-time inflection points, calculating average burn rate r = w_web / t_b.

05

Multi-Pressure Throat Variation Sweep

Repeat tests across modular nozzle throats to achieve steady chamber pressures P_c = 2.5, 3.5, 5.0, 6.5, 8.0, and 10.0 MPa.

06

Saint-Robert Log-Log Linear Regression

Plot ln(r) versus ln(P_c), perform linear regression to extract pressure exponent n as the slope, and verify combustion stability (n < 1.0).
