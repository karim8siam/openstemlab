Quantify total flavonoid antioxidant content in Ginkgo biloba herbal extracts using aluminum chloride chromophore chelation spectrophotometry at 425 nm. Standardized parameter settings: 5.0, 14.0, 23.0, 32.0, 41.0, 50.0 μg/mL.

    Instrument Initialization & Baseline Tare

    Power on the analytical instrumentation for Herbal Flavonoid Spectrophotometry, allow a 30-minute electronic warm-up period, and verify transducer zero-tare using certified standards.

  2. Step 2

    Standard Solution & Matrix Specimen Preparation

    Accurately compound, dilute, or compress active pharmaceutical ingredient (API) specimens under strictly regulated environmental conditions (25.0 ± 0.5 °C).

  3. Step 3

    Systematic Independent Variable Setting

    Adjust the independent testing parameter (Quercetin Conc) across the designated analytical range (5.0 to 50.0 μg/mL) in uniform calibrated steps.

  4. Step 4

    Analytical Signal Acquisition & Telemetry Logging

    Execute the measurement cycle and record steady-state telemetry for Aluminum Chelate Chromophore Absorbance with high-precision digital integration.

  5. Step 5

    Replicate Verification & Equilibrium Inspection

    Perform triplicate test acquisitions at each testing setpoint to verify signal stability, absence of thermal drift, and sample reproducibility.

  6. Step 6

    Regression Modeling & Pharmacopoeial Parameter Derivation

    Plot Quercetin Conc (μg/mL) against Absorbance A425 (AU), execute ordinary least-squares (OLS) linear regression, and calculate Flavonoid Chelate Extinction Coefficient (m) against compendial benchmarks.
