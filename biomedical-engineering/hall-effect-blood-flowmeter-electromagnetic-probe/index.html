Biomedical assumptions and boundary condition limits

The linear induction model applies across non-turbulent and turbulent regimes up to 300 mL/min for the 6 mm probe. Evaluated using a 6.0 mm internal diameter cannulated flow probe with a 250 Hz square-wave AC magnetic excitation field (B = 0.050 Tesla). Perfusion fluid is physiological saline (0.9% NaCl, electrical conductivity 1.6 S/m) pumped from 50 to 300 mL/min.

Biomedical apparatus & physiological sensors.

Cannulated Electromagnetic Flow Probe (6 mm lumen)

Provides transverse electromagnet pole pieces and diametrically opposed platinum electrodes.

Square-Wave AC Magnet Excitation Driver

Supplies regulated 250 Hz alternating current to electromagnet coils to eliminate DC polarization.

Precision Peristaltic Physiological Perfusion Pump

Delivers calibrated, non-pulsatile volumetric saline flow rates from 50 to 300 mL/min.

Synchronous Gated Demodulator & Preamplifier

Samples induced electrode potential during steady-state magnetic field plateaus.

Precision Analytical Balance & Gravimetric Catch Reservoir

Verifies true volumetric flow rate by timed gravimetric mass collection (±0.1 mL/min).

Differential Bio-Potential Voltage Telemetry Console

Digitizes microvolt-level induced signals with 24-bit resolution and low-pass filtering.

FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Determine the induced transverse bio-potential across cannulated physiological saline flow under an applied transverse magnetic field and verify Faraday's law of induction. Standardized parameter settings: 50, 100, 150, 200, 250, 300 mL/min.

01

Fluid Circuit Priming and Zero Flow Balancing

Fill the flow loop with 0.9% physiological saline at 37.0 °C, purge all air bubbles from the probe lumen, stop pump, and adjust zero offset to 0.00 μV.

02

Electromagnet AC Drive Stabilization

Energize probe electromagnet with 250 Hz square-wave excitation; verify magnetic flux density B = 0.050 T at center bore.

03

Initial Flow Rate Standardization

Set perfusion pump to deliver steady 50 mL/min volumetric flow rate, verified via timed gravimetric collection on electronic balance.

04

Induced Potential Sampling

Sample the synchronously gated microvolt potential V_i across platinum pickup electrodes over a 30-second integration window.

05

Flow Progression Sequence

Increment pump delivery to 100, 150, 200, 250, and 300 mL/min, recording stabilized induced voltage V_i at each flow step.

06

Induction Constant Regression Analysis

Plot induced voltage V_i against volumetric flow rate Q, computing the induction constant k_q via linear regression through the origin.
