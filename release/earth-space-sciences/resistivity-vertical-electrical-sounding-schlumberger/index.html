FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Map subsurface hydrogeological strata and freshwater aquifer boundaries by measuring apparent electrical resistivity using a Schlumberger four-electrode array. Standardized parameter settings: 5.0, 14.0, 23.0, 32.0, 41.0, 50.0 m.

  1. Step 1

    Instrument Initialization & Environmental Baseline Tare

    Power on the analytical instrumentation for Schlumberger VES Hydrogeophysics, calibrate baseline telemetry against certified reference standards, and verify optical/mechanical alignment.

  2. Step 2

    Specimen Preparation & Geological Conditioning

    Prepare, cut, calibrate, or orient the testing specimens under strictly regulated environmental laboratory conditions conforming to USGS and IAU guidelines.

  3. Step 3

    Independent Setpoint Parameter Adjustment

    Adjust the independent testing parameter (Electrode Spacing) across the designated analytical range (5.0 to 50.0 m) in uniform calibrated steps.

  4. Step 4

    Scientific Telemetry Acquisition & Logging

    Execute the experimental run and record steady-state telemetry for Apparent Subsurface Resistivity (Rho_a) with high-precision digital integration.

  5. Step 5

    Replicate Inspection & Environmental Equilibrium Check

    Perform duplicate test acquisitions at each testing setpoint to verify signal stability, absence of thermal drift, and sample reproducibility.

  6. Step 6

    Regression Calculation & Geophysical Parameter Derivation

    Plot Electrode Spacing (m) against Apparent Resistivity (Ω·m), calculate ordinary least-squares (OLS) linear regression parameters, and derive Apparent Resistivity Stratigraphic Gradient (k_rho) against certified reference benchmarks.
