FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Measure high-frequency (50 kHz) four-electrode transthoracic electrical impedance changes (ΔZ) during breathing cycles and calibrate against spirometric tidal volume. Standardized parameter settings: 0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 2.5, 3.0 L.

01

Tetrapolar Electrode Montage Application

Clean skin with alcohol wipes and affix four electrodes along the mid-axillary line: outer pair for 50 kHz current injection, inner pair for voltage pickup.

02

Baseline Impedance Balance and Carrier Setup

Energize 50 kHz 200 μA oscillator; record baseline thoracic resting end-expiratory impedance (Z_0, typically 25 to 50 Ω).

03

Simultaneous Spirometer Coupling

Seat subject comfortably and fit nose clip; connect subject to calibrated turbine spirometer to log air volume simultaneously.

04

Standardized Shallow Inhalation (0.5 L)

Instruct subject to inhale to achieve a spirometric tidal volume V_t of exactly 0.50 L; log stabilized peak impedance excursion ΔZ in Ohms.

05

Tidal Volume Step Progression

Guide subject through voluntary breathing increments of 1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 2.5, and 3.0 L, recording paired (V_t, ΔZ) values across 5 breaths per step.

06

Pneumographic Sensitivity Regression

Plot respiratory impedance amplitude ΔZ against tidal volume V_t, fitting linear regression through the origin to determine sensitivity k_v.
