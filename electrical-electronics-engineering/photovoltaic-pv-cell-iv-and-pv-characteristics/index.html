FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Measure current-voltage (IV) and power-voltage (PV) curves of a silicon solar photovoltaic panel under variable solar irradiance, determining short-circuit current (I_sc), open-circuit voltage (V_oc), fill factor (FF), and maximum power point (P_mpp). Standardized parameter settings: 200.0, 350.0, 500.0, 650.0, 800.0, 950.0 W/m².

  1. Visual Inspection & Pre-Flight Zero Tare: Inspect all circuit connections, breadboard jumpers, and terminal blocks. Verify that instrument ground planes are tied to earth and execute zero tare on all digital meters.
  2. Excitation Source & Baseline Calibration: Energize the regulated supply, set baseline operating thresholds, and calibrate the solar irradiance g using the precision standard reference meter.
  3. Systematic Parameter Variation & Settling: Increment the solar irradiance g systematically across the designated experimental range (200.0 to 950.0 W/m²), permitting thermal settling at each setpoint.
  4. Multi-Meter & Oscilloscope Waveform Acquisition: Record the primary output metric (Short-Circuit Photocurrent I_sc) alongside auxiliary telemetry. Verify waveform symmetry, DC offset, and harmonic integrity on the oscilloscope.
  5. Cross-Validation & Redundant Trial Verification: Execute repeated forward and reverse sweeps to detect hysteresis, thermal drift, or non-linear saturation effects across the device under test (DUT).
  6. Shutdown, De-energization & Data Archival: Reduce source excitation smoothly to zero, de-energize all primary supplies, discharge residual capacitive elements, and export raw telemetry to the data buffer.

Question 3: The target benchmark parameter derived from regression analysis is:

Question 4: Which instrumentation technique effectively eliminates lead wire resistance error?

Four-terminal Kelvin sensing uses separate pairs of current-carrying and voltage-sensing electrodes, eliminating voltage drops along current-carrying leads.
