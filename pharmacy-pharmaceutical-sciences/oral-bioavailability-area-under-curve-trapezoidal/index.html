FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Calculate the absolute systemic bioavailability (F) of an oral tablet formulation by evaluating dose-dependent Area Under the Curve (AUC) using trapezoidal numerical integration. Standardized parameter settings: 100.0, 200.0, 300.0, 400.0, 500.0, 600.0 mg.

  1. Step 1

    Instrument Initialization & Baseline Tare

    Power on the analytical instrumentation for Bioavailability & AUC Integration, allow a 30-minute electronic warm-up period, and verify transducer zero-tare using certified standards.

  2. Step 2

    Standard Solution & Matrix Specimen Preparation

    Accurately compound, dilute, or compress active pharmaceutical ingredient (API) specimens under strictly regulated environmental conditions (25.0 ± 0.5 °C).

  3. Step 3

    Systematic Independent Variable Setting

    Adjust the independent testing parameter (Oral Dose) across the designated analytical range (100.0 to 600.0 mg) in uniform calibrated steps.

  4. Step 4

    Analytical Signal Acquisition & Telemetry Logging

    Execute the measurement cycle and record steady-state telemetry for Total Area Under Plasma Curve with high-precision digital integration.

  5. Step 5

    Replicate Verification & Equilibrium Inspection

    Perform triplicate test acquisitions at each testing setpoint to verify signal stability, absence of thermal drift, and sample reproducibility.

  6. Step 6

    Regression Modeling & Pharmacopoeial Parameter Derivation

    Plot Oral Dose (mg) against AUC_0-inf (mg·h/L), execute ordinary least-squares (OLS) linear regression, and calculate Systemic Exposure Sensitivity Slope (m) against compendial benchmarks.
