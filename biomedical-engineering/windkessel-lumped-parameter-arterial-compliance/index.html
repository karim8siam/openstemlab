Biomedical assumptions and boundary condition limits

The two-element Windkessel model neglects wave reflection phenomena that create localized systolic pressure amplification in peripheral arteries. Evaluated on a mock cardiovascular circulatory flow loop with a silicone elastic aorta and variable peripheral resistance valve. Systemic parameters: peripheral resistance R_p = 1.00 mmHg·s/mL (133.3 kPa·s/L), end-systolic dicrotic notch pressure P_es = 100 mmHg. Diastolic time constant τ = R_p · C_a = 800 ms, yielding decay rate k_w = 1/800 ms = 0.00125 ms⁻¹ (arterial compliance C_a = 1.067 mL/mmHg). Evaluated across times 100 to 600 ms.

FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Determine the arterial Windkessel decay rate constant k_w and evaluate total systemic arterial compliance C_a from the exponential diastolic pressure runoff curve. Standardized parameter settings: 100.0, 200.0, 300.0, 400.0, 500.0, 600.0 ms.

01

Circulatory Loop Priming and Transducer Calibration

Prime flow loop with 40% glycerol-water blood analog (viscosity 3.8 cP at 22 °C), purge air, and calibrate Millar pressure transducer against mercury manometer.

02

Ventricular Hemodynamic Stabilization

Set pneumatic driver to 72 BPM (stroke volume 70 mL, systolic duration 300 ms, diastolic duration 533 ms); verify stable 120/80 mmHg aortic pressure wave.

03

Dicrotic Notch Incisura Identification

Locate the dicrotic notch (closure of aortic valve) marking end-systole (t = 0, P_es = 100.0 mmHg) to define the onset of pure diastolic runoff.

04

Diastolic Pressure Waveform Sampling

Sample decaying arterial pressure P(t) at standardized post-incisura intervals: t = 100, 200, 300, 400, 500, and 600 ms across 10 cardiac cycles.

05

Logarithmic Transformation Calculation

Compute normalized pressure ratio P(t)/P_es and take natural logarithm y = ln(P/P_es) at each time step.

06

Windkessel Decay Regression Analysis

Plot ln(P/P_es) versus decay time t, determine slope -k_w via linear regression, and calculate total arterial compliance C_a = 1 / (R_p · k_w).
