FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Delineate ancient livestock enclosures, settlement middens, and habitation boundaries by quantifying soil extractable orthophosphate concentrations. Standardized parameter settings: 50.0, 100.0, 150.0, 200.0, 250.0, 300.0 mg/kg.

  1. Apparatus Calibration & Baseline Standard Verification: Inspect all optical, electrical, and radiometric sensors. Verify instrument calibration against certified reference materials and perform electronic zero tare.
  2. Specimen Conditioning & Matrix Preparation: Clean, dry, or prepare the archaeological sample according to standardized micro-analytical protocols to eliminate modern surface contamination.
  3. Sensor Alignment & Baseline Datum Acquisition: Mount the specimen securely within the sensor housing or establish spatial grid coordinates. Verify alignment of the phosphate concentration with primary excavation datum.
  4. Systematic Parameter Variation & Acquisition: Increment the phosphate concentration systematically across the designated domain (50.0 to 300.0 mg/kg), permitting full signal equilibration at each measurement station.
  5. Multi-Channel Telemetry Recording & Signal Integration: Record the primary dependent variable (Molybdenum Blue Optical Absorbance A_882) alongside auxiliary environmental monitors. Validate signal stability and confirm low noise thresholds.
  6. Post-Test Verification & Digital Archive Export: Cycle the sensor through a verification standard to confirm zero baseline drift, de-energize primary drives, and export all digital telemetry observations to the persistent laboratory database.
