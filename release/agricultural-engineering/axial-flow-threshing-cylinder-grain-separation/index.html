FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Determine the effect of threshing drum peripheral speed on grain separation efficiency and unthreshed grain loss in an axial-flow combine threshing mechanism. Standardized parameter settings: 20.0, 22.4, 24.8, 27.2, 29.6, 32.0 m/s.

01

Concave Clearance Calibration

Set the front concave inlet clearance to 12.0 mm and the rear outlet clearance to 6.0 mm using feeler thickness gauges, locking the adjustment lever.

02

Standardized Batch Preparation

Prepare uniform 10.0 kg batches of conditioned wheat sheaves with known grain-to-straw ratio (approximately 1:1.2).

03

Initial Speed Setting

Set rotor peripheral velocity to v_c = 20.0 m/s (rotor speed 510 RPM on 75 cm diameter cylinder), allowing the motor to stabilize at idle.

04

Uniform Batch Feeding

Feed the 10.0 kg wheat batch uniformly onto the feed conveyor over exactly 15 seconds, collecting all concave-separated material and straw discharge.

05

Grain Separation Recovery

Clean and weigh all grain separated through the concave (m_sep), hand-thresh all remaining unthreshed heads in the straw to find unthreshed loss (m_loss), and compute separation percentage E_t = m_sep / (m_sep + m_loss) × 100%.

06

Velocity Incrementation

Repeat testing across velocities of 22.4, 24.8, 27.2, 29.6, and 32.0 m/s, plotting E_t versus v_c and evaluating kernel mechanical damage.
