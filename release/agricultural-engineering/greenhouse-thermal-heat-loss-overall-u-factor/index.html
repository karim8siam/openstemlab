FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Evaluate the sensible heat loss from an agricultural greenhouse enclosure across varying indoor-outdoor temperature differentials to determine the overall heat transfer coefficient (U-factor). Standardized parameter settings: 5.0, 10.0, 15.0, 20.0, 25.0, 30.0 °C.

01

Nocturnal Test Standardization

Initiate testing at least two hours after sunset to ensure zero solar thermal storage influence in the floor and soil profile.

02

Heating Power Step Input

Energize internal electric heaters to establish an initial indoor-outdoor temperature difference ΔT of approximately 5 °C.

03

Thermal Equilibrium Verification

Maintain heater output until all interior aspirated thermocouples and heat flux plates achieve steady-state (dT/dt < 0.2 °C/h for 30 minutes).

04

Synchronous Telemetry Acquisition

Log heater power consumption, individual heat flux sensor signals, and spatial air temperatures over a 15-minute integration window.

05

Incremental Differential Progression

Increment the heating controller through 10, 15, 20, 25, and 30 °C differentials, repeating steady-state heat loss logging at each step.

06

U-Factor Regression Analysis

Plot envelope heat loss flux q_h against temperature difference ΔT, fitting a linear regression line to compute overall U_ov.
