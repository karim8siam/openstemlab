FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Analyze the kinematics of an agricultural reciprocating mower/combine cutterbar and determine the knife-to-forward velocity ratio across increasing machine travel speeds. Standardized parameter settings: 1.0, 1.4, 1.8, 2.2, 2.6, 3.0 m/s.

01

Cutterbar Alignment & Register Check

Verify cutterbar knife register by ensuring that knife section tip centers align precisely with guard centers at the stroke reversal dead-center points.

02

Knife Clip Clearance Adjustment

Set hold-down knife clips to provide 0.25 to 0.50 mm vertical clearance above knife sections, preventing binding while maintaining close shear contact with ledger plates.

03

Initial Speed Synchronization

Set forward carriage drive speed to V_f = 1.0 m/s and synchronize wobble-box crank speed to establish target cutting ratio.

04

Kinematic Waveform Logging

Execute test run over the 15-meter track, recording linear encoder position-time telemetry at 1000 Hz throughout the cutting stroke.

05

Speed Stepping

Increment machine forward speed across five additional steps up to 3.0 m/s, measuring peak knife velocity V_k for each trial.

06

Velocity Ratio Extraction

Compute peak knife velocity V_k = ω·(S/2), plot V_k against V_f, and determine the kinematic velocity ratio K_v via linear regression.
