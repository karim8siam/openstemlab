Aeronautical assumptions and boundary condition limits

FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Measure elevator deflection angle delta_e required to maintain steady pitch equilibrium across flight airspeeds and determine control effectiveness C_m_delta_e. Standardized parameter settings: 0.25, 0.4, 0.6, 0.8, 1.0, 1.15 dimensionless.

01

Model Zero Tare and Zero-Elevator Trim

Set elevator to zero (delta_e = 0 deg) and perform wind-off tare calibrations on the 6-component balance.

02

Wind Tunnel Speed Stabilization

Start the wind tunnel to V_inf = 32.0 m/s, recording free-stream dynamic pressure q_inf = 627 Pa.

03

Target Lift Coefficient Pitching

Set model angle of attack to achieve target trim lift coefficient C_L = 0.25.

04

Automated Trim Nulling

Activate the closed-loop auto-trimmer, which drives the elevator until balance pitching moment C_m is driven precisely to zero.

05

Lift Coefficient Stepping

Step the target lift coefficient through C_L = 0.40, 0.60, 0.80, 1.00, and 1.15, recording the equilibrium elevator trim angle delta_e,trim at each point.

06

Trim Gradient Linear Regression

Plot delta_e,trim versus C_L, perform linear regression to determine the elevator trim gradient ddelta_e / dC_L, and extract control effectiveness C_m_delta_e.
