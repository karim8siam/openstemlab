FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Quantify sensible heat production and determine minimum winter ventilation airflow in a confinement livestock housing facility across varying animal biomass loads. Standardized parameter settings: 2000, 4000, 6000, 8000, 10000, 12000 kg.

01

Stocking Biomass Standardization

Weigh and group nursery feeder pigs into uniform pens to establish the initial test biomass level of 2000 kg.

02

Ventilation Rate Calibration

Set exhaust ventilation to a constant continuous airflow rate verified via calibrated intake orifice plate.

03

Room Thermal Stabilization

Allow animals to acclimate for 4 hours until spatial room temperatures and floor temperatures achieve steady thermal balance.

04

Enthalpy and Heat Balance Acquisition

Measure ventilation airflow rate, temperature rise (T_exhaust - T_inlet), and wall transmission losses over a 2-hour monitoring window.

05

Biomass Incrementation Progression

Systematically increase pen stocking biomass to 4000, 6000, 8000, 10000, and 12000 kg, repeating thermal balance measurements.

06

Sensible Heat Coefficient Derivation

Calculate sensible heat output Q_s = V_dot · ρ · C_p · (T_exhaust - T_inlet) + Q_structural and plot Q_s versus biomass M_a to determine q_a.
