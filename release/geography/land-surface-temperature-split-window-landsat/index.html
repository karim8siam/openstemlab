FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Retrieve radiometric Land Surface Temperature (LST) from Landsat TIRS thermal infrared bands using split-window atmospheric and surface emissivity corrections. Standardized parameter settings: 285.0, 290.0, 295.0, 300.0, 305.0, 310.0 K.

  1. Sensor Calibration & Zero Benchmark Verification: Inspect all hydrographic, meteorological, or optical sensors. Verify calibration against certified reference instruments and establish digital zero tare.
  2. Field Site Setup & Geodetic Survey Benchmark Alignment: Deploy the measurement rig across the designated landscape transect or flume channel. Align survey axes with local geodetic benchmarks and leveling datum.
  3. Initial Gauge Alignment & Parameter Setpoint Selection: Position the sensor array at the target sampling station. Select the initial brightness temperature t_10 setpoint and confirm steady-state baseline readings.
  4. Systematic Parameter Variation & Acquisition: Increment the brightness temperature t_10 systematically across the designated domain (285.0 to 310.0 K), permitting full hydrodynamic or atmospheric stabilization at each step.
  5. Multi-Sensor Telemetry Recording & Signal Integration: Record the primary dependent variable (Atmospherically Corrected Land Surface Temperature LST) alongside auxiliary meteorological and channel parameters. Verify signal stability and repeatability.
  6. Post-Survey Verification & Data Archive Export: Check sensors against the baseline benchmark to verify absence of post-run drift, secure all field equipment, and export digital observations to the persistent geospatial database.
