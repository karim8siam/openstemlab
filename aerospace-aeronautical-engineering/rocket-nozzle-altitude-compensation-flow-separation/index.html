FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Detect shock-induced boundary layer separation in over-expanded rocket nozzles using the Summerfield criterion across ambient back-pressures. Standardized parameter settings: 1.2, 1.8, 2.5, 3.4, 4.4, 5.5 dimensionless.

01

Nozzle Inspection and Chamber Sealing

Inspect divergent bell wall taps and seal the nozzle exhaust inside the variable back-pressure altitude chamber.

02

Chamber Pressurization

Supply high-pressure dry air to establish constant nozzle chamber stagnation pressure P_c = 1.0 MPa.

03

Altitude Back-Pressure Regulation

Adjust chamber vacuum pumps to establish ambient back-pressures corresponding to parameter (P_c/P_a)^0.65 = 1.2, 1.8, 2.5, 3.4, 4.4, and 5.5.

04

Wall Pressure Profile Recording

Sample all 32 static pressure taps simultaneously across the nozzle bell contour.

05

Separation Point Identification

Locate the exact axial station where wall static pressure abruptly jumps from isentropic expansion toward ambient back-pressure.

06

Separation Scaling Regression

Plot separation area ratio A_sep / A_t versus (P_c/P_a)^0.65, perform linear regression to determine scaling slope m, and verify the Summerfield ratio.
