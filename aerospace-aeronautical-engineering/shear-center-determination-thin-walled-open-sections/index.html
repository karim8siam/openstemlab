Aeronautical assumptions and boundary condition limits

Thin-walled open section theory assumes wall thickness t is small compared to flange width and web height (t/b < 0.1). Linear regression of twist angle against load offset yields a slope of 0.2400 mrad/mm and intercept b = 4.4400 mrad, locating the shear center at e_s = -18.500 mm with R^2 > 0.999999.

Aeronautical apparatus & wind tunnel sensors.

Thin-Walled C-Channel Test Beam

Extruded aluminum C-channel (h = 100 mm, b = 40 mm, t = 2.5 mm, L = 1000 mm) mounted as a cantilever.

Transverse Offset Loading Bar

Rigid perpendicular cross-arm attached to beam tip allowing load application at precise offsets e from web.

Calibrated Deadweight Loading Carrier

Applies constant vertical shear force P = 2.0 kN at millimeter-graduated offset stations.

Twin Precision Inclinometer / Laser Angular Sensors

Measures beam cross-sectional torsional twist angle theta with 0.01 mrad resolution.

Multi-Channel Linear Variable Differential Transformers (LVDT)

Measures vertical bending deflection v to separate bending from twisting.

Digital Structural Mechanics DAQ Workstation

Logs offset-twist data, executes linear regression, and computes experimental shear center e_s.

FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Apply transverse shear loads at variable offsets along a thin-walled C-channel spar to locate the shear center e_s where zero torsional twist occurs. Standardized parameter settings: -25.0, -15.0, -5.0, 5.0, 15.0, 25.0 mm.

01

C-Channel Rigid Clamp Mounting

Clamp the root of the C-channel beam into the heavy structural reaction wall, ensuring a fully restrained fixed boundary.

02

Offset Cross-Arm Zero Alignment

Attach the graduated offset loading bar to the free tip, calibrating e = 0 mm precisely at the centerline of the vertical web.

03

Offset Load Application Sweep

Apply vertical test load P = 2.0 kN at offsets e = -25.0, -15.0, -5.0, +5.0, +15.0, and +25.0 mm relative to the web.

04

Torsional Twist Angle Acquisition

Record the equilibrium cross-sectional twist angle theta (in milliradians) at each load offset using the laser inclinometer.

05

Zero-Twist Crossing Identification

Identify the specific load offset where twist angle theta is zero, confirming pure uncoupled bending.

06

Shear Center Linear Regression

Plot theta versus e, perform linear regression to determine slope m and intercept b, and compute shear center offset e_s = -b / m.
