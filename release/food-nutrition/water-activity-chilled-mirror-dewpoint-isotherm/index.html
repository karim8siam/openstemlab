FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Measure water activity (aw) in low-moisture snack foods across a moisture gradient using chilled-mirror dewpoint psychrometry. Standardized parameter settings: 4.0, 6.0, 8.0, 10.0, 12.0, 14.0, 16.0, 18.0, 20.0 g/100g.

  1. Step 1

    Sample Homogenization & Environmental Conditioning

    Milling, blending, and conditioning food matrix specimens for Water Activity Sorption Isotherm under strictly regulated temperature and humidity conditions in accordance with AOAC official methods.

  2. Step 2

    Instrument Standardization & Blank Calibration

    Calibrate instrumentation baseline using certified analytical standards or reagent blanks prior to sample acquisition runs.

  3. Step 3

    Independent Setpoint Parameter Adjustment

    Adjust the independent testing parameter (Moisture Content) across the designated range (4.0 to 20.0 g/100g) in steady increments.

  4. Step 4

    Analytical Titration / Spectrophotometric Acquisition

    Execute the assay run and record real-time analytical telemetry for Water Activity (aw) with 64-bit IEEE 754 precision logging.

  5. Step 5

    Replicate Verification & Matrix Inspection

    Confirm chemical equilibrium, inspect reaction clarity, and replicate analytical readings across successive sample batches.

  6. Step 6

    Regression Calculation & Nutritional Parameter Derivation

    Plot Moisture Content (g/100g solids) against Water Activity (aw), calculate ordinary least-squares regression parameters, and derive Sorption Isotherm Hygroscopic Slope (S_aw) against certified food composition benchmarks.
