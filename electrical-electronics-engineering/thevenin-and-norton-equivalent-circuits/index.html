FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Determine the Thévenin open-circuit equivalent voltage (V_th), Norton short-circuit current (I_N), and equivalent internal resistance (R_th) of an active linear DC resistive network under variable load conditions. Standardized parameter settings: 0.02, 0.04, 0.06, 0.08, 0.1, 0.12 A.

  1. Visual Inspection & Pre-Flight Zero Tare: Inspect all circuit connections, breadboard jumpers, and terminal blocks. Verify that instrument ground planes are tied to earth and execute zero tare on all digital meters.
  2. Excitation Source & Baseline Calibration: Energize the regulated supply, set baseline operating thresholds, and calibrate the load current using the precision standard reference meter.
  3. Systematic Parameter Variation & Settling: Increment the load current systematically across the designated experimental range (0.02 to 0.12 A), permitting thermal settling at each setpoint.
  4. Multi-Meter & Oscilloscope Waveform Acquisition: Record the primary output metric (Measured Terminal Voltage V_L) alongside auxiliary telemetry. Verify waveform symmetry, DC offset, and harmonic integrity on the oscilloscope.
  5. Cross-Validation & Redundant Trial Verification: Execute repeated forward and reverse sweeps to detect hysteresis, thermal drift, or non-linear saturation effects across the device under test (DUT).
  6. Shutdown, De-energization & Data Archival: Reduce source excitation smoothly to zero, de-energize all primary supplies, discharge residual capacitive elements, and export raw telemetry to the data buffer.
