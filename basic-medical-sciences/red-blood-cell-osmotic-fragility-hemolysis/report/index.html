9. Sources of Experimental Error and Mitigation

  • Thermal Fluctuations: Ambient temperature shifts alter enzyme kinetics and gas solubility. Mitigation: Thermostatically regulated water baths (37.0 ± 0.1 °C).
  • Pipetting and Dilution Errors: Volumetric meniscus reading errors. Mitigation: Use of calibrated positive-displacement micro-pipettes with verified tips.
  • Photometric & Optical Cuvette Scratches: Optical scattering increases baseline absorbance. Mitigation: Optical-grade quartz cuvettes inspected and cleaned with lens paper.
  • Biological Specimen Degradation: Proteolytic enzyme degradation or cell lysis over time. Mitigation: Keeping biological reagents on ice prior to assay and completing trials within 2 hours of preparation.
