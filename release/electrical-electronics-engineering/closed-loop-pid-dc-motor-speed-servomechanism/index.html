FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Implement analog and digital PID feedback control on a DC servomotor, analyzing step response overshoot, steady-state error, settling time, and disturbance rejection under variable loop gains. Standardized parameter settings: 1.0, 1.8, 2.6, 3.4, 4.2, 5.0 gain.

  1. Visual Inspection & Pre-Flight Zero Tare: Inspect all circuit connections, breadboard jumpers, and terminal blocks. Verify that instrument ground planes are tied to earth and execute zero tare on all digital meters.
  2. Excitation Source & Baseline Calibration: Energize the regulated supply, set baseline operating thresholds, and calibrate the proportional gain k_p using the precision standard reference meter.
  3. Systematic Parameter Variation & Settling: Increment the proportional gain k_p systematically across the designated experimental range (1.0 to 5.0 gain), permitting thermal settling at each setpoint.
  4. Multi-Meter & Oscilloscope Waveform Acquisition: Record the primary output metric (Closed-Loop Natural Angular Frequency ω_n) alongside auxiliary telemetry. Verify waveform symmetry, DC offset, and harmonic integrity on the oscilloscope.
  5. Cross-Validation & Redundant Trial Verification: Execute repeated forward and reverse sweeps to detect hysteresis, thermal drift, or non-linear saturation effects across the device under test (DUT).
  6. Shutdown, De-energization & Data Archival: Reduce source excitation smoothly to zero, de-energize all primary supplies, discharge residual capacitive elements, and export raw telemetry to the data buffer.
