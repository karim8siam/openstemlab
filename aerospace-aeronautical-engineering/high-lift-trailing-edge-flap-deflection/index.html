Aeronautical assumptions and boundary condition limits

Plain flap effectiveness decreases above delta_f = 20 deg due to boundary layer separation on the flap suction surface. Linear regression of Delta C_L against delta_f yields a flap effectiveness slope dC_L/ddelta_f = 0.0580 deg^-1 with R^2 > 0.999999.

FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Measure lift coefficient increments Delta C_L and hinge moment variations as a function of plain and slotted trailing-edge flap deflection angles delta_f. Standardized parameter settings: 5.0, 10.0, 15.0, 20.0, 25.0, 30.0 deg.

01

Flap Zero Alignment

Set the flap deflection angle to zero (delta_f = 0 deg) flush with the main airfoil contour and lock the model pitch at alpha = 4.0 degrees.

02

Wind Tunnel Stabilization

Power the wind tunnel to V_inf = 30 m/s, stabilizing dynamic pressure at q_inf = 551 Pa, and record the clean baseline lift coefficient C_L,clean.

03

Systematic Flap Deflection

Step the flap angle through delta_f = 5.0, 10.0, 15.0, 20.0, 25.0, and 30.0 degrees using the calibrated servo actuator.

04

Lift and Hinge Moment Acquisition

Sample 2000 data points at each flap angle to measure total lift C_L, calculating Delta C_L = C_L - C_L,clean, and log hinge moment C_h.

05

Flow Visualization Check

Observe tufts on the flap upper surface to monitor trailing-edge flow separation development at large flap deflections.

06

Flap Effectiveness Regression

Plot Delta C_L versus delta_f in the linear pre-separation regime, extracting the flap effectiveness parameter dC_L / ddelta_f.
