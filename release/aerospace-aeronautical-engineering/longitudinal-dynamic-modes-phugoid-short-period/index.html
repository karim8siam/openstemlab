FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Extract natural frequency omega_n and damping ratio zeta of the rapid angle-of-attack short-period mode and slow energy-exchange phugoid mode from aircraft state-space flight dynamics. Standardized parameter settings: 40.0, 50.0, 60.0, 75.0, 88.0, 100.0 m/s.

01

Level Flight Trim at Initial Airspeed

Trim the aircraft in steady level flight at V_0 = 40.0 m/s, recording baseline state vectors and elevator trim angle.

02

Elevator Doublet Excitation

Trigger a standardized 1.0-second elevator pulse doublet (+2 deg / -2 deg) to excite longitudinal dynamic modes.

03

Short-Period Mode Identification

Isolate the first 5 seconds of pitch rate q(t) telemetry to determine the rapid short-period frequency and damping.

04

Phugoid Oscillation Recording

Track the subsequent 120 seconds of airspeed V(t) and altitude h(t) oscillations to record the slow phugoid cycle.

05

Systematic Airspeed Stepping

Repeat doublet excitation across airspeeds V_0 = 50.0, 60.0, 75.0, 88.0, and 100.0 m/s, calculating natural frequency omega_n,p.

06

Lanchester Phugoid Linear Regression

Plot reciprocal frequency 1/omega_n,p versus V_0, perform linear regression, and compare the slope to Lanchester's 1/(sqrt(2)*g).
