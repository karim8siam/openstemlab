Biomedical assumptions and boundary condition limits

The linear PWV-MAP relationship applies over physiological pressure ranges (70 to 120 mmHg); extreme pressures display exponential stiffening. Evaluated on a simulated human arterial tree phantom with anatomically branched elastic silicone aorta, carotid, and femoral conduits (effective carotid-to-femoral transit distance Δx = 0.450 m). Mean arterial pressure MAP controlled from 70.0 to 120.0 mmHg. Pressure-dependent PWV scaling slope k_p = 0.0450 (m/s)/mmHg with baseline PWV_0 = 2.8000 m/s (PWV = 6.85 m/s at normotensive MAP = 90 mmHg).

Precision Caliper & Tape Transit Distance Gauge

Digitizes simultaneous carotid and femoral pulse waves with sub-millisecond time resolution.

Automated Foot-to-Foot Waveform Intersection Algorithm

Identifies initial systolic upstroke inflection point (transit time foot) via intersecting tangent method.

Continuous Arterial Line Reference Transducer

Measures true transmural mean arterial pressure (MAP) to ±0.1 mmHg.

FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Determine carotid-femoral pulse wave velocity (cfPWV) across varying mean arterial pressures (MAP) using dual applanation tonometry and evaluate pressure-dependent vascular stiffening. Standardized parameter settings: 70.0, 80.0, 90.0, 100.0, 110.0, 120.0 mmHg.

01

Anatomical Transit Distance Calibration

Measure superficial surface distance from suprasternal notch to carotid site and from suprasternal notch to femoral site; calculate net aortic transit distance Δx = 0.450 m.

02

Applanation Tonometer Positioning

Position tonometer probes over the right common carotid artery and right femoral artery, adjusting hold-down pressure until maximum signal amplitude is registered.

03

Baseline MAP Stabilization

Set circulatory simulator mean arterial pressure to 70.0 mmHg and verify stable heart rate (70 BPM) and cardiac output.

04

Synchronous Foot-to-Foot Transit Time Capture

Record 10 consecutive pulse wave pairs at 2000 Hz; execute intersecting tangent algorithm to detect foot transit time delay Δt, calculating PWV = Δx / Δt.

05

Mean Arterial Pressure Progression

Increment circulatory MAP through 80, 90, 100, 110, and 120 mmHg, capturing stabilized cfPWV over 10 cycles at each pressure step.

06

Pressure Sensitivity Linear Regression

Plot cfPWV versus MAP and determine the vascular pressure sensitivity slope k_p and baseline velocity PWV_0 via linear regression.
