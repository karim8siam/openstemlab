FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Investigate the frequency-dependent contact impedance and half-cell potential stability of sintered Ag/AgCl skin biopotential electrodes across AC excitation frequencies. Standardized parameter settings: 2.3026, 3.2189, 4.1352, 5.0515, 5.9678, 6.9078 ln(Hz).

01

Skin Phantom Hydration and Electrode Mounting

Apply a uniform 1.0 mm layer of chloride hydrogel to the phantom substrate and affix two Ag/AgCl electrodes at 50.0 mm center-to-center distance.

02

DC Offset Potential Verification

Connect electrodes to the digital multimeter; verify DC half-cell offset potential is stable and under 2.0 mV (ensuring matched electrode pairs).

03

LCR Analyzer Excitation Setup

Configure the impedance analyzer in four-wire mode with safe physiological current excitation of 10.0 μA RMS.

04

Low-Frequency Baseline Measurement

Apply 10 Hz sinusoidal excitation (ln(10) = 2.3026 ln[Hz]); record stabilized contact impedance magnitude Z_e in kΩ.

05

Frequency Sweep Progression

Increment frequency through 25, 62.5, 156, 390, and 1000 Hz (ln(f) = 3.2189, 4.1352, 5.0515, 5.9678, 6.9078), recording Z_e at each point.

06

Dispersion Regression Analysis

Plot contact impedance Z_e against ln(f), performing linear regression to evaluate the electrode dispersion gradient k_z.
