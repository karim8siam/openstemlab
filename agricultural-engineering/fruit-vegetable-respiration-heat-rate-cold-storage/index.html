Agricultural assumptions and boundary condition limits

The linear kinetic model applies within the commercial refrigerated storage range of 2 °C to 22 °C. Evaluated on fresh Gala apples (moisture content 84.5% w.b.) in a sealed 50-liter acrylic respiration chamber under normoxic atmospheric conditions (21% O2 baseline). Linearized temperature sensitivity is evaluated between 2 °C and 22 °C.

FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Measure the carbon dioxide evolution rate and respiration heat production of stored agricultural produce across storage temperatures using closed-system gas analysis. Standardized parameter settings: 2.0, 6.0, 10.0, 14.0, 18.0, 22.0 °C.

01

Produce Sample Preparation

Weigh exactly 10.00 kg of uniform, blemish-free Gala apples and insert core thermistors into two representative reference fruits.

02

Chamber Temperature Equilibration

Place the produce in the environmental chamber at 2.0 °C for 6 hours until fruit pulp core reaches within 0.2 °C of setpoint.

03

Hermetic Sealing and Baseline Purge

Seal the respiration chamber lid and flush briefly with fresh air to establish standard 400 ppm baseline atmospheric CO2.

04

Gas Evolution Rate Measurement

Isolate the chamber and record NDIR CO2 accumulation over a 60-minute incubation period, ensuring CO2 remains below 10,000 ppm.

05

Temperature Progression Sequence

Repeat thermal equilibration and 60-minute CO2 accumulation trials at 6.0, 10.0, 14.0, 18.0, and 22.0 °C.

06

Rate Calculation and Kinetic Regression

Calculate R_CO2 in mg/(kg·h) and plot against temperature T, fitting a linear regression to determine thermal respiration sensitivity k_r.
