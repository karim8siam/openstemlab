FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Measure the aerodynamic velocity distribution and chaff winnowing separation efficiency across a reciprocating combine cleaning shoe sieve under variable cleaning fan speeds. Standardized parameter settings: 3.0, 3.8, 4.6, 5.4, 6.2, 7.0 m/s.

01

Sieve Louver & Fan Choke Setup

Set the chaffer sieve louvers to 12 mm opening and lower cleaning sieve to 8 mm opening, aligning the fan windboard deflectors toward the front third of the chaffer.

02

Velocity Grid Mapping

Engage the cleaning fan and traverse the hot-wire anemometer array across the sieve grid to establish average baseline face air velocity U = 3.0 m/s.

03

Test Mixture Preparation

Blend a standardized 15.0 kg test batch containing 10.5 kg clean wheat and 4.5 kg wheat chaff (70:30 grain-to-chaff ratio).

04

Feed Delivery & Sieve Operation

Engage sieve reciprocating shake (300 cycles/min) and feed the mixture onto the pre-cleaner pan over 30 seconds.

05

Fraction Separation & Weighing

Weigh clean grain collected in the bottom hopper (m_clean), chaff captured in the rear cyclone (m_chaff_out), and residual chaff on the sieve to calculate chaff removal efficiency R_c = (m_chaff_out / m_chaff_total) × 100%.

06

Airflow Velocity Stepping

Increase fan speed across velocities of 3.8, 4.6, 5.4, 6.2, and 7.0 m/s, recording chaff removal R_c and monitoring grain blown-over loss.
