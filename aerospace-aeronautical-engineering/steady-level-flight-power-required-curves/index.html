FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Determine minimum drag speed V_md, parasite vs induced drag crossover, and power required curves P_R(V) for an aircraft in steady unaccelerated level cruise. Standardized parameter settings: 0.6, 1.0, 1.5, 2.2, 3.0, 4.0 kPa.

01

Zero Tare and Weight Calibration

Perform wind-off balance tare across all load cells with the 1:10 scale business jet model mounted on the sting.

02

Level Flight Trim Initialization

Set simulated aircraft weight to W = 45.0 kN, programming the auto-trimmer to maintain L = W at all test velocities.

03

Dynamic Pressure Stepping

Step the wind tunnel dynamic pressure through q = 0.60, 1.00, 1.50, 2.20, 3.00, and 4.00 kPa.

04

Drag Force Acquisition

Sample balance drag force across 3000 points at each dynamic pressure setting, resolving total drag into D_0 and D_i.

05

Parasite Thrust Isolation

Subtract theoretical induced drag D_i = W^2 / (q * S * pi * e * AR) from total drag to isolate parasite thrust T_parasite.

06

Parasite Drag Area Regression

Plot T_parasite (in kN) versus q (in kPa), perform linear regression to determine equivalent parasite area f = S * C_D0, and calculate (L/D)_max.
