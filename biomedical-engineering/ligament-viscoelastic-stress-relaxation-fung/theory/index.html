Biomedical and physiological foundations of Ligament Viscoelastic Relaxation.

Ligaments and tendons are dense fibrous connective tissues that mechanically guide joint kinematics, stabilize articulating bones, and prevent abnormal joint subluxation (such as anterior tibial translation resisted by the anterior cruciate ligament, ACL). Structurally composed of hierarchically crimped type-I collagen fibers embedded in a proteoglycan-rich ground substance, biological soft tissues exhibit pronounced time- and history-dependent viscoelastic behaviors, including hysteresis, creep, preconditioning, and stress relaxation. Under a rapid step-strain held constant over time (ε = ε_0 for t > 0), the tensile stress σ(t) peaks immediately upon stretching and then decays asymptotically as internal viscous fluid flow, proteoglycan shearing, and collagen fibril sliding dissipate energy. To mathematically describe this non-linear viscoelasticity, Y.C. Fung formulated the Quasi-Linear Viscoelastic (QLV) theory. In Fung's QLV framework, the reduced relaxation function G(t) = σ(t) / σ_0 characterizes the time-dependent relaxation behavior independently of the non-linear elastic response: G(t) = [1 - c_r · ln(t / τ_1)] / [1 + c_r · ln(τ_2 / τ_1)]. Over intermediate physiological time scales (where τ_1 ≪ t ≪ τ_2, typically 5 s to 300 s), G(t) is linear with respect to the natural logarithm of time: σ(t)/σ_0 = -c_r · ln(t) + G_0, where c_r is the continuous relaxation spectrum parameter. In this laboratory investigation, bone-patellar tendon-bone or ACL specimens tested in a 37.0 °C saline bio-chamber evaluate tensile stress relaxation to determine Fung's parameter c_r.

σ(t)/σ₀ = -c_r · ln(t) + G_0 = -0.0450 · ln(t) + 0.9800

Viva voce questions & analytical theory

What is the physical interpretation of Fung's parameter c_r?

Fung's parameter c_r quantifies the intensity of the continuous relaxation spectrum of the tissue; higher c_r values indicate greater viscous energy dissipation, faster stress relaxation, and more pronounced viscoelastic damping.

Why does stress relax in a ligament held at constant strain?

At constant elongation, water and mobile proteoglycans flow through the collagen fibrillar network, and crimped collagen fibrils undergo microscopic molecular realignment and sliding, dissipating internal strain energy into heat.

What is the purpose of mechanical preconditioning in soft tissue testing?

Unpreconditioned soft tissues exhibit progressive shifting of stress-strain loops due to internal fluid redistribution; cyclically loading 10 to 20 times establishes an internal fluid equilibrium that yields highly repeatable mechanical measurements.

What are the lower and upper relaxation time limits τ_1 and τ_2 in Fung's QLV theory?

τ_1 represents the fast molecular relaxation process of collagen fibrils (milliseconds), while τ_2 represents the slow macroscopic fluid exudation and ground-substance relaxation (hundreds of seconds).

How does chemical cross-linking (e.g., glutaraldehyde) affect ligament viscoelasticity?

Exogenous cross-linking introduces rigid chemical bonds between collagen molecules, restricting inter-fibrillar sliding and fluid exudation, which significantly decreases c_r and reduces viscoelastic stress relaxation.

Why is the ramp phase rise time critical in stress relaxation experiments?

If the ramp is too slow, significant relaxation occurs during the loading phase before peak strain is reached, violating the mathematical assumption of an instantaneous Heaviside step strain and introducing substantial errors in G(t).

Experimental precautions and patient safety

  • The rise time of the loading ramp must be under 100 ms to minimize relaxation occurring prior to peak strain.
  • Verify that mid-substance ligament strain is tracked optically; grip compliance can cause mid-substance strain to creep even when crosshead is held fixed.
  • Ensure bone blocks are potted rigidly in PMMA to avoid micro-motion at the clamping boundary.
  • Maintain continuous 37 °C saline immersion; surface drying dramatically alters ligament relaxation kinetics.

Theoretical assumptions & biological limits

  • The linear semi-logarithmic relation applies over the intermediate time window (τ_1 ≪ t ≪ τ_2); it deviates at very short (< 1 s) and very long (> 10⁴ s) times.
  • Fung's QLV theory assumes time-dependence and strain-dependence are fully separable: σ(ε, t) = G(t) * σ_e(ε), which is an approximation.
  • Does not account for non-uniform fiber recruitments across the anteromedial and posterolateral bundles of the native ACL.
  • Assumes 1D uniaxial tension, neglecting complex in-vivo multi-axial twisting and shear kinematics.
