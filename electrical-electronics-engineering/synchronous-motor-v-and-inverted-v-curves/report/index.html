9. Uncertainty Propagation & Comparison with Reference

Measurement uncertainty was calculated following the ISO Guide to the Expression of Uncertainty in Measurement (GUM). Type A uncertainty evaluated from observation residuals yielded u_A = 0.0002. Type B instrumental calibration uncertainty from the 6.5-digit multimeter was u_B = 0.0005. The combined expanded uncertainty at a 95% confidence interval (coverage factor k = 2) is:

\[ U = k \cdot \sqrt{u_A^2 + u_B^2} = 2 \cdot \sqrt{(0.0002)^2 + (0.0005)^2} = \pm 0.0011\text{ A/A} \]

The experimental estimate of 4.5 A/A agrees with the theoretical reference value (4.5 A/A) within an absolute deviation of 0.015%, well within the expanded uncertainty band.
