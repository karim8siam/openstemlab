FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Determine the soil water retention curve (pF curve) of an agricultural loam using a porous ceramic pressure plate extractor across varying matric suctions. Standardized parameter settings: 1.0, 1.6, 2.2, 2.8, 3.4, 4.0 ln(kPa).

01

Ceramic Plate Pre-Saturation

Submerge the porous ceramic plate in de-aired water for 24 hours to ensure 100% saturation of all ceramic microscopic pores, verifying zero air entry.

02

Soil Core Loading & Saturation

Place soil sample rings onto the plate, add water to the plate surface, and allow soil cores to saturate by capillarity from the bottom for 18 hours.

03

Initial Suction Chamber Seal

Transfer the loaded plate into the pressure vessel, connect the effluent drain tube, seal the chamber lid, and apply initial pneumatic pressure P = 2.7 kPa (ln ψ = 1.0).

04

Hydraulic Equilibrium Attainment

Maintain pressure until outflow from the drain tube ceases completely (typically 24 to 48 hours), confirming thermodynamic equilibrium between soil water and air pressure.

05

Sample Extraction & Weighing

Quickly depressurize the chamber, extract replicate soil rings, record wet mass, and oven-dry at 105°C for 24 hours to calculate volumetric water content θ.

06

Multi-Pressure Retention Curve

Repeat testing across suctions corresponding to ln ψ = 1.6, 2.2, 2.8, 3.4, and 4.0 (up to 54.6 kPa in the ceramic extractor), plotting θ versus ln ψ to evaluate λ_ret.
