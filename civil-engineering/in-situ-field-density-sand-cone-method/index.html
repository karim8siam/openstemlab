FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Determine the in-situ wet density, in-situ dry density, and relative compaction percentage (RC) of compacted earth fill using the sand cone apparatus according to ASTM D1556 / AASHTO T191. Standardized parameter settings: 1200.0, 1400.0, 1600.0, 1800.0, 2000.0, 2200.0, 2400.0 cm³.

01

Apparatus Calibration: Cone Funnel Mass & Sand Density

Determine calibrated bulk density of Ottawa sand ρ_sand (1.420 g/cm³) in a 1-liter mold; fill sand cone jar; invert on base plate; open valve to determine mass of sand filling cone alone (M_cone = 1560 g).

02

Test Surface Preparation & Base Plate Anchoring

Level a 500 x 500 mm test area on compacted fill; seat base plate flat; secure corners with steel spikes to prevent shifting.

03

Test Hole Excavation & Soil Collection

Excavate circular hole through plate opening to depth of ~150 mm using chisel and scoop; collect every gram of excavated soil into sealed container; record moist mass M_wet.

04

Sand Cone Inversion & Valve Opening

Weigh filled sand cone assembly (M_initial); seat inverted cone centrally into base plate recess; open valve fully; allow sand to flow by gravity until motion stops; close valve.

05

Retained Sand Weighing & Hole Volume Calculation

Weigh remaining sand and jar (M_final); compute sand used M_used = M_initial - M_final; compute net sand in hole M_hole = M_used - M_cone; calculate hole volume V_h = M_hole / ρ_sand.

06

Moisture & Relative Compaction Determination

Dry excavated soil sample in oven at 110 °C to determine moisture content w; compute in-situ dry density ρ_d = M_s / V_h; calculate relative compaction RC = (ρ_d / MDD) x 100%.

THEORY AND STRUCTURAL CONTEXT

Civil engineering background & physical principles.

Quality assurance and quality control (QA/QC) in civil earthwork construction require rigorous verification that compacted subgrades, roadway base courses, and structural backfills achieve specified field densities. Standardized under ASTM D1556 and AASHTO T191, the sand cone method provides the definitive, physical benchmark field test against which modern nuclear density gauges are calibrated. In this test, a metal base plate with a 165 mm (6.5 inch) circular hole is clamped flat onto the freshly compacted fill. A test hole approximately 150 mm deep is excavated by hand using chisels and scoops, with every single particle of excavated soil collected into a sealed moisture container. The volume of the irregular hole V_h is determined by inverting a standardized sand cone apparatus over the hole and opening the control valve. Standard uniform, clean, dry Ottawa sand (passing 850 μm No. 20, retained on 600 μm No. 30) of pre-calibrated bulk density ρ_sand flows by gravity into the hole and funnel cone. Subtracting the calibrated mass of sand required to fill the metal cone funnel alone yields the net mass of sand filling the test hole, from which volume V_h = M_sand / ρ_sand is evaluated. Weighing excavated moist soil M_wet and measuring moisture content w provides in-situ dry density: ρ_d = M_wet / [V_h (1 + w)] = M_s / V_h. Relative compaction RC = (ρ_d / MDD) x 100% must meet contract specifications (typically ≥ 95%). In this laboratory, sand cone tests are performed across hole volumes from 1200 to 2400 cm³ to evaluate field dry density ρ_d.
