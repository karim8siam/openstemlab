FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Determine the cooling saturation efficiency and water evaporation rate of a cellulose wet-pad ventilation system under varying approach face air velocities. Standardized parameter settings: 0.5, 0.8, 1.1, 1.4, 1.7, 2.0 m/s.

01

Pad Pre-Wetting and Saturation

Energize the recirculation pump for 20 minutes to thoroughly wet the cellulose pad until uniform water curtaining is established.

02

Initial Airflow Adjustment

Set the exhaust fan frequency drive to produce an approach face velocity of 0.5 m/s measured via multi-point pitot tube array.

03

Steady-State Conditioning

Operate the system for 10 minutes until downstream dry-bulb temperature reaches steady equilibrium.

04

Psychrometric and Gravimetric Logging

Record upstream and downstream dry-bulb and wet-bulb temperatures and log water reservoir mass depletion over a 15-minute window.

05

Face Velocity Increment Progression

Increment face air velocity to 0.8, 1.1, 1.4, 1.7, and 2.0 m/s, repeating gravimetric and psychrometric measurements at each stage.

06

Saturation Efficiency and Consumption Analysis

Calculate cooling saturation efficiency η_c = (T_db,in - T_db,out)/(T_db,in - T_wb,in) and plot evaporation rate m_dot_w against face velocity v_pad.
