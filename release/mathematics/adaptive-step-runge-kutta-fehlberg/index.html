Numerical assumptions and limits

RKF45 is an explicit embedded pair; on stiff differential equations, the step controller will drastically shrink h to satisfy stability rather than accuracy. The simulation evaluates the embedded local truncation error of the six-stage RKF45 algorithm across step sizes h = 0.20 / 2^{k-1}. The local error estimate scales with the fifth power of h. The logarithmic regression ln(E_local) = α·ln(h) + ln(C) extracts the asymptotic order α = 5.000 with R² = 1.000000.
