Agricultural assumptions and boundary condition limits

Affinity laws assume identical internal fluid streamlines and neglect Reynolds number viscosity changes at low speeds. Evaluated using a single-stage end-suction centrifugal pump (impeller diameter D = 160 mm) pumping clean water across speeds from 1000 to 2400 RPM.

FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Determine the head-discharge (H-Q), brake power, and overall efficiency characteristic curves of an agricultural centrifugal pump and verify affinity affinity laws across speeds. Standardized parameter settings: 1.0, 1.69, 2.56, 3.61, 4.84, 5.76 (k-rpm)².

01

Pump Priming & Sensor Zeroing

Fill the suction line and pump volute casing with water, bleeding all air through the top vent valve until no bubbles emerge. Zero all pressure transducers at static equilibrium.

02

Initial Speed Setting

Set variable-frequency motor drive to N = 1000 RPM ((N/1000)² = 1.00), leaving the discharge throttle valve fully closed to establish the shutoff point.

03

Shutoff Head Measurement

Record suction vacuum and discharge gauge pressure at zero flow (Q = 0) and compute total shutoff dynamic head H_0 = (P_d - P_s)/(ρ·g) + Δz.

04

H-Q Characteristic Curve Run

At 1750 RPM, systematically open the discharge valve in eight increments from shutoff (Q = 0) to maximum discharge, recording H, Q, shaft power P, and efficiency η.

05

Speed Affinity Stepping

Return throttle valve to shutoff and increment pump speed across five additional stages: 1300, 1600, 1900, 2200, and 2400 RPM, recording H_0 for each speed.

06

Affinity Scaling Regression

Plot shutoff head H_0 against normalized speed squared (N/1000)², perform linear regression through the origin, and determine affinity constant k_N.
