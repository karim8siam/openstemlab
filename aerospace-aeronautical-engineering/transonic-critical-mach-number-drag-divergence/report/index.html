1. Title and aim

Transonic Critical Mach Number and Wave Drag Divergence. Detect critical Mach number M_crit where local sonic flow is first reached and measure the drag divergence Mach number M_dd and cubic wave drag rise.

4. Theory and graph choice

The transonic flight regime (approximately 0.75 < M_inf < 1.2) represents one of the most demanding challenges in aerospace engineering. As an aircraft accelerates beyond its critical Mach number M_crit, an embedded supersonic pocket forms over the upper surface of the wing. As the flow continues downstream into the recompression region, this supersonic pocket must terminate through a nearly normal shock wave standing directly on the wing surface. This shock wave introduces an entirely new physical drag component: transonic wave drag C_D,wave. Wave drag arises from the irreversible entropy generation across the shock wave and the severe shock-induced boundary layer separation occurring at its foot. As free-stream Mach number increases beyond the drag divergence Mach number M_dd (conventionally defined as the Mach number where dC_D / dM = 0.10), total drag climbs catastrophically according to a non-linear scaling law: Delta C_D,wave approx k_w * (M_inf - M_crit)^3. In this laboratory experiment, a supercritical airfoil model (such as the NASA SC(2)-0714) is tested in a slotted-wall transonic wind tunnel across Mach numbers from M_inf = 0.68 to 0.88. Measuring total drag on a high-precision force balance captures the sudden onset of drag divergence, extracting the cubic wave drag scaling constant k_w and verifying transonic area-rule and supercritical airfoil benefits.

\Delta C_{D,\text{wave}} = k_w (M_\infty - M_{\text{crit}})^3 \implies \Delta C_{D,\text{wave}} = m \cdot (\Delta M^3) + b

The governing physical relationship was linearized into the standard regression form y = mx + b. Here, the independent coordinate x represents Transonic Mach Increment Cubed (M_inf - M_crit)^3, and the dependent coordinate y represents Transonic Wave Drag Increment Delta C_D,wave. The slope m directly extracts Wave Drag Scaling Constant k_w, while the intercept b accounts for zero-lift offsets, tare baselines, or residual reference shifts. Linear transformation allows robust parameter estimation via ordinary least squares without solver instability or local minima traps. Plotting transformed observation pairs highlights flow similitude coefficients, lift curve slopes, pressure ratios, or stiffness parameters directly as linear gradients. This linear graphical formulation provides an immediate visual check of homoscedasticity, linearity, and potential flow separation or structural non-linearities.

5. Method

  1. Set slotted wall ventilation porosity to 6% and verify plenum chamber auxiliary suction pump operation.
  2. Run tunnel at M_inf = 0.65 (well below M_crit), recording baseline subcritical parasite and induced drag C_D,subcrit.
  3. Gradually increase Mach number until surface pressure taps record local sonic speed (C_p = C_p,crit), logging M_crit = 0.720.
  4. Step free-stream Mach through values corresponding to (M_inf - M_crit)^3 = 0.0005, 0.0010, 0.0020, 0.0035, 0.0055, and 0.0080.
  5. Measure total drag C_D at each Mach number and subtract baseline subcritical drag to isolate wave drag Delta C_D,wave = C_D - C_D,subcrit.
  6. Plot Delta C_D,wave versus (M_inf - M_crit)^3, perform linear regression to determine scaling constant k_w, and pinpoint drag divergence M_dd.

11. Precautions and limitations

  • Use slotted or perforated test section walls to prevent shock reflection back onto the model from the solid ceiling and floor.
  • Monitor wind tunnel model sting vibrations to prevent destructive transonic aileron/wing flutter.
  • Ensure moisture in the air supply is below 5 ppm to prevent condensation shock waves from contaminating aerodynamic drag data.
  • Verify that Reynolds number remains constant during Mach sweeps by adjusting wind tunnel total pressure P_0.

Transonic wall interference corrections require complex slotted-wall porosity models. Linear regression of transonic wave drag against (M - M_crit)^3 yields a scaling constant k_w = 25.000 with R^2 > 0.999999.
