FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Characterize the coupled yaw-roll Dutch roll oscillation, roll subsidence damping time constant tau_roll, and spiral mode divergence/stability. Standardized parameter settings: 0.08, 0.12, 0.18, 0.24, 0.3, 0.35 s.

01

Lateral Equilibrium Trim

Trim the aircraft in wings-level steady level flight at V_0 = 60 m/s with zero sideslip (beta = 0, phi = 0).

02

Roll Inertia Setting

Set simulated roll inertia parameter I_xx / (q S b^2) to 0.08 s, ensuring neutral aileron trim.

03

Aileron Step Excitation

Apply a clean 5-degree aileron step command, recording the rapid exponential rise of roll rate p(t) to steady-state.

04

Roll Subsidence Time Constant Extraction

Determine the time constant tau_roll required for roll rate to reach 63.2% of its steady-state asymptotic value.

05

Systematic Inertia Stepping

Step the inertia parameter through 0.12, 0.18, 0.24, 0.30, and 0.35 s, extracting tau_roll at each setting.

06

Dutch Roll & Spiral Verification

Apply a rudder doublet to record the Dutch roll period and verify spiral mode stability over 60 seconds.

07

Roll Damping Linear Regression

Plot tau_roll versus the roll inertia parameter, perform linear regression to determine slope k, and compute roll damping derivative C_lp.
