FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Evaluate binary physical mixtures of active drug and formulation excipients using Differential Scanning Calorimetry (DSC) to detect solid-state chemical incompatibilities and eutectic transitions. Standardized parameter settings: 0.05, 0.14, 0.23, 0.32, 0.41, 0.5 mol/mol.

  1. Step 1

    Instrument Initialization & Baseline Tare

    Power on the analytical instrumentation for DSC Drug-Excipient Compatibility, allow a 30-minute electronic warm-up period, and verify transducer zero-tare using certified standards.

  2. Step 2

    Standard Solution & Matrix Specimen Preparation

    Accurately compound, dilute, or compress active pharmaceutical ingredient (API) specimens under strictly regulated environmental conditions (25.0 ± 0.5 °C).

  3. Step 3

    Systematic Independent Variable Setting

    Adjust the independent testing parameter (Excipient Fraction) across the designated analytical range (0.05 to 0.5 mol/mol) in uniform calibrated steps.

  4. Step 4

    Analytical Signal Acquisition & Telemetry Logging

    Execute the measurement cycle and record steady-state telemetry for Drug Endothermic Peak Melting Temperature with high-precision digital integration.

  5. Step 5

    Replicate Verification & Equilibrium Inspection

    Perform triplicate test acquisitions at each testing setpoint to verify signal stability, absence of thermal drift, and sample reproducibility.

  6. Step 6

    Regression Modeling & Pharmacopoeial Parameter Derivation

    Plot Excipient Fraction against Melting Peak Tm (°C), execute ordinary least-squares (OLS) linear regression, and calculate Cryoscopic Eutectic Depression Constant (m) against compendial benchmarks.
