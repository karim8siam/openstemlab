Aeronautical assumptions and boundary condition limits

Linearized Prandtl-Glauert theory assumes small perturbations; errors increase for thick airfoils (> 15% thickness) or large alpha. Linear regression of |C_p| against the Prandtl-Glauert factor yields a baseline incompressible suction |C_p0| = 0.6500 with R^2 > 0.999999.

FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Quantify the amplification of airfoil pressure coefficients and lift curve slopes as Mach number increases from incompressible (M ~ 0) to high subsonic speeds. Standardized parameter settings: 1.02, 1.064, 1.12, 1.195, 1.291, 1.414 dimensionless.

01

Model Pitch Trimming

Set the NACA 0012 model angle of attack to a constant alpha = 4.0 degrees, locking the turntable mechanism rigidly.

02

Low-Speed Incompressible Baseline

Establish baseline flow at Mach M_inf = 0.20 (1/sqrt(1-M^2) = 1.020) and record the baseline incompressible suction peak C_p0.

03

Mach Number Stepping

Step wind tunnel speed through Mach M_inf = 0.34, 0.45, 0.55, 0.63, and 0.71 (corresponding to 1/beta_PG = 1.064 to 1.414).

04

Suction Peak Pressure Sampling

Acquire 5000 static pressure samples at the leading-edge suction peak tap (x/c = 0.05) at each Mach plateau.

05

Pressure Coefficient Normalization

Compute C_p = (P_static - P_inf) / q_inf at each Mach number, taking absolute value |C_p|.

06

Prandtl-Glauert Linear Regression

Plot |C_p| versus 1/sqrt(1 - M_inf^2), perform linear regression to determine the slope, and verify the Prandtl-Glauert rule up to M_crit.
