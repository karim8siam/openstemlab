Agricultural assumptions and boundary condition limits

The linear model applies strictly to the steady pre-saturation slip range (s ≤ 0.25) before catastrophic soil shear failure occurs. Linearized traction development model valid in the operational working range of 5% to 25% slip on settled silt loam soil. FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Determine tractor wheel slip, net traction coefficient, and tractive efficiency on agricultural soils across varying drawbar pull loads using an instrumented loading car and fifth-wheel tachometer. Standardized parameter settings: 0.05, 0.09, 0.13, 0.17, 0.21, 0.25 fraction.

01

Zero-Load Distance Calibration

Operate the tractor over a surveyed 100-meter test course in neutral drawbar condition to record baseline drive wheel revolutions and establish theoretical rolling radius under zero net pull.

02

Dynamometer Car Hitching

Hitch the mobile loading car horizontally through the calibrated strain-gauge load cell, verifying that the hitch line is parallel to the ground surface to prevent spurious vertical forces.

03

Engine Speed Standardization

Set tractor engine throttle to rated speed (2200 RPM) in standard field gear (Gear B-2) to ensure constant input mechanical power to the transmission.

04

Incremental Drawbar Loading

Increment the eddy-current braking load on the dynamometer car in six distinct steps, allowing the tractor to reach steady-state velocity for at least 30 seconds per trial.

05

Synchronous Telemetry Acquisition

Simultaneously log true ground velocity from the Doppler radar, wheel rotation counts from axle encoders, and drawbar draft force from the load cell over standard 50-meter runs.

06

Tractive Metrics Reduction

Calculate slip ratio s = (V_t - V_a)/V_t and net traction coefficient κ = P/W_d, plotting the traction curve and identifying peak tractive efficiency.
