    Specimen Conditioning & Calibration

    Prepare and condition standardized dental specimens for Dental Amalgam Creep Compliance under strictly regulated ambient conditions (23.0 ± 1.0 °C and 50 ± 5% relative humidity) according to ISO testing standards.

  2. Step 2

    Transducer Zeroing & Baseline Initialization

    Calibrate the instrumentation zero baseline. Verify sensor sensitivity using certified reference standards prior to initiating experimental runs.

  3. Step 3

    Independent Setpoint Parameter Adjustment

    Set the independent variable (Stress) across the designated experimental span (20.0 to 60.0 MPa) in steady increments.

  4. Step 4

    Data Acquisition & Real-Time Monitoring

    Execute the measurement sequence and record real-time transducer telemetry for Static Creep Strain with 64-bit precision logging.

  5. Step 5

    Replicate Verification & Artifact Inspection

    Inspect specimen integrity, confirm absent interfacial flaws or micro-cracks, and replicate measurements across successive trials.

  6. Step 6

    Mathematical Regression & Parameter Evaluation

    Plot Compressive Stress (MPa) against Creep Strain (%), calculate ordinary least-squares fit parameters, and derive Amalgam Creep Compliance (J_c) against standard reference benchmarks.
