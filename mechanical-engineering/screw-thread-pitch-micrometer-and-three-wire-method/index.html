FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Measure the effective pitch diameter of external triangular metric screw threads using precision best-size wires and a micrometer, verifying thread form tolerances. Standardized parameter settings: 8.0, 10.0, 12.0, 16.0, 20.0, 24.0 mm.

  1. Visual Inspection & Apparatus Zero Tare: Inspect all mechanical fixtures, load train alignments, safety cages, and hydraulic/electrical connections. Execute zero tare on digital indicators and balance the measurement bridge.
  2. Initial Calibration & Datum Alignment: Apply a minor pre-load to eliminate mechanical backlash and seating slack. Calibrate the major diameter against the primary standard datum.
  3. Systematic Parameter Variation & Settling: Increment the major diameter systematically across the designated experimental range (8.0 to 24.0 mm), permitting mechanical and thermal equilibrium at each step.
  4. Multi-Sensor Telemetry Acquisition: Record the primary dependent variable (Dimension Over Precision Best Wires M) alongside auxiliary channel telemetry. Verify linear proportionality and signal stability before advancing.
  5. Forward & Reverse Hysteresis Verification: Execute both ascending loading and descending unloading sequences to detect any friction lag, mechanical backlash, or permanent plastic deformation in the test assembly.
  6. Controlled De-Energization & Data Archival: Release all mechanical loads smoothly to zero, de-energize primary drives, secure moving linkages, and export all digital telemetry observations to the persistent laboratory notebook.
