FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Investigate the elastic angle of twist in solid circular steel shafts under pure torsional moment, verifying the torsion formula and calculating the shear modulus of rigidity (G). Standardized parameter settings: 0.02, 0.04, 0.06, 0.08, 0.1, 0.12 rad/m.

  1. Visual Inspection & Apparatus Zero Tare: Inspect all mechanical fixtures, load train alignments, safety cages, and hydraulic/electrical connections. Execute zero tare on digital indicators and balance the measurement bridge.
  2. Initial Calibration & Datum Alignment: Apply a minor pre-load to eliminate mechanical backlash and seating slack. Calibrate the angle of twist per length against the primary standard datum.
  3. Systematic Parameter Variation & Settling: Increment the angle of twist per length systematically across the designated experimental range (0.02 to 0.12 rad/m), permitting mechanical and thermal equilibrium at each step.
  4. Multi-Sensor Telemetry Acquisition: Record the primary dependent variable (Applied Torsional Torque T) alongside auxiliary channel telemetry. Verify linear proportionality and signal stability before advancing.
  5. Forward & Reverse Hysteresis Verification: Execute both ascending loading and descending unloading sequences to detect any friction lag, mechanical backlash, or permanent plastic deformation in the test assembly.
  6. Controlled De-Energization & Data Archival: Release all mechanical loads smoothly to zero, de-energize primary drives, secure moving linkages, and export all digital telemetry observations to the persistent laboratory notebook.
