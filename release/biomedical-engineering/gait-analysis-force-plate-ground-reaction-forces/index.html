Biomedical assumptions and boundary condition limits

The linear velocity model applies across normal walking speeds (0.8 to 1.8 m/s); running involves a discrete gait transition with different mechanics. Evaluated on healthy adult human subjects (body mass 70.0 kg, BW = 686.7 N) walking across an instrumented Bertec multi-component piezoelectric force platform (600 mm × 400 mm). Walking velocities from 0.80 to 1.80 m/s tracked via optical timing gates. Linear velocity scaling k_v = 0.2800 BW/(m/s).

Biomedical apparatus & physiological sensors.

Multi-Component Piezoelectric Force Platform

Measures triaxial forces (Fx, Fy, Fz) and moments (Mx, My, Mz) with 0.1 N resolution and 1000 Hz bandwidth.

Instrumented 10-Meter Gait Walkway

Provides seamless, flush-mounted level walking surface preventing targeting artifacts.

Dual Infrared Optical Timing Gates

Accurately measures average walking speed v_w over the middle 3-meter capture zone.

Multichannel Charge Amplifier & Signal Conditioner

Converts piezoelectric charge outputs into calibrated analog voltage signals with high drift stability.

Synchronous 16-Bit Data Acquisition Telemetry Console

Digitizes 6-channel force plate signals at 1000 Hz with digital Butterworth low-pass filtering (cutoff 20 Hz).

Body Mass Precision Calibration Scale

Determines subject static body weight (BW) to ±0.1 N prior to gait testing.

FOLLOW THE METHOD

A reason behind every step.

Measure multi-axis ground reaction forces (GRF) using an instrumented piezoelectric force plate and evaluate the linear velocity scaling of the peak vertical force F_v/BW. Standardized parameter settings: 0.8, 1.0, 1.2, 1.4, 1.6, 1.8 m/s.

01

Subject Anthropometry and Zero Tare Calibration

Measure subject body mass on calibrated digital scale, compute static body weight BW = m·g, and zero the force plate charge amplifier.

02

Optical Timing Gate Alignment

Position infrared timing gates 3.0 meters apart centered around the flush-mounted force platform and calibrate electronic timer.

03

Familiarization and Baseline Stride Setup

Allow subject several practice walking passes to establish natural stride length and ensure clean, un-targeted foot strikes onto the platform.

04

Controlled Velocity Gait Capture

Guide subject to walk at a standardized slow velocity (v_w = 0.80 m/s), verified by timing gate readout, and record 1000 Hz triaxial GRF telemetry.

05

Velocity Progression Sequence

Repeat clean foot strike trials across walking speeds of 1.00, 1.20, 1.40, 1.60, and 1.80 m/s, capturing three valid trials per speed step.

06

Peak Vertical Force Normalization and Regression

Extract peak weight-acceptance vertical force F_v, normalize to body weight (F_v/BW), plot versus velocity v_w, and calculate scaling factor k_v.
